For the blueprints of ancient city structures, see [Ancient City/Structure/Blueprints](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ancient_City/Structure/Blueprints \"Ancient City/Structure/Blueprints\")\n.\n\n### Environment aesthetics\n\n[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ancient_City?action=edit&section=3 \"Edit section: Environment aesthetics\")\n\nAn ancient city features a very large palace with a footprint of around 220 blocks on each horizontal axis, stretching throughout a [deep dark](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Deep_dark \"Deep dark\")\n biome. The floor of the city always generates at Y=-51. The palace is made up of long corridors with 2-block-deep floors of gray [wool](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wool \"Wool\")\n, with the occasional rugs of blue, light blue, and cyan wool or [carpet](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Carpet \"Carpet\")\n floors to reduce vibrations. Off to the sides of the main corridors are smaller ruin structures, which often contain one or two [loot chests](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Loot_chest \"Loot chest\")\n. The city's center features a frame that contains exclusive [reinforced deepslate](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Reinforced_deepslate \"Reinforced deepslate\")\n blocks, though these are unobtainable in [Survival](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Survival \"Survival\")\n mode. Other unique blocks such as [soul lanterns](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Soul_lantern \"Soul lantern\")\n, [candles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Candle \"Candle\")\n, and different forms of [deepslate](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Deepslate \"Deepslate\")\n and [sculk](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sculk_family \"Sculk family\")\n can be found throughout the city. [Sculk shriekers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sculk_shrieker \"Sculk shrieker\")\n generate much more frequently in ancient cities than in the normal deep dark biome. The city always generates at the same position within a chunk (for example, the blocks that make up the center frame structure are always on the western edge of a chunk).\n\nSome of the city's structures resemble other elements of the game. The city center bears a resemblance to [early iterations of the warden's design](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#Concept_artwork \"Warden\")\n, and other structures are similar in appearance to [pillager outposts](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_outpost \"Pillager outpost\")\n, the wool rooms in [woodland mansions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Woodland_mansion \"Woodland mansion\")\n, and [abandoned mineshafts](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mineshaft \"Mineshaft\")\n.\n\n#### Overall view\n\n[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ancient_City?action=edit&section=4 \"Edit section: Overall view\")\n\nThe following three renders are of the same individual ancient city.\n\n*   [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Ancient_City_SE.png \"The view from the direction of the entrance\")\n    \n    The view from the direction of the entrance\n    \n*   [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Ancient_City_NW.png \"Another direction\")\n    \n    Another direction\n    \n*   [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Ancient_City_D.png \"The vertical view (The entrance is in the lower part of this picture)\")\n    \n    The vertical view (The entrance is in the lower part of this picture)\n    \n\n#### Redstone circuits\n\n[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ancient_City?action=edit&section=5 \"Edit section: Redstone circuits\")\n\nUnderneath the frame at the city center lies a series of hidden basement rooms with naturally generating [redstone circuitry](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_circuits \"Redstone circuits\")\n.\n\nThe secret entrance is located at the base of the structure, underneath the wooden bridge between the city center and the wall. There is a [piston door](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piston_door \"Piston door\")\n that is controlled by a [sculk sensor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sculk_sensor \"Sculk sensor\")\n in the basement's redstone circuits.\n\n*   [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Ancient_city_basement_1.png \"Basement of city_center_1\")\n    \n    Basement of `city_center_1`\n    \n*   [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Ancient_city_basement_2.png \"Basement of city_center_2\")\n    \n    Basement of `city_center_2`\n    \n*   [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Ancient_city_basement_3.png \"Basement of city_center_3\")\n    \n    Basement of `city_center_3`\n    \n*   [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Ancient_city_basement_3_2.png \"Piston door circuit in the basement of city_center_3\")\n    \n    Piston door circuit in the basement of `city_center_3`\n    \n*   [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Ancient_city_basement_2_2.png \"Furnace in the basement of city_center_2\")\n    \n    Furnace in the basement of `city_center_2`\n    \n\nFor `city_center_1`, there is a [chiseled deepslate](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chiseled_deepslate \"Chiseled deepslate\")\n block in front of the frame. Making a vibration around it can activate the [sculk sensor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sculk_sensor \"Sculk sensor\")\n and then open the piston door. There is also a [pulse extender](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pulse_extender \"Pulse extender\")\n between the sculk sensor and piston door, which makes the piston door close 180 [game ticks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Game_tick \"Game tick\")\n after the sculk sensor is deactivated.\n\nThe circuit in `city_center_2` is similar to the former, but with a signal strength filter. Only vibrations with a frequency equal to 8 (eating or drinking) can activate the piston door. Creating these vibrations on the path in the front of the frame in the city center activates the door.\n\nFor `city_center_3`, making a vibration on the path in front of the frame on the city center can activate the sculk sensor. Additionally, there is a [T flip-flop](https://minecraft.wiki/w/T_flip-flop \"T flip-flop\")\n between the sculk sensor and piston door. After the sculk sensor is activated, the piston door remains open until the sculk sensor is activated again.\n\nThere are three basic redstone circuits in the basement:\n\n*

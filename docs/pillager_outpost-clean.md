Pillager Outpost
================

This article is about the structure in _Minecraft_. For other uses, see Outpost.

A **pillager outpost** is an assortment of generated structures inhabited by pillagers. Pillager outposts are one of the most reliable ways to find raid captains,
 who can be killed to obtain ominous bottles,
 which can then be used to start raids and increase the difficulty of trial chambers.

Generation
----------

Edit

Pillager outposts are semi-rare structures, generating every several hundred to couple thousand blocks.[\[1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_Outpost#cite_note-1)
 This makes them rarer than villages
[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_Outpost#cite_note-2)
 but less rare than woodland mansions.

Pillager outposts can generate naturally in any village-generating biome, including:

*   
    Plains
    
*   
    Sunflower Plains
    
*   
    Desert
    
*   
    Savanna
    
*   
    Snowy Plains
    
*   
    Taiga
    
*   
    Snowy Taiga
    
*   
    Meadow
    
*   
    Taiga Hills
     (unused)
*   
    Snowy Taiga Hills
     (unused)

Pillager outposts also generate in the following biomes, where villages do not spawn:

*   
    Grove
    
*   
    Snowy Slopes
    
*   
    Jagged Peaks
    
*   
    Frozen Peaks
    
*   
    Stony Peaks
    
*   
    Cherry Grove
    

Pillager outposts never generate too close to, or within, villages. They can do so in  as well as generate far away from villages.

If an outpost spawns in the water or in the air (e.g., spawning near a savanna plateau
), then a platform of grass
 (or sand
), dirt, and stone spawns underneath it in a circular shape. This also applies to the smaller structures such as tents, targets, log piles, and cages. On occasion, the smaller structures still spawn in the air despite having a platform below them, depending on how high up they are.

The outpost tower generates each time. Up to 4 other smaller structures may spawn around the periphery of the main outpost structure.

Eight extra air blocks also generate around the outpost, inside of the ground, creating small trenches.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_Outpost#cite_note-3)

Mobs
----

Edit

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |
| Allay | Iron Golem | Pillager |

Iron golems and allays spawn only during the generation of the outpost structures. An iron golem has a 85% chance of spawning. Meanwhile, the allay has a 50% chance of spawning. These mobs spawn in cages. The iron golem can punch pillagers who are one block away from the fences of the cage.

Both regular pillagers and pillager captains can continuously spawn around the structure. Pillagers can still spawn if a player is inside the tower. they spawn in a 72×58×72 area centered on the top level of the watchtower, the same level as the chest. Along the Y-axis, this area extends 28 blocks upward and 30 blocks downward from the center of the platform.

Pillagers always spawn on the red block. Note that the entrance is at the bottom of this image.

Pillagers have a higher chance of spawning if there are more blocks where mobs spawn (e.g. ice, grass block, bedrock).

Additionally, pillagers can also spawn on spots in the outbuildings of the outpost. The spawning spots are either level with, or one block above the generated floor of the outbuilding. Extending the floor of an outbuilding outside the originally generated boundary of the floor can sometimes reveal additional spawning spots.

Only pillagers may spawn within a pillager outpost spawn area. Pillagers may naturally spawn on any valid opaque block (except leaves) as long as the block light level is 8 or less. There is a 6% chance for a pillager to spawn as a patrol leader.

There are 15 jigsaw blocks that can generate the structures around the tower. Each structure has a 1 in 13 chance of being generated, including allay and iron golem cages. When generated, iron golem cages spawn one golem, and allay cages spawn two allays.

A total of eight pillagers, including the pillager captain, spawns in a pillager outpost.

### Bad Omen

Edit

Upon killing an outpost captain, it drops an Ominous Bottle of a random level between 1 and 5. Drinking it causes the player to receive the corresponding level of the Bad Omen effect, active for 100 minutes (5 in-game days).

Main article: /Structure

Structure blocks can be used to manually load pieces of the pillager outpost from the `/data/minecraft/structures/pillager_outpost` folder in minecraft.jar. To do so, set a structure block to Load mode, enter `pillager_outpost/_<structure_name>_`, and press LOAD.

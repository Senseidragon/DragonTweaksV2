A fox will trust and defend a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player \"Player\")\n that spawned it through [breeding](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeding \"Breeding\")\n, but will run away from non-sneaking players in any other circumstance.\n\nSpawning\n--------\n\nFoxes spawn in groups of two to four, and can spawn on [grass blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Grass_block \"Grass block\")\n, [coarse dirt](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Coarse_dirt \"Coarse dirt\")\n, [podzol](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Podzol \"Podzol\")\n, [snow blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snow_block \"Snow block\")\n, and [snow](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snow \"Snow\")\n. In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n, they can spawn on any block. 5% of them spawn as babies.\n\nFoxes have two color variants, red and snow, which depend on the [biome](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Biome \"Biome\")\n they spawn in. Red foxes spawn in [taigas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Taiga \"Taiga\")\n, [old growth pine taigas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Old_growth_pine_taiga \"Old growth pine taiga\")\n, [old growth spruce taigas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snowy_taiga \"Snowy taiga\")\n.\n\nWhen [bred](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bred \"Bred\")\n, the color of the baby fox that is spawned depends on the color of its Baby foxes follow adult foxes.\n\nDuring the [night](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Night \"Night\")\n, foxes sometimes go to nearby [villages](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Village \"Village\")\n.\n\nFoxes flee from nearby [wolves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolves \"Wolves\")\n, [polar bears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Polar_bear \"Polar bear\")\n or players unless the player is trusted or approaches them while [sneaking](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sneaking \"Sneaking\")\n. They take no damage or speed reduction while moving through [sweet berry bushes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sweet_berry_bush \"Sweet berry bush\")\n.\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Fox_jump.gif)\n\nAn animation showing a fox's jump during an attack on a [chicken](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chicken \"Chicken\")\n.\n\nFoxes attack [chickens](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chicken \"Chicken\")\n, [rabbits](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rabbit \"Rabbit\")\n, [cod](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cod \"Cod\")\n, [salmon](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Salmon \"Salmon\")\n and [tropical fish](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tropical_fish \"Tropical fish\")\n, and baby [turtles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Turtle \"Turtle\")\n while they are on land. Foxes don't attack [players](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player \"Player\")\n, [wolves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolves \"Wolves\")\n, [polar bears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Polar_bear \"Polar bear\")\n, [zoglins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zoglin \"Zoglin\")\n, \"Johnny\" [vindicators](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vindicator \"Vindicator\")\n, [wardens](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden \"Warden\")\n or [goats](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Goat \"Goat\")\n even if they are being attacked by them.\n\nFoxes attack either by running against or by pouncing. Like [rabbits](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rabbit \"Rabbit\")\n and [goats](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Goat \"Goat\")\n, foxes can pounce more than one block high without status effects or player input. Similar to goats, they do not attack the player when hit. A fox prepares to pounce by tilting and lowering its head and lowering its body to become shorter. Then it leaps up to four blocks\\[_[verify](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Special:TalkPage/Fox \"Special:TalkPage/Fox\")\\\n_\\] into the [air](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Air \"Air\")\n, jumping over [fences](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fence \"Fence\")\n and [walls](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wall \"Wall\")\n if needed. If the target mob moves from its location during this sequence, the pounce misses the target. In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n, foxes also shake side to side before pouncing and their legs wave while in midair.\n\nFoxes can safely fall 5 blocks before they start to accumulate fall damage, unlike most mobs, which can only fall 3 blocks safely.\n\nWhile foxes are capable of jumping over fences and walls, they cannot see through them. A fox does not try to attack a mob on the same y-level if there is a fence between them.\n\nRed foxes prefer to attack chickens, rabbits and baby turtles, all of which are on land, over cod, salmon, and tropical fish; conversely, snow foxes prefer to attack cod, salmon and tropical fish over land-dwelling creatures.\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Fox_in_snow_layer.png)\n\nA snow fox stuck in a snow layer when attacking.\n\nA fox that pounces into a [snow layer](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snow_layer \"Snow layer\")\n becomes momentarily stuck, remaining face-down, emitting [particles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Particles \"Particles\")\n while shaking, and returning to normal shortly after.\n\nFoxes swim to attack fish in water for food. Baby foxes following adults into the water cannot swim and may eventually drown.\n\nFoxes attempt to run toward the closest attackable [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob \"Mob\")\n, even if attached to a [lead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lead \"Lead\")\n, but do not attempt to break the lead. A fox wakes up from sleeping to attack a mob.\n\nWild wolves are aggressive toward foxes when within 18‌\\[_[JE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition \"Java Edition\")\\\n only_\\]/16‌\\[_[BE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\\\n only_\\] [blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block \"Block\")\n. In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition \"Java Edition\")\n, adult [polar bears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Polar_bear \"Polar bear\")\n also sneak toward foxes to attack them; in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n adult polar bears attack foxes within 16 blocks.\n\n### Sleeping\n\nFoxes are one of the few mobs in the game that can sleep, along with [cats](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cat \"Cat\")\n, [villagers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager \"Villager\")\n and [bats](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bat \"Bat\")\n.\n\nDuring the [day](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Day \"Day\")\n, if a [thunderstorm](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Thunderstorm \"Thunderstorm\")\n is not occurring, foxes attempt to find a space with a sky [light](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Light \"Light\")\n level of 14 or less and sleep. Block light has no effect on this. While it sleeps, it slowly moves its head up and down. A fox wakes if approached by a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player \"Player\")\n or [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob \"Mob\")\n. Foxes do not flee if a player approaches while [sneaking](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sneaking \"Sneaking\")\n until the player gets on an adjacent [block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block \"Block\")\n.\n\nIf the sky [light](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Light \"Light\")\n at the fox's position becomes 15 or above, either due to a [block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block \"Block\")\n above being destroyed or the fox being moved, the fox wakes up.\n\nFoxes can drop and pick up [items](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Item \"Item\")\n in their sleep.\n\nTrusting foxes can fall asleep even if attached to a [lead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lead \"Lead\")\n. They can also be moved with leads while sleeping.\n\nFoxes do not sleep if they are within 12 [blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block \"Block\")\n of an [armor stand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Armor_stand \"Armor stand\")\n, or a [camera](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Camera \"Camera\")\n in [_Minecraft Education_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Education \"Minecraft Education\")\n.\n\n### Sitting\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Information_icon.svg \"File:Information icon.svg\")\n\nThis section is missing information about: More specific mechanics\n\n \n\nPlease expand the section to include this information. Foxes that have not been bred by the player always sit while in the Nether, even while attached to a [lead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lead \"Lead\")\n.\n\n### Holding items\n\nIf any [item](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Item \"Item\")\n is on the ground near a fox, it travels to the item and picks it up, and the item appears in the fox's mouth. However, usually a fox chooses to attack a mob over picking up an item. This behavior is not limited to [food](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Food \"Food\")\n and animal products; a fox can pick up any item that the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player \"Player\")\n can pick up. However, they prefer to pick up food items, and if they have already picked up non-food items, they drop the non-food items to pick up food instead. A fox can also swap a food item it is holding for a different food item.\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Tamed_Fox_With_Egg_in_Mouth.png)\n\nA fox with an egg inside its mouth.\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Fox_harvesting_sweet_berries_without_eating_them.png)\n\nA fox harvesting sweet berries without eating them.\n\nA fox eats any food item it picks up (besides cake) after 30 seconds and the fox is affected by any side effects from the food, such as poison or teleportation. For example, if a fox eats an [enchanted golden apple](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enchanted_golden_apple \"Enchanted golden apple\")\n, it obtains the [Absorption](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Absorption \"Absorption\")\n, [Resistance](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Resistance \"Resistance\")\n, [Fire Resistance](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fire_Resistance \"Fire Resistance\")\n and [Regeneration](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Regeneration \"Regeneration\")\n. If a fox eats any type of soup or stew, an empty [bowl](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bowl \"Bowl\")\n is dropped. If a fox eats a [chorus fruit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chorus_fruit \"Chorus fruit\")\n, it teleports accordingly.\n\nIf there is a stage 3 or 4 [sweet berry bush](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sweet_berry_bush \"Sweet berry bush\")\n within a 16 [block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block \"Block\")\n radius of a fox, it sprints toward the bush and eats the [sweet berries](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sweet_berries \"Sweet berries\")\n, dropping the item it was holding. However, if the fox stands on a solid block directly above the sweet berry bush and is already holding an item, the fox will harvest the sweet berries, making them drop, but will not eat them nor drop the item it was holding. Foxes are not damaged or slowed by sweet berry bushes.\n\nAlthough visually holding items in its mouth, they are functionally being held in its [main hand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dual_wield \"Dual wield\")\n; therefore, the fox is affected by any effects of a given held item. A [sword](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sword \"Sword\")\n increases its damage, and a [totem of undying](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Totem_of_undying \"Totem of undying\")\n saves it from death, for instance. If a fox picks up a [weapon](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Weapon \"Weapon\")\n or an item with [enchantments](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enchantments \"Enchantments\")\n, such as Looting and Fire Aspect, these items affect the fox's attack as if the fox is wielding the weapon in its main hand, although the item retains its [durability](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Durability \"Durability\")\n when used by the fox. If a fox is holding a [totem of undying](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Totem_of_undying \"Totem of undying\")\n, it receives the effects after taking fatal damage. In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n, if a fox wields a sword with the Fire Aspect enchantment while attacking a mob on a [campfire](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Campfire \"Campfire\")\n or a soul campfire, it ignites the campfire and does critical [damage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage \"Damage\")\n to the entity.\n\nFoxes do not pick up items when `gamerule mobGriefing` is set to `false`.\n\n### Breeding\n\nWhen [sweet berries](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sweet_berries \"Sweet berries\")\n or [glow berries](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glow_berries \"Glow berries\")\n are used on two foxes, the foxes [breed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breed \"Breed\")\n, producing a baby fox. If a red fox is bred with a snow fox, the baby has a 50% chance of being either a red or snow fox.\n\n### Trust\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Player_being_attacked_by_a_fox.png)\n\nA fox that trusts a different player attacking the player.\n\n[Breeding](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeding \"Breeding\")\n two adults with [sweet berries](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sweet_berries \"Sweet berries\")\n or [glow berries](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glow_berries \"Glow berries\")\n produces a baby fox that trusts the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player \"Player\")\n that bred it and does not flee from that player as it grows up; however, because baby foxes also follow nearby adult foxes, an adult running away from the player may cause the baby to do the same.\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Trusting_fox.jpg)\n\nA trusting fox, notice it does not run from the player or attack the chicken next to it.\n\nNaturally spawned baby foxes do not trust players.\n\nFoxes attack specific [mobs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob \"Mob\")\n that hurt a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player \"Player\")\n they trust. When attacking phantoms, foxes do not jump to attack.\n\nIn [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n, foxes that trust a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player \"Player\")\n attack any mob that harms their trusted player, including other players. In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition \"Java Edition\")\n, foxes defend the player from the following damage sources:\n\n*   melee attacks from players and mobs except [dolphins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dolphin \"Dolphin\")\n    , [iron golems](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_golem \"Iron golem\")\n    , [wolves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolves \"Wolves\")\n    , [bees](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bee \"Bee\")\n    , [polar bears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Polar_bear \"Polar bear\")\n    , [killer bunnies](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Killer_bunnies \"Killer bunnies\")\n    , [slimes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slime \"Slime\")\n    , [magma cubes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Magma_cube \"Magma cube\")\n    , [hoglins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hoglin \"Hoglin\")\n    , or [zoglins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zoglin \"Zoglin\")\n    .\n*   projectile damage from arrows, fireballs or wind charges.\n*   defensive damage from pufferfish.\n\nFoxes do not retaliate against players who harm them, but they still attack players who damage players they trust.\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Trusting_fox_attacking_zombie.jpg)\n\nA trusting fox attacking a zombie that hit the player it trusts.\n\nTrusting foxes retreat from polar bears and wolves but not tamed wolves. A tamed wolf whose owner attacks a trusting fox also attacks the fox.\n\nThe player can summon a neutral fox in [singleplayer](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Singleplayer \"Singleplayer\")\n with the command `/[summon](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands/summon \"Commands/summon\")  fox ~ ~ ~ {Trusted:[I;<player UUID>]}`, where `<player UUID>` is the [UUID](https://minecraft.wiki/w/UUID \"UUID\")\n of the player as an int-array, with commas separating the 4 integers. Like other neutral mobs, foxes are passive in [Peaceful](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Peaceful \"Peaceful\")\n difficulty. Unlike other neutral mobs, a neutral fox is not aggravated by [firework](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Firework \"Firework\")\n damage.\n\nSounds\n------\n\n_[Java This is noticeable when they attempt to attack fish.\n*   There is a [splash](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Splash \"Splash\")\n     screen \"In case it isn't obvious, foxes aren't players\" in the game, which was added from the description of the bug [MC-166292](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MC-166292 \"mojira:MC-166292\")\n    .\n*   The first and third screeching sounds foxes make are the same. The first sound is the third in reverse, but higher-pitched, and the third is the first in reverse, but lower-pitched.\n*   Fox sounds are actually combined and heavily edited cat and dog

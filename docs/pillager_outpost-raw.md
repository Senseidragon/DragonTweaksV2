generate too close to, or within, villages. They can do so in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n as well as generate far away from villages.\n\nIn [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition \"Java Edition\")\n, if an outpost spawns in the water or in the air (e.g., spawning near a [savanna plateau](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Savanna_plateau \"Savanna plateau\")\n), then a platform of [grass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Grass_block \"Grass block\")\n (or [sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sand \"Sand\")\n), [dirt](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dirt \"Dirt\")\n, and [stone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stone \"Stone\")\n spawns underneath it in a circular shape. This also applies to the smaller structures such as tents, targets, [log](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Log \"Log\")\n piles, and cages. On occasion, the smaller structures still spawn in the air despite having a platform below them, depending on how high up they are.\n\nIn [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n, pillager outposts generate with foundations similar to that of a woodland mansion: watchtowers use [cobblestone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cobblestone \"Cobblestone\")\n, birch [planks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Planks \"Planks\")\n, and dark oak logs as their foundation; tents and cages generate dark oak planks as their foundation; log piles use horizontal dark oak logs as their foundation; and targets generate cobblestone as a foundation.\n\nThe outpost tower generates each time. Up to 4 other smaller structures may spawn around the periphery of the main outpost structure.\n\nEight extra [air](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Air \"Air\")\n blocks also generate around the outpost, inside of the ground, creating small trenches.[\\[3\\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_Outpost#cite_note-3)\n\nMobs\n----\n\n[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_Outpost?action=edit&section=2 \"Edit section: Mobs\")\n\n|     |     |     |\n| --- | --- | --- |\n| [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay \"Allay\") | [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Golem \"Iron Golem\") | [](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager \"Pillager\") |\n| [Allay](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay \"Allay\") | [Iron Golem](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Golem \"Iron Golem\") | [Pillager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager \"Pillager\") |\n\nIron golems and allays spawn only during the generation of the outpost structures. An iron golem has a 85% chance of spawning. Meanwhile, the allay has a 50% chance of spawning. These mobs spawn in cages. The iron golem can punch pillagers who are one block away from the fences of the cage.\n\nBoth regular pillagers and [pillager captains](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_captain \"Pillager captain\")\n can continuously spawn around the structure. Pillagers can still spawn if a player is inside the tower. In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition \"Java Edition\")\n, they spawn in a 72×58×72 area centered on the top level of the watchtower, the same level as the chest. Along the Y-axis, this area extends 28 blocks upward and 30 blocks downward from the center of the platform.\n\nIn [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n, pillagers spawn at the highest available position in up to 4 columns depending on which direction the structure generates. The number of spawning locations vary based on the orientation of the structure: 2 spots when the door is facing east/west (50%), 4 for north (25%), and 1 for south (25%). The first spawning column is one block diagonally toward the chest at the top from the center of the structure; the other possible 3 are 8 blocks east, south, and southeast of the first. Pillagers can spawn in these columns, beginning from the bottom floor and extending vertically up to 18 blocks. Pillagers spawn on the northwest corner of a block and require a clear 2×2×2 area for spawning. Any solid block in this region inhibits spawning, while transparent blocks like leaves or glass don't impede spawning if they aren't positioned on the actual spawning spot.\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:PillagerOutpost_SpawnBlock_Bedrock.png)\n\nPillagers always spawn on the red block in _[Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n_. Note that the entrance is at the bottom of this image.\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Pillager_spawn_request.jpeg)\n\nPillagers have a higher chance of spawning if there are more blocks where mobs spawn (e.g. ice, grass block, bedrock).\n\nAdditionally, pillagers can also spawn on spots in the outbuildings of the outpost. The spawning spots are either level with, or one block above the generated floor of the outbuilding. Extending the floor of an outbuilding outside the originally generated boundary of the floor can sometimes reveal additional spawning spots.\n\nIn [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition \"Java Edition\")\n, only pillagers may spawn within a pillager outpost spawn area. Pillagers may naturally spawn on any valid opaque block (except leaves) as long as the block light level is 8 or less. There is a 6% chance for a pillager to spawn as a patrol leader.\n\nIn [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition \"Java Edition\")\n, there are 15 jigsaw blocks that can generate the structures around the tower. Each structure has a 1 in 13 chance of being generated, including allay and iron golem cages. When generated, iron golem cages spawn one golem, and allay cages spawn two allays.\n\nA total of eight pillagers, including the pillager captain, spawns in a pillager outpost.\n\n### Bad Omen\n\n[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_Outpost?action=edit&section=3 \"Edit section: Bad Omen\")\n\nUpon killing an outpost captain, it drops an [Ominous Bottle](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ominous_Bottle \"Ominous Bottle\")\n of a random level between 1 and 5.

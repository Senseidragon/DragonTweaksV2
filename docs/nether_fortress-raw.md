Nether Fortress
===============

"Fortress" redirects here. For other uses, see [Fortress (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fortress_(disambiguation) "Fortress (disambiguation)")
.

A **Nether fortress** is an uncommon large [structure](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Structure "Structure")
 made of [Nether bricks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_brick "Nether brick")
 found in [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether "The Nether")
, consisting of bridges, corridors, and towers. Nether fortresses are the only place where [wither skeletons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_skeleton "Wither skeleton")
 and [blazes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze "Blaze")
 spawn. They also contain [Nether wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_wart "Nether wart")
.

Generation
----------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress?action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Generation")

Nether fortresses generate in all [Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether "Nether")
 biomes. To do so, the game splits the Nether into regions in which one of either a fortress or a [bastion remnant](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bastion_remnant "Bastion remnant")
 can generate. The regions are 432×432 blocks in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
 and 480×480 blocks in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
.

[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Java_Nether_Structure_Generation1.png)

The Nether structure generation in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
. The black lines represent each region and the dots represent coordinates. The green color is where they can generate and red is where they cannot.

Each region has a four chunk separation located on the south and east borders of the region in which neither a fortress nor a bastion can generate.

[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Bedrock_Nether_Structure_Generation.png)

The Nether structure generation in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
. The black lines represent each region and the dots represent coordinates. The green color is where they can generate and red is where they cannot.

This leaves only a 368×368 block section in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
 or 416×416 block section in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
 where a structure can generate.

Two structures never generate in the same region, although they might overlap if they generate close to the separation border. The chance of a fortress attempting to generate instead of a bastion is 2⁄5 (40%) in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
 and 1⁄3 (33.3%) in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
. However, if a bastion attempts to generate in the [basalt deltas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Basalt_deltas "Basalt deltas")
, a fortress generates instead, resulting in fortresses being the most common in the basalt deltas.

Nether fortresses can generate buried in [netherrack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherrack "Netherrack")
. In such a case, the interior is not filled with [netherrack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherrack "Netherrack")
; all hallways and passages are clear except for open walkways and bridges. It is possible but rare for [glowstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glowstone "Glowstone")
 or crimson and warped [huge fungi](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Huge_fungi "Huge fungi")
 to generate inside the fortress pathways.

Structure
---------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Structure")

Main article: [/Structure](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress/Structure "Nether Fortress/Structure")

Nether fortresses are large complexes composed of [Nether bricks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_bricks "Nether bricks")
 supported by pillars that tower high above the [lava seas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lava_sea "Lava sea")
.

[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Enclosed_Fortress.png)

Segments of a Nether fortress that are fully enclosed.

The fortress generation starts with a plain four-way crossing centered at chunk coordinates 11, ~, 11 of the designated [chunk](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chunk "Chunk")
.

[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Nether_Fortress_Crossroad.png)

A crossroad found where walkways intersect.

A fortress has two areas, an exterior area of open bridges and an interior area of enclosed corridors. Both the bridges and corridors can end in an unfinished passageway structure or may simply end without elaboration. Fortresses can tunnel through [netherrack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherrack "Netherrack")
, giving the "exterior" areas an appearance of tunnels with Nether brick floor and [netherrack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherrack "Netherrack")
 walls and ceilings. At broken sections the terrain is not cleared, which may create a tunnel that leads straight into a wall of [netherrack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherrack "Netherrack")
.

[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Blaze_Fortress.png)

The general pattern of the walkways.

[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Nether_Brick_Well.png)

A lava well found inside a Nether fortress.

The exterior consists of:

*   Straight bridges.
*   Up to five plain four-way crossings.
*   Up to four four-way crossings with arches made of Nether brick and Nether brick fences.
*   Up to four small rooms with a single entrance and full-block "stairs" leading to the roof, which may have a single path leading out.
*   Up to 2 blaze monster spawner platforms: structures consisting of three full-block "stairs" leading to a small platform fenced with Nether brick fence, with a [blaze](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze "Blaze")
     [monster spawner](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Monster_spawner "Monster spawner")
     in the center.

[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Nether_Fortress_Large_Stairs_Room.png)

Stairs in a Nether fortress, with [Nether wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_wart "Nether wart")
 growing next to them.

The interior of the structures have 1×2 windows with [Nether brick fences](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fence "Fence")
 as the windowpanes. The fences also form gate-like structures at the entrances of some rooms and corridors. Rooms include:

*   The lava well room, which is the connection between the interior and exterior areas.
*   Straight corridors.
*   Up to five four-way crossings.
*   Up to 20 corridor turns (10 right-turns and 10 left-turns), each with a 1⁄3 chance of having a [loot chest](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress#Loot)
     in the corner.
*   Up to three stairways made from actual stair blocks, leading downward.
*   Up to two three-way intersections with a small exterior balcony.
*   Up to two stairways leading up to a garden of [soul sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Soul_sand "Soul sand")
     and [Nether wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_wart "Nether wart")
     at the base of the stairs, a corridor leading away from the upper landing and a corridor behind the stairs. If the room is generated embedded in netherrack, only one block above the landing is cleared, with a further indent finishing above the stairs.

[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Nether_fortress_corridor_crossing_%22area_bounding_blocks%22.png)

An outline of the "bounding boxes".

### Bounding boxes

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress?action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Bounding boxes")

The structure bounding box for the 4-way intersection is pictured above (top-down view), and consists of a 19×11×19 volume centered on the floor block in the center of the intersection. This contrasts many of the other structure bounding boxes as their outlines tend to tightly follow the physical bounds of the structure. Notably, this also means that the fortress supports, which generate outside of that volume and go down to the ground and often into the lava ocean, are not considered part of the fortress for spawning purposes.

The area bounding box is a rectangular box that covers the entire fortress (again, excluding the supports). This is simply the smallest rectangular bounding box that can hold the bounding boxes of all structure pieces in the fortress.

Mobs
----

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress?action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Mobs")

Fortresses use a list of possible [mobs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob "Mob")
 to spawn that is separate from the rest of [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether "The Nether")
, regardless of the biome the fortress generates in. This includes [zombified piglins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombified_piglin "Zombified piglin")
, [skeletons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton "Skeleton")
, and [magma cubes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Magma_cube "Magma cube")
, as well as two exclusive mobs not found anywhere else: [blazes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze "Blaze")
 and [wither skeletons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_skeleton "Wither skeleton")
.

[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Nether_Fortress_Blaze_Spawner.png)

A blaze monster spawner generated in the Nether fortress.

Mobs spawn at a much higher rate if the fortress is surrounded by [soul sand valley](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Soul_sand_valley "Soul sand valley")
 or [warped forest](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warped_forest "Warped forest")
 biomes, as hostile mobs in these biomes spawn much less frequently, allowing more hostile mobs to spawn in the fortress‌\[_[JE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
 only_\][\[1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress#cite_note-1)
.

### Java Edition

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Java Edition")

In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
, the spawning algorithm has two checks:

1.  It checks if the spawn coordinates are within the "bounding box" of a single piece (e.g. corridor or walkway) of the fortress. (referred as "structure bounding box" above) In this case the block type of the ground does not matter.
2.  It checks if the spawn coordinates are within the "bounding box" (referred as "area bounding box" above) of the entire fortress and whether the ground consists of [Nether bricks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_bricks "Nether bricks")
     (_not_ [Nether brick slabs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_brick_slab "Nether brick slab")
    ).

If either check passes, it uses the special mob list for fortresses rather than the list for the biome when choosing the mob to spawn. The actual mob spawning proceeds as normal for the mob chosen from this list.

### Bedrock Edition

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress?action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Bedrock Edition")

In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
, instead of spawning anywhere within a structural bounding box, _most_ mobs spawn only in _structure spawn locations_ along varied-length lines spaced apart 4-11 blocks throughout the fortress. They are not set to a particular Y level other than "inside the structure" and are indeed columns several blocks high. They do not require any special type of block, any regular spawnable block suffices.

To identify these spawning columns, glass panes or iron bars can be placed all over the fortress, 1 block above surface blocks. This keeps the mobs stationary. (This technique works in Bedrock because structure spawns occur in the northwest corner of blocks.) Note that these spots may be on top of the raised side blocks, so these side blocks have to be removed before a glass pane grid can be placed.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress?action=edit&section=21 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   In rare cases, a fortress may not generate any indoor rooms except one single lava room[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress#cite_note-8)
    . This usually occurs when a portion of the exterior section of the fortress blocks the exit to the interior section of the lava well room.
*   Sometimes, two or more fortresses can generate close to or even within one another, creating an even larger cumulative fortress.
*   There is an opening out of the fortress under the lava well, with a single block of [Nether bricks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_bricks "Nether bricks")
     from which the [lava](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lava "Lava")
     spreads out.
*   The soul sand valley [bone blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone_blocks "Bone blocks")
     and [basalt pillars](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Basalt_pillars "Basalt pillars")
     (down to within three blocks as-usual of a walkway) can replace more Nether bricks. Less-often, [huge fungi](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Huge_fungi "Huge fungi")
    , [Nether wart blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_wart_block "Nether wart block")
    , [warped wart blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warped_wart_block "Warped wart block")
    , [crimson stems](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crimson_stem "Crimson stem")
    , and [warped stems](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warped_stem "Warped stem")
     can replace Nether brick, most visibly along walls.
*   A fortress can be seen in the background in the Nether in _[A Minecraft Movie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/A_Minecraft_Movie "A Minecraft Movie")
    _.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress#cite_note-9)

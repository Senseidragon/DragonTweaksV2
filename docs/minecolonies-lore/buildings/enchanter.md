The [[Enchanter's Tower]] is where the Enchanter produces enchanted books, provided they have an Ancient Tome to work with. The Enchanter spends their days wandering the colony and watching other workers go about their routines — the more they observe, and the higher their Mana skill, the more potent the enchantments they are able to produce. They will not apply enchanted books to tools or armor; that part is left to the player.

A higher tower level and a stronger Knowledge skill both raise the ceiling on enchantment quality. Lower-level towers tend to produce weaker enchantments far more often than strong ones; a fully upgraded tower makes higher-level enchantments possible, though they remain uncommon.

The Enchanter can also craft scrolls on request. The Ultrasafe Colony Teleport Scroll lets a player return to their colony and is made from paper, a compass, and a Scepter of Gold. A Group Teleport variant exists that can move multiple players at once, but requires the tower to be at least level 2. Once the More Scrolls research is completed at the [[University]], the Enchanter can also craft the Spatial Guard Reinforcements Scroll, which calls in guard support, and the Worker-Where-Are-You Scroll, which reveals the location of a stuck or missing worker.

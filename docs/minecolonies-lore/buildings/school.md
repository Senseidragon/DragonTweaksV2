The [[School]] is where the Teacher will level up the Pupils (children) their Intelligence skill. Paper will increase the leveling speed of the Pupils. 

The level of the [[School]] determines how many Pupils can be taught at a time.

| Building Level | Pupils Taught |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 1              | 2                                             |
| 2              | 4                                             |
| 3              | 6                                             |
| 4              | 8                                             |
| 5              | 10                                            |

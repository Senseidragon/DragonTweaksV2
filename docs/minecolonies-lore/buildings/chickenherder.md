The [[Chicken Farmer's Hut]] Hut is where the Chickenherder will raise chickens, collect eggs, and butcher chickens for food. You will have to capture and bring in two chickens to the [[Chicken Farmer's Hut]], as the Chickenherder will not catch and bring in any chickens.

> **Note:** The Chickenherder will only keep two chickens alive per hut level, so at level 5 they will have 10 chickens in their holding pens to breed and butcher. This means they will be faster at producing and collecting meat, drops, and other byproducts, like eggs. So:

| Building Level | Chickens Housed |
| -------------- | --------------- |
| 1              | 2               |
| 2              | 4               |
| 3              | 6               |
| 4              | 8               |
| 5              | 10              |

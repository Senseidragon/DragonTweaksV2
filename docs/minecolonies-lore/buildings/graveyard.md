The [[Graveyard]] is where the Undertaker buries colonists who have died. When a citizen falls, they leave behind a grave containing everything they had in their inventory at the time of death. The player can interact with the grave to retrieve those items directly. The Undertaker will walk to the grave — or run, if the relevant research has been completed — collect its contents, and return to the [[Graveyard]].

On returning, the Undertaker attempts to revive the deceased citizen. The base revival chance is very low, but it can be improved through research, the Undertaker's own Mana skill, the level of the [[Graveyard]], and the use of totems. The maximum possible revival chance is 12.5%, with the ceiling raised by upgrading the [[Mystical Site]] and using multiple totems. By default without upgrades or totems, the cap is only 2.5%.

If revival fails, the Undertaker buries the citizen in the [[Graveyard]] and places a named grave marker. This marker does not store items. A higher-level [[Graveyard]] can accommodate more graves before running out of designated burial space, though the actual capacity varies by colony style. The Undertaker is exempt from the mourning period that affects other colonists after a death, so they can continue working even when the rest of the colony grieves.

The [[Gatehouse]] stations two guards at fixed positions in the structure to defend your colony's entrance. Unlike the [[Guard Tower]] or [[Barracks]], guards at the [[Gatehouse]] do not patrol. They remain at their designated positions at all times but will fight back when engaged by hostile mobs.

You may hire any combination of two guards: two Knights, two Archers, or one of each.

> **Note:** The [[Gatehouse]] can only be upgraded to level 3, not level 5.

The [[Gatehouse]] also enables colony connections. By placing colony signs in the world, you can create connections between your colony and other colonies. These connections are the foundation for future trading, diplomacy, and inter-colony mechanics.

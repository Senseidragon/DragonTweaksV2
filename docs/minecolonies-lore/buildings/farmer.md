The [[Farmer's Hut]] is where the Farmer tends fields and grows crops for the colony. The Farmer works with wheat, carrots, potatoes, beets, melons, pumpkins, and most modded crops with normal growth behavior. To get started, the Farmer needs a hoe, an axe for harvesting, and the seed or planting material for the crop they will grow. The day/night cycle must be active — each day the Farmer completes one action per field, whether hoeing, planting, or harvesting.

Fields are set up by placing a Field block — a scarecrow-shaped marker — in a plot of farmland, then opening its interface to assign a crop. For crops without separate seed items like carrots or potatoes, place the raw vegetable directly into the Field. The field can be resized up to 11x11 blocks around the marker. The Farmer can maintain one field per building level, up to five fields at level 5.

The Farmer can also craft seeds, carved pumpkins, hay bales, and coarse dirt on request, provided they have been taught the recipes. The number of craftable recipes doubles with each building level: 10 at level 1, rising to 160 at level 5.

MineColonies adds several crops that only the Farmer can grow — players cannot cultivate them directly. These colony-exclusive crops produce higher-quality food for citizens. Which crops are available depends on the local biome; see the [[Food]] page for details.

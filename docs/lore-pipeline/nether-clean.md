## Water does not work here

Liquid water cannot exist in the Nether under normal play. There is no way to place a water source here at all -- a water bucket used against any block produces a hiss and a burst of steam and leaves you holding an empty bucket; the water never appears. The one exception is a cauldron, which can hold water normally. This means you cannot bucket water to put out fire on yourself or on the ground, and you cannot pour water next to lava to make obsidian, anywhere in the Nether.

## Fire and lava

Fire here never burns out on its own. Lava is far more dangerous than in the Overworld -- it flows about twice as far and three times as fast. Lava pockets are buried throughout the netherrack in every biome, so unsupported mining is a real risk of breaking into a hidden pocket.

## Light and navigation

There is no day or night here -- light comes only from fire, lava, portals, glowstone, and shroomlights. Even in pitch darkness the ambient light is roughly equal to light level 8 in the Overworld, which is brighter than a fully dark Overworld night. Compasses spin uselessly unless bound to a lodestone; clocks just spin and cannot tell time. Maps render as useless static. None of these tools work normally here.

## Beds

Do not sleep in a bed in the Nether. It explodes instead of letting you sleep, with explosion power comparable to a TNT block.

## Biomes

Nether Wastes is the most common biome (~36%) -- open netherrack terrain with glowstone clusters; ghasts, zombified piglins, and magma cubes are common here. Crimson Forest (~22%) has tall crimson fungi, weeping vines, and shroomlights, with hoglins, piglins, and zombified piglins. Soul Sand Valley (~17%) has soul sand, basalt, exposed bone fossils, and soul fire under a blue fog; ghasts and skeletons are common, endermen rare. Basalt Deltas (~16%) is a volcanic field of basalt and blackstone with a very high magma cube spawn rate. Warped Forest (~9%) is the safest biome -- endermen and striders are the only mobs that spawn there.

## Structures

Nether Fortresses hold blazes and wither skeletons. Bastion Remnants (blackstone fortifications, generate everywhere except Basalt Deltas) hold piglins and piglin brutes. Nether Fossils (bone-block formations unique to Soul Sand Valley) are decorative/loot only. Ruined Portals are broken, partially-buried portal frames with a loot chest nearby. In Java Edition, a given Nether region generates either a fortress or a bastion, never both.

## Mobs that spawn here

Blazes, wither skeletons, skeletons, ghasts, hoglins, magma cubes, piglins, piglin brutes, zombified piglins, and endermen are all native to the Nether. Striders are the only fully passive Nether-native mob.

## Size and building

The Nether is 128 blocks tall, capped by a roughly 5-layer bedrock ceiling at the top and another 5-layer bedrock floor at the bottom -- you cannot build or mine past either.

## Other hazards

Snow golems take rapid fire damage and die almost immediately here unless protected by Fire Resistance -- never bring one through a portal unprotected.

# Nether Portal -- Complete Article

## Overview

The **Nether portal** is a player-constructed structure made of obsidian that serves as a gateway between the Overworld and the Nether dimensions in Minecraft.

## Creation

### Construction Requirements

Portals are built as vertical rectangular frames of obsidian with dimensions ranging from 4x5 (minimum) to 23x23 (maximum). The four corner blocks are optional but automatically included when portals are generated by the game. Adjacent portals may share obsidian blocks, and horizontal construction (like End portals) is not possible.

### Activation

Once the obsidian frame is complete, fire must be placed inside to activate the portal, creating the characteristic vortex appearance. Fire can be applied through:

- Flint and steel
- Fire charges
- Fireball or small fireball impacts
- Lightning strikes
- Natural fire spread to adjacent flammable materials

Important note: "The fire must be the last placed block in the structure -- a fire on an incomplete frame does not result in the portal activating upon the placement of the last obsidian block."

Portals cannot be activated in the End or custom dimensions, only in the Overworld or Nether.

### Portal Generation

If no corresponding portal exists within range when an entity uses a portal, the game automatically creates one. "If no corresponding portal within range exists in the other dimension when a portal is used by almost any entity except withers and the ender dragon (Java Edition only) or by a player (Bedrock Edition only), one is created there."

## Behavior

### Teleportation Mechanics

Player travel:
- Survival mode: 80 game ticks (4 seconds) in portal
- Creative mode: 1 game tick (1/20 second) in portal

Players can exit the portal before the animation completes to abort teleportation. Java Edition allows configuration via gamerules `playersNetherPortalDefaultDelay` and `playersNetherPortalCreativeDelay`.

Entity travel:
- Most mobs, items, and vehicles can pass through portals
- Exceptions: Withers and Ender Dragons cannot travel through portals
- Non-player entities travel instantly
- 300 game tick (15-second) cooldown after travel prevents immediate re-entry
- Exception: Minecarts and boats have 10 tick (0.5-second) cooldown
- Projectiles have 0.1-second cooldown (as of version 24w33a)

Special conditions:
- In Bedrock Edition, a parrot on the player's shoulder prevents portal transit
- Entities with passengers can now use portals by default (as of Java Edition 1.21-pre1)

### Chunk Loading (Java Edition Only)

When an entity teleports through a Nether portal, the destination chunk loads at level 30 (fully loaded with entity processing). This effect spreads to adjacent chunks with decreasing intensity, creating 8 additional "entity ticking" chunks. "These chunks remain loaded for 15 seconds but this timer gets refreshed each time the entity passes through the portal (including mobs wandering through it from either direction)."

This mechanic enables construction of "chunk loaders" but "Permanently-loaded chunks created using chunk loaders create a measurable amount of lag."

### Portal Linkage Between Dimensions

#### Coordinate Conversion

The Nether uses a 1:8 horizontal coordinate ratio to the Overworld:
- Overworld to Nether: (floor(X / 8), Y, floor(Z / 8))
- Nether to Overworld: (X x 8, Y, Z x 8)

The Y-axis remains unchanged.

#### Portal Search

The game searches for existing portals within specific ranges:
- Overworld search: 256x256 block area (128-block radius)
- Nether search: 32x32 block area (16-block radius)

"Starting at these destination coordinates, the game looks for all nearby portal points of interest...If any candidate portal point of interest is found, then the game selects the closest one as determined by its distance in the new coordinate system (including the Y coordinate, which can cause seemingly more distant portals to be selected), and teleports the entity to the location in the new portal calculated by a special algorithm."

Distance calculation uses Euclidean distance, not taxicab distance, and includes Y-coordinate differences.

#### Entity Positioning Algorithm

When portals have the same orientation and shape, entities attempt to preserve their relative position. If the destination portal is at 90 degrees to the source portal, entity yaw and velocity are rotated 90 degrees clockwise.

#### Portal Creation

If no existing portal is found, the game creates one by looking for the closest suitable location within 16 blocks horizontally (any distance vertically) of the incoming entity's destination coordinates.

Valid locations must have:
- 3x4 buildable blocks
- 4 blocks of air above all 12 blocks
- Long axis matching the source portal's orientation

If no suitable 3x4 location exists, the game searches for a 1x4 area with 4 blocks of air above.

Forced portal creation: If both checks fail, a portal is forced at the target coordinates (Y constrained between 70 and 10 below world height: 118 for Nether, 310 for Overworld). A 2x3 platform of obsidian with air 3 high above is created at the target location, overwriting whatever might be there.

Note on large portals: if a portal constructed in the Nether exceeds 18 blocks wide, each end will connect to two separate Overworld portals.

### Mob Behavior

#### Zombified Piglins

In Java Edition, Zombified Piglins have a chance to spawn on the bottom frame of Overworld portals if any Nether portal block above receives a block tick. In Bedrock Edition, they spawn in squares adjacent to portals, not inside them.

Spawn frequency varies by difficulty:
- Easy: baseline
- Normal: twice as often as Easy
- Hard: three times as often as Easy

Spawned Zombified Piglins have a full 15-second portal cooldown and cannot re-enter the spawning portal immediately.

#### Other Mobs

No other mobs spawn from Nether portals. However, "Active portals also repel hoglins."

### GUI Restrictions

"The player cannot open their inventory while standing in an active portal, and any other GUI (such as that of a chest or villager) is immediately closed when opened."

## Trivia

- Portals can be placed tunnel-like, sharing frames. More than six connected portals make inner portals invisible (though particles remain visible). Connected portals share the 4-second countdown.
- Intersecting portals: if two portals intersect and fire is lit in the intersection, only the portal aligned with the x-axis activates.
- Nether portals can theoretically spawn pre-lit if ruined portals or woodland mansion obsidian rooms intersect to form complete frames with fire spreading naturally into the middle.

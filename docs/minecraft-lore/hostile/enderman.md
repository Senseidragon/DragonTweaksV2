# Enderman

A tall (2.9 blocks) neutral mob with 40 HP found in all three dimensions. The only natural source of ender pearls. Provoked by eye contact -- looking at its face for 1/4 second triggers aggro. Teleports constantly to avoid damage. Immune to most projectiles. Picks up and moves certain blocks. Extremely hostile to endermites.

## Stats

- Health: 40 HP
- Attack: Easy 4.5 HP, Normal 7 HP, Hard 10.5 HP
- Speed: 0.3 (faster when aggro)

## Spawning

Spawns in all Overworld biomes at light 0 except mushroom fields and deep dark. Spawns in all End biomes at light 0 -- very densely in the End. Spawns in Nether in warped forests (most common), Nether wastes (rare), and soul sand valleys (very rare). Only mob that spawns naturally in all three dimensions.

## Provoking

An enderman is passive until:
- A player/mob directly attacks it
- A player within 64 blocks looks at its face (including back of head, upper legs) for 5 game ticks (1/4 sec) without a carved pumpkin equipped
- It detects an endermite within 64 blocks

**Exemption**: Looking through a transparent block or wearing a carved pumpkin helmet -- the enderman is not provoked.

## Staring / Paralysis

Maintaining eye contact from within 16 blocks of an already-aggravated enderman paralyzes it (stops moving). It continues shaking and screaming but cannot attack. Moving within 4 blocks of a paralyzed enderman causes it to teleport away immediately.

## Combat

When provoked, runs to the back of the player to attack. Does not attack from the front. Teleports frequently during combat (up to 19 blocks horizontal, 22 blocks vertical per attempt). Pursues the player through walls indefinitely while in follow range.

**Water**: Takes 1 HP damage from rain, water, splash water bottles. Continuously teleports in rain until it finds a dry location or dies.

**Projectile immunity**: Teleports away from incoming arrows, tridents, and most projectiles before they land. NOT immune to the warden's sonic boom.

## Teleportation

Teleports when dodging projectiles, chasing a target, taking damage, in bright sky during the day, and randomly. Each attempt picks a random spot in a 64x64x64 cube. Requires at least 3 non-solid blocks above the destination.

Endermen cannot teleport while in a minecart or boat (though they try repeatedly in rain, dying in the process).

## Block Picking

5% chance each tick to pick up a random nearby block if it is on the holdable list (includes dirt, clay, sand, gravel, mushrooms, pumpkins, TNT, cactus, and several others). Cannot pick up from flat full-block floors. Does not despawn while holding a block.

## Drops

- Ender pearl (0-1 per kill)

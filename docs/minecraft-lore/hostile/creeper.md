# Creeper

A silent hostile mob with 20 HP that detonates when within 3 blocks of the player. The most structurally dangerous mob -- explosions destroy blocks, can breach walls and floors, and a charged variant hits with twice the power of TNT. Creepers make no idle sounds, making them nearly impossible to detect until they start hissing.

## Stats

- Health: 20 HP
- Explosion power (normal): 3 (comparable to TNT)
- Explosion power (charged): 6 (equivalent to an end crystal)
- Maximum explosion damage:
  - Normal creeper: Easy 22.5 HP, Normal 43 HP, Hard 64.5 HP
  - Charged creeper: Easy 43.5 HP, Normal 85 HP, Hard 127.5 HP
- Fuse time: 1.5 seconds (30 ticks)
- Trigger range: within 3 blocks of player

## Spawning

At light level 0, any biome except mushroom fields and deep dark. Always spawn alone.

Charged creeper: when lightning strikes within 4 blocks of a normal creeper. Can be triggered naturally, by Channeling trident, or lightning rod.

## Behavior

Creepers pursue any player within 16 blocks (14 blocks if player is crouching). They do NOT attack other mobs unless attacked first.

When within 3 blocks, the creeper stops, hisses, and begins a 1.5-second countdown. The explosion requires an uninterrupted line of sight for the full fuse duration -- breaking line of sight cancels the detonation even if the hiss has already started. A detonation is also canceled if the player moves more than 7 blocks away. The hiss sound plays in full regardless of whether it explodes.

Creepers jump down toward players if they can survive the fall; large falls pre-charge the swell (fall from 16+ blocks can trigger explosion within 5 ticks of landing).

Despite having no arms, creepers can climb ladders and vines, but only by accident -- they do not intentionally path through them.

Using flint and steel or a fire charge directly on a creeper forces detonation.

## Weaknesses

- No idle sounds -- approach is nearly silent. Only footstep sounds, indistinguishable from the player's own.
- Does NOT flee from wolves, iron golems, or zoglins -- these mobs will be damaged or killed by a surprise detonation.
- IS fled by nothing -- creepers do not fear non-cat mobs.
- Tamed wolves, iron golems, and zoglins do NOT pre-target creepers; they will not attack approaching creepers unprovoked.

## Cat and Ocelot Counter

Creepers flee from cats and ocelots within 6 blocks at speed faster than their pursuit speed. They do not attack cats or ocelots. A creeper that has already started its detonation countdown does not flee unless the player also leaves blast radius.

Detection range halved (8 blocks) when player wears a creeper head.

## Charged Creeper and Mob Heads

A charged creeper explosion that kills a zombie, skeleton, wither skeleton, piglin, or creeper causes one of those mobs to drop its corresponding mob head. Only one head drops per explosion even if multiple valid mobs die. This is the only natural way to obtain zombie, skeleton, wither skeleton, and piglin mob heads.

Charged creepers have the same 1.5-second fuse as normal creepers.

## Drops

- Gunpowder (primary)
- Music disc (if killed by a skeleton's arrow)
- Creeper head (only from charged creeper explosion)

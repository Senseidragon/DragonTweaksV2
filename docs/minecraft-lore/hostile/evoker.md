# Evoker

A spell-casting illager with 24 HP and 2 armor points. The rarest and most dangerous raid mob, found in woodland mansions and waves 5+ of raids. Evokers are the only source of totems of undying. They use two attacks: armor-piercing fang lines and summoned vex swarms.

## Stats

- Health: 24 HP
- Armor: 2 points
- Fang attack: Easy 4 HP, Normal 6 HP, Hard 9 HP (bypasses armor; not mitigated by armor but is mitigated by Protection enchantment)
- Vex summon (vex attack): Easy 5.5 HP, Normal 9 HP, Hard 13.5 HP per vex hit
- Spell cooldown: 2 seconds after fang attack, 5 seconds after vex summon

## Spawning

**Woodland mansions**: Floors 2 and 3 only, in specific rooms. Do not respawn after initial spawn. Do not despawn naturally (unless difficulty set to Peaceful).

**Raids**: Wave 5+ on Normal/Hard. Can spawn riding a ravager at wave 7 (Hard only). Up to 5 evokers on Hard difficulty. May spawn as a raid captain.

## Fang Attack

Evokers summon 16 evoker fangs in a straight line toward the target. At close range (within 3 blocks), they summon two rings of fangs around themselves instead (5 inner, 8 outer). Fangs appear with a grey-purple particle and low-pitched sound.

Fangs do not seek -- they rise from the ground at fixed positions and can be dodged. Fangs cannot spawn inside deep pits or above walls; they follow the terrain (up stairs, for example). An evoker in a boat over water cannot summon fangs because there are no opaque blocks beneath.

Fangs deal no damage to any illager. Any neutral or hostile mob (except illagers and goats) hit by fangs retaliates and attacks the evoker.

## Vex Summon

Evokers summon 3 vexes when there are fewer than 8 vexes within 16 blocks. Summon signal is purple-white particles and a higher-pitched sound. Vexes can spawn inside solid blocks if those blocks obstruct the target spawn point -- so fewer than 3 may appear.

Evokers can simultaneously summon fangs while casting vexes if the player attacks them during the vex cast.

## Sheep Spell

When not in combat, evokers turn any blue sheep within 16 blocks from blue to red ("wololo" sound and brownish particles). Purely cosmetic in most contexts.

## Cross-Mob Interactions

- Fang attacks never damage illagers
- Vexes summoned by the evoker do not attack illagers
- Evokers can ride ravagers during raids
- Any non-illager non-goat mob hit by evoker fangs retaliates against the evoker

## Drops

- Totem of Undying (always -- only source in survival)
- Emerald (rare)

# Zoglin

An undead hostile mob with 40 HP created when a hoglin spends 15 seconds in the Overworld or End. Attacks all players and nearly all mobs indiscriminately -- the only exceptions are creepers, ghasts, and other zoglins. Never retreats or flees (unlike hoglins). Uses the same tusk-upward launch attack as hoglins; shield does NOT prevent the fling.

## Stats

- Health: 40 HP
- Adult attack: Easy 2.5-5 HP, Normal 3-8 HP, Hard 4.5-12 HP
- Baby attack: Easy/Normal 0.5 HP, Hard 0.75 HP
- Knockback resistance: 60%
- Attack rate: adults every 2 sec (Java), babies every 1 sec

## Spawning

Created only by hoglin zombification. A hoglin in the Overworld or End shakes for 15 seconds then converts to a zoglin. The new zoglin gets Nausea I for 10 seconds (cosmetic only).

Baby zoglins never grow up -- a baby hoglin that converts becomes a permanently-baby zoglin.

## Behavior

- Attacks all survival/adventure mode players and mobs EXCEPT: creepers, ghasts, and other zoglins
- This includes hoglins (its own converted kin) and zombified piglins
- Adult tosses its target into the air; shield does NOT prevent the fling
- Baby attacks but does not fling
- Hoglins do not attack zoglins unless provoked by them
- When a zoglin attacks a piglin: the piglin retreats or fights back
- Piglin brutes never retreat from zoglins; they counter-attack if attacked first or if a nearby piglin/brute is attacked

## Undead Properties

- Damaged by Instant Health, healed by Instant Damage
- Smite enchantment effective
- Ignored by the Wither

## Drops

- Rotten flesh (1-3)

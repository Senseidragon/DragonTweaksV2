# Endermite

A tiny hostile arthropod with 8 HP. Has a 5% chance to spawn when a player-thrown ender pearl lands. Despawns within 2 minutes regardless of distance unless named with a name tag. Endermen are inherently hostile toward endermites -- this can be exploited to lure endermen.

## Stats

- Health: 8 HP
- Hitbox: 0.3 blocks tall, 0.4 blocks wide
- Attack: Easy/Normal 2 HP, Hard 3 HP

## Spawning

5% chance on ender pearl landing. On Java, spawns at the player's original position; on Bedrock, spawns at the pearl's landing site.

Despawns within 2 minutes even if in a minecart, boat, or boat with chest -- unless named with a name tag.

## Behavior

- Attacks players on sight
- Endermen are hostile toward any endermite -- this makes named endermites useful for drawing endermen to a specific location (enderman farm lure mechanism)

## Drops

None.

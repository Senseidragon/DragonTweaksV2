# Bogged

A mushroom-covered skeleton variant found in swamps and mangrove swamps. 16 HP (less than a regular skeleton's 20). Fires Poison arrows and attacks at a slower rate than other skeletons. Shearing removes the mushrooms (2 mushrooms drop) but does not harm the bogged; it cannot be sheared again. Does NOT convert to a stray in powder snow -- it takes freeze damage instead.

## Stats

- Health: 16 HP
- Attack (bow): Easy 2-4 HP, Normal 3-5 HP, Hard 4-8 HP + Poison 4 sec (3 HP total)
- Attack cooldown: 3.5 sec on Easy/Normal, 2.5 sec on Hard (1.5 sec slower than a regular skeleton)

## Spawning

Swamps and mangrove swamps, replacing about 30% of skeletons. In swamps: groups of 4 at light level 0. In mangrove swamps: groups of 1-2 at light levels 0-7 (can spawn during the day under dense canopy). 1/3 chance selected as "ranged" mob for trial chambers.

## Behavior

- Shoots Poison arrows: Poison 4 sec, 3 HP drain per arrow (does not kill below 0.5 HP)
- Slower fire rate than skeletons -- leaves larger windows for approach
- Holding a tipped arrow or spectral arrow in offhand always fires that type (arrow not consumed)
- If a bogged kills a creeper, the creeper drops a random music disc (shares the "skeletons" entity type tag)
- Takes freeze damage from powder snow; does NOT convert to a stray
- Immune to Poison (undead)

## Shearing

Using shears on a bogged drops 2 mushrooms (each randomly brown or red). After shearing, the mushrooms are permanently removed and the bogged cannot be sheared again. This does not affect its combat behavior.

## Drops

- Bone
- Arrow

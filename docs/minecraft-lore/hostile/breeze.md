# Breeze

A hostile mob with 30 HP found only in trial chambers. Moves by jumping large distances. Attacks with wind charges that deal almost no direct damage but launch targets into the air -- fall damage is the real threat. Deflects all projectiles except wind charges. The only source of breeze rods.

## Stats

- Health: 30 HP
- Wind charge: Easy/Normal 1 HP, Hard 1.5 HP (direct hit)
- Attack range: 16 blocks
- Attack cooldown: 32 ticks (1.6 seconds)

## Spawning

Spawns from specific trial spawners surrounded by chiseled tuff, found only in combat chambers (never corridors). One breeze spawner per combat chamber, usually in the center. Spawning requires line of sight to the trial spawner.

## Behavior

**Wind charge**: Deals minimal direct damage. The wind burst from impact launches the target upward -- fall damage is the primary danger. Wind charges can be deflected by hitting them with a melee attack or another projectile (similar to ghast fireballs).

**Block interactions**: Wind bursts toggle non-iron doors, trapdoors, buttons, levers, and fence gates; extinguish lit candles; and ring bells. These are affected by the `mobGriefing` game rule (except bell ringing).

**Projectile deflection**: The breeze deflects all incoming projectiles (arrows, crossbow bolts, etc.) except wind charges. Direct melee attacks are not deflected.

**Movement**: Jumps aggressively -- covers large horizontal and vertical distances to reposition.

## Drops

- Breeze rod (only source)

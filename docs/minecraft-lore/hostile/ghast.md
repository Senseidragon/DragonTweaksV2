# Ghast

A large floating hostile mob with only 10 HP but a 4x4 block hitbox. Found in Nether wastes, soul sand valleys, and basalt deltas. Fires explosive fireballs that can be deflected back to one-shot the ghast. The only source of ghast tears.

## Stats

- Health: 10 HP
- Hitbox: 4x4 blocks (very large target)
- Speed: 0.7
- Attack (fireball direct hit): Easy 4 HP, Normal 6 HP, Hard 9 HP
- Attack (explosion): Easy max 9.5 HP, Normal max 17 HP, Hard max 25.5 HP (proximity-dependent)
- A deflected fireball deals 1000 HP to any ghast it directly hits

## Spawning

Requires a 5x5 wide and 4 tall free space with a solid block below. Spawns in any light level.

- Nether wastes: groups of 4
- Soul sand valleys: groups of 4 (only biome where ghasts cannot spawn if another ghast is within 16 blocks)
- Basalt deltas: spawn individually

Ghasts do not spawn inside Nether fortresses but can wander near them. 5% of spawn attempts succeed on Java Edition.

## Behavior

Ghasts wander aimlessly and shoot fireballs at players. Their fireball explosions set fire to nearby blocks and destroy blocks with blast resistance below 3.5 -- Nether brick and most stone survive, but netherrack does not.

**Fireball deflection**: Any melee attack or projectile that hits the fireball deflects it. A deflected fireball that directly hits a ghast deals 1000 HP instantly -- guaranteed one-shot regardless of health. This is the primary combat technique against ghasts.

**Important**: If a deflected fireball hits a different mob (not the ghast), that mob retaliates against the player who deflected it, not against the ghast.

## Drops

- Ghast tear (used for potions of Regeneration and end crystal crafting)
- Gunpowder

# Wither Skeleton

A tall (2.4 blocks), black melee undead mob exclusive to Nether fortresses. Attacks inflict Wither effect for 10 seconds -- drains 1 HP every 2 seconds and can kill. The only source of wither skeleton skulls (required to summon the Wither boss). Immune to fire and the Wither effect.

## Stats

- Health: 20 HP
- Attack (stone sword): Easy 5 HP, Normal 8 HP, Hard 12 HP + Wither for 10 seconds
- Speed: 0.25 idle, 0.3125 attacking
- Height: 2.4 blocks (cannot fit through 2-block tall openings)

## Wither Effect

Any hit from a wither skeleton applies Wither for 10 seconds: health bar turns black, -1 HP every 2 seconds = up to 5 HP drain. Unlike Poison, Wither can kill (takes health below 0.5 HP). There is no item that cures Wither in survival (only milk bucket).

## Spawning

Nether fortresses only. Groups of 5 (or 2-3 in larger fortress area). Light level 0-7.

On Normal/Hard, the Wither boss itself spawns 3-4 wither skeletons when it drops below half health.

The only mob that can spawn inside wither roses (immune to the damaging effect).

## Behavior

Wither skeletons sprint toward players, iron golems, baby turtles, piglins, and piglin brutes within 16 blocks. Villagers and wandering traders are ignored.

They flee from wolves. If a wolf attacks, the wither skeleton turns to fight back.

Immune to fire and lava. Do not burn in sunlight, but still seek shade or water if in the Overworld (remnant of skeleton behavior).

On Normal/Hard, wither skeletons can pick up dropped gear: swords higher than stone tier and any armor. They refuse bows. If given a bow via commands, they shoot flaming arrows (inherent fire, not enchantment-based) that deal normal arrow damage plus 4 HP fire damage.

Cannot fit through 2-block doorways due to 2.4-block height.

## Skull Drop

Skull drop rate: 2.5% base, +1% per Looting level (5.5% with Looting III). Skull also drops 100% when killed by a charged creeper. Three skulls are required to summon the Wither boss -- farming skulls is a time-intensive grind.

## Undead Properties

- Damaged by Instant Health, healed by Instant Damage
- Ignored by the Wither
- Smite enchantment effective
- Cannot swim but does not drown

## Drops

- Coal (33% base)
- Bone
- Wither skeleton skull (2.5% base, Looting increases, 100% from charged creeper)
- Stone sword (8.5% base)

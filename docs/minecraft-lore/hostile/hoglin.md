# Hoglin

A hostile Nether mob with 40 HP found in crimson forests and bastion remnants. Attacks by thrusting its tusks upward, launching targets into the air. 60% knockback resistance. The only hostile overworld-breeadable mob and the only breedable Nether mob. Converts to a zoglin after 15 seconds in the Overworld or End.

## Stats

- Health: 40 HP
- Adult attack: Easy 2.5-5 HP, Normal 3-8 HP, Hard 4.5-12 HP (variable per hit)
- Baby attack: Easy/Normal 0.5 HP, Hard 0.75 HP
- Knockback resistance: 60%
- Attack reach: 1.9 blocks

## Spawning

Crimson forests in herds of 3-4 at any light level, on most solid blocks except Nether wart blocks and shroomlights. 20% chance to spawn as baby (5% on Bedrock). Also spawn in hoglin stable bastions upon generation (persistent, never despawn).

## Repellents

Hoglins are repelled by and flee from:
- Warped fungi placed in the world
- Active Nether portals
- Respawn anchors

A hoglin near a repellent will not attack even if a player is in range -- unless it has already committed to an attack.

## Behavior

- Attacks any player within 16 blocks; shield does NOT prevent knockback
- 60% knockback resistance -- cannot be easily pushed back
- Attacking one hoglin provokes all nearby hoglins (attacking a baby does not trigger this)
- If piglins attack hoglins (hunt), the outnumbered side flees and makes retreat sounds
- Baby hoglins attack the player but deal negligible damage; flee temporarily if hit
- Baby piglins ride baby hoglins; up to 3 can stack on one

**Zombification**: Converts to a zoglin after 15 seconds in the Overworld or End. Zoglin gets Nausea I for 10 seconds (cosmetic only).

## Breeding

Breed with crimson fungi. 5-minute cooldown between breedings; babies mature in 20 minutes. Feeding crimson fungi to a hoglin (even without successful breeding) prevents it from despawning. Hoglins cannot breed while fleeing from repellents.

## Drops

- Raw porkchop (cooked if on fire)
- Leather

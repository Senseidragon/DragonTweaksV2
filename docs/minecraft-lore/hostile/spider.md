# Spider

A neutral arthropod with 16 HP that becomes hostile at light level 11 or below. Spiders are uniquely dangerous because they can climb any vertical surface, pass through 1-wide gaps at their height, and can spawn with persistent status effects on Hard difficulty.

## Stats

- Health: 16 HP
- Attack: Easy 2 HP, Normal 2 HP, Hard 3 HP
- Speed: 0.3
- Hitbox: 0.9 high x 1.4 wide (wider than tall -- fits through 1x2 gaps sideways)
- Immune to Poison and immune to cobweb slowdown

## Spawning

At light level 0, in a 3x1x3 space with no full block directly above the center. Not in mushroom fields or deep dark. In sulfur caves, cave spiders replace normal spiders.

Monster room spawners: 25% of rooms. Woodland mansion secret rooms (cobweb rooms).
Trial chamber spawners: 1/3 melee slot.

**Hard difficulty status effects**: Each spawn group has a (10 x regional difficulty)% chance to spawn with a permanent effect. All spiders in the pack share the same effect:
- Speed (40% chance)
- Strength (20% chance)
- Regeneration (20% chance)
- Invisibility (20% chance, but eyes remain visible)

**Spider jockey**: 1% chance that a spider spawns with a skeleton riding it. The skeleton controls all movement. The spider can still wall-climb (skeleton suffocates if they run into a ceiling). Contact with water separates them.

## Behavior

**Neutral at high light**: Spiders are neutral when the light level immediately around them is above 11. They become hostile below 12. If a hostile spider chases a target into a well-lit area (above 11), it returns to neutral. Exception: spiders always pursue in retaliation to an attack regardless of light level.

Spiders do NOT target villagers or wandering traders at any light level, despite their association with iron golems.

Spiders see targets at 16 blocks and pounce at close range.

**Wall climbing**: Spiders climb any solid vertical surface that is not a magma block. When pursuing and blocked by a wall, they climb it even if they lose aggression. After losing aggression, spiders continue moving forward blindly for 2 seconds -- this causes uninitiated climbs. They also climb boats and shulker boxes.

**Armadillo counter**: Spiders flee from unrolled armadillos. A rolled-up armadillo does not trigger flight.

## Baby Zombie Jockey

15% of nearby baby zombies, husks, or drowned can mount a spider that was not already a spider jockey. The baby zombie acts as rider and the spider provides the mobility.

## Drops

- String (primary)
- Spider eye (rare)

# Snow Golem

A fragile player-built passive mob that pelts nearby monsters with snowballs. Snow golems deal no damage to most mobs -- snowballs only provoke retaliation -- but they do deal 3 HP per hit to blazes, making them one of the few practical blaze counters in the Nether. They also leave a trail of snow as they walk.

## Spawning

Built by stacking two snow blocks vertically and placing a carved pumpkin or jack o'lantern on top last. The pumpkin must be placed last regardless of orientation -- the build works upright, sideways, or upside-down. Can be built by a player, dispenser, or enderman. Cannot be built by pistons.

## Behavior

Snow golems wander aimlessly and throw one snowball per second at any monster within 10 blocks -- including creepers. The range extends to 16 blocks (Euclidean) when the golem is enclosed or caged. They do not target players or other golems.

The snowballs deal zero damage to nearly everything. Mobs that are hit will retaliate and attack the golem, which has only 4 HP and dies almost immediately. The golem's practical role is not combat -- it is aggro-pulling and distraction.

Blazes are the exception: each snowball deals 3 HP to a blaze, making a snow golem a legitimate blaze-killing tool given enough snowballs and sufficient golem survival.

Endermen teleport away when struck by a snowball rather than retaliating -- a snow golem can be used to scatter or displace endermen without provoking a fight.

Breezes deflect snowballs entirely and are unaffected.

## Environmental Vulnerabilities

Snow golems take 1 HP per tick from rain, water contact, and any biome with a temperature above 1.0 -- deserts, savannas, badlands, and all Nether biomes. This kills them within seconds. The Fire Resistance effect allows a snow golem to survive in hot biomes without taking heat damage.

In cold and temperate biomes the golem is safe. It is immune to fall damage and powder snow.

## Snow Trail

As the golem moves, it deposits a 1-layer snow cover on walkable surfaces beneath it. This is disabled if the `mobGriefing` game rule is false. The trail is a renewable source of snow in cold biomes and a useful indicator of golem pathfinding.

## Drops

- Death: 0-15 snowballs (Looting does not increase this)
- Sheared: 1 carved pumpkin (removes the pumpkin from its head; the face underneath is revealed)

## Notes

- An iron golem accidentally hit by a snow golem's snowball will retaliate and kill it in one swing.
- Snow golems are the only renewable source of snowballs without a snow biome.
- In the Nether, a snow golem will die almost instantly from the biome heat unless given Fire Resistance -- but with Fire Resistance it can freely farm blaze kills.

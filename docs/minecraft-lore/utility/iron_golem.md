# Iron Golem

A powerful village defender with 100 HP and devastating melee damage. Iron golems are either summoned automatically by villagers under threat or built by players. The two origins behave differently in important ways.

## Spawning

Villagers summon iron golems when gossiping (requires 5 participating villagers) or when panicking (requires only 3). Each participating villager must have slept within the last 20 minutes and must not have detected a golem within 16 blocks in the last 30 seconds. Iron golems ignore the `doMobSpawning` game rule and spawn regardless of its setting.

Players can build an iron golem by placing four iron blocks in a T-shape and placing a carved pumpkin or jack o'lantern on the center top block last. The pumpkin must be placed last. Iron golems can also be found caged in dark oak cages at pillager outposts -- freeing them turns them into allies against the pillagers.

## Behavior

Iron golems patrol and attack hostile mobs on sight, swinging their arms to fling targets into the air. On Normal difficulty the swing deals 7.5-21.5 HP; on Hard, 11.25-32.25 HP. They have 100% knockback resistance against normal attacks, though wind charges and the Wind Burst enchantment can still knock them back. They target one mob at a time until it kills it before switching.

Iron golems cannot attack anything more than 2.75 blocks above them -- standing on a three-block pillar puts a player completely out of reach of a golem below.

Naturally-spawned golems can detect raiders through solid walls and underground. Player-built golems cannot.

## Critical Gaps

Iron golems do **not** attack creepers. Creepers can still damage and kill a golem with their explosion, but the golem will not pre-target or pursue them. Goats also do not provoke golems -- goats will randomly charge a golem, but the golem ignores them unless hit.

More importantly: iron golems are **not provoked when a mob attacks a nearby villager**. They only retaliate when directly attacked themselves. A zombie killing villagers a few blocks away will not draw golem aggro unless the golem was already hostile to zombies by proximity or the zombie hits the golem.

## Player Relations

A player-built golem never attacks players under any circumstances, even if the player strikes it. It will, however, attack the player's tamed wolf if the wolf attacks the golem first.

A naturally-spawned village golem turns hostile toward players whose village popularity drops to -15 or lower, or whose reputation with any nearby villager reaches -100 or lower. The golem remains hostile until both conditions recover.

## Healing and Damage Indicators

Crack patterns on the golem's body indicate health: no cracks above 75 HP, light cracks 50-75 HP, heavier cracks 25-50 HP, severe cracks below 25 HP. Using an iron ingot on the golem heals one crack stage (+25 HP) and consumes the ingot.

## Drops

- Poppies
- Iron ingots
- No XP

## Notes

- Caged golems at pillager outposts can attack pillagers through the 1-block cage walls via air-block gaps.
- A snow golem accidentally hitting an iron golem with a snowball will provoke the iron golem to retaliate.
- Iron golems can climb ladders and vines if the path requires it.

# Salmon

A passive aquatic mob found in rivers and cold/frozen oceans. Common food source and bucketable.

## Spawning

Spawns in groups of 1-5 in cold and frozen oceans and their deep variants, and in rivers and frozen rivers. Three sizes: small (31.6%), normal (52.6%), large (15.8%) -- size affects hitbox only, not behavior or drops. Swim in schools of up to 7; may occasionally leave their school and despawn.

## Stats

- 3 HP (1.5 hearts)
- Passive
- Vulnerable to Impaling enchantment

## Behavior

Fully passive. Can swim up waterfalls approximately 4-5 blocks (mimics real-world salmon behavior). Bucketable -- salmon placed from a bucket do not despawn naturally. Cannot be bred.

Cannot survive out of water -- flop and die from suffocation. Cannot survive in cauldron water.

**Notable threat:** Axolotls always attack salmon on sight.

## Cross-Mob Interactions

Axolotls hunt salmon on sight.

Feeding **raw salmon** (or raw cod) to a dolphin causes it to swim toward and guide the player to the nearest shipwreck or ocean ruins. Baby dolphin growth is accelerated by feeding fish -- each feeding reduces remaining growth time by 10%.

Ocelots approach players holding raw salmon within 10 blocks. Each raw salmon fed has a 1/3 chance of gaining the ocelot's trust. Trusting ocelots can be bred with raw salmon.

Cats are tamed and bred with raw cod or raw salmon (see Cat entry).

## Drops

- 1 raw salmon (cooked salmon if killed by fire)
- 5% chance of 1 bone meal
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf

# Horse

A passive rideable mob with variable stats. Unlike donkeys and mules, horses can wear armor and have stats that vary per individual, making selective breeding meaningful.

## Spawning

Spawns in plains and sunflower plains (~11%, groups of 2-6) and savanna biomes (~1.5-2%, groups of 2-6). Also generates in village stables and animal pens at world generation.

## Stats (per individual, set at spawn)

- **Health:** 15-30 HP (average 22.5)
- **Speed:** 4.86-14.57 blocks/sec (average ~9.71 b/s). ~82% of horses are faster than a minecart.
- **Jump strength:** 0.4-1.0, clearing 1.15-5.92 blocks (average ~3.1 blocks)
- Stats are inherited via parent-averaging on breeding and are **not** affected by food.

## Taming

Mount repeatedly with empty hand. Temper starts at 0/100; each ride attempt picks a random 0-99 value: if less than current temper - tamed; otherwise temper +5 and player is bucked off. Feeding also raises temper. An untamed horse's walking speed while unridden gives a rough read on its Speed stat before taming.

## Equipment

- **Saddle:** required for rider control. Removable with shears.
- **Horse armor:** leather, iron, gold, or diamond (3-11 armor points). Removable with shears -- if both saddle and armor are equipped, shears removes armor first, then saddle on second use.
- Foals cannot be equipped or ridden.
- Rider minimum clearance: 2.75 blocks (rider suffocates below this). Cannot fit through 1-block-wide gaps.
- Can be led alongside a boat with a lead.

## Healing

Hay bale restores 20 HP. Golden apple restores 10 HP. Wheat restores 2 HP.

## Breeding

Breed two tamed horses with golden apple or golden carrot. Offspring stats are averaged from both parents with variance. Cross-breed with a donkey to produce a mule (mules cannot breed). Foal inherits color/markings from parents (~80%) or gets a random coat (~20% markings, ~11% color chance).

35 coat combinations (7 base colors x 5 marking patterns) -- all cosmetic, no effect on stats.

## Drops

- Leather
- Saddle and armor drop if equipped
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf
- 1-7 XP on successful breeding

# Camel

A large passive mob found in deserts and desert villages. Camels are naturally tame rideable mounts that carry two players simultaneously and provide significant protection from ground-level melee mobs due to their height.

## Spawning

One camel spawns at the center of every desert village during world generation -- a reliable, guaranteed source. They also spawn rarely in desert biomes.

## Stats

- 32 HP (16 hearts)
- Passive; naturally tame (no taming required)

## Behavior

Camels wander aimlessly and periodically sit for 2-7 seconds before standing again. A sitting camel's hitbox drops to only 0.945 blocks tall -- nearly flat. When a player approaches holding cactus, a sitting camel immediately stands and follows.

Camels can step up 1.5 blocks automatically, allowing them to walk over fences and walls without jumping -- unlike horses, donkeys, and mules.

Safe fall distance is 6 blocks (double the standard 3). Fall damage taken is half normal (rounded up). Riders take the same fall damage as the camel.

Camels heal 2 HP when fed cactus. They also have a slow passive regen (1 HP per ~15 seconds on average). **Touching a cactus block still damages them** -- the food item heals but the block does not.

## Riding

Camels are naturally tame and need only a saddle to ride. Up to two players can ride at once -- the rear passenger cannot damage the driver or the camel. If the front rider dismounts, the rear rider takes control.

**Speed:** Walking up to 3.9 blocks/s; sprinting up to 8.2 blocks/s (double-tap forward or hold sprint).

**Dash:** The jump bar becomes a dash charge bar. Holding jump charges a longer dash. A full dash launches 12 blocks forward and 1 block upward. Cooldown: 2.75 seconds. Camels cannot sprint immediately after dashing.

**Height advantage:** Most ground-level melee mobs cannot reach riders -- zombies, vindicators, hoglins, silverfish, endermites, and gold-sword piglins are all blocked. Spiders, wolves, slimes, and magma cubes can still reach riders (they jump or climb). Almost no mob deliberately targets a camel to reach its rider -- the exceptions are zoglins, wardens, withers, and vindicators named "Johnny."

Baby camels cannot be saddled or ridden.

## Breeding

Bred with cactus. Follow players holding cactus within 10 blocks. After breeding, 1-7 XP drops; 5-minute cooldown. Baby matures in 20 minutes (each cactus feeding cuts ~10 seconds from growth time).

## Notes

- Every desert village spawns exactly one camel -- a reliable early-game mount source.
- The dash can cross a 12-block gap or clear a 1-block-high obstacle mid-flight.
- Sitting camels have an extremely low hitbox (0.945 blocks) -- useful to know when building enclosures.
- Saddle can be removed from a living camel with shears.

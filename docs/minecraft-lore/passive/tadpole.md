# Tadpole

A bucketable aquatic mob that hatches from frogspawn in groups of 2-5. It grows into one of three frog variants based on the biome it matures in -- not based on its parents' variant. Unlike every other baby mob in the game, the tadpole is treated as a completely separate mob from the frog, not merely a younger version of it.

## Stats

- **Health:** 6 HP
- **Size:** 0.4 x 0.3 blocks (very small)

## Spawning

Hatches from frogspawn (laid by pregnant frogs near water). Groups of 2-5 per frogspawn block. There is no natural frog spawning -- all frogs in the world come from tadpoles maturing.

## Behavior

Tadpoles swim aimlessly in water. On land they flop and seek the nearest water source; they die in under 20 seconds without water.

They follow players holding a slimeball. Feeding slimeballs accelerates growth by 10% per feeding. A golden dandelion freezes growth; a second restarts it.

**Important:** Unlike adult frogs, tadpoles are actively hunted by axolotls.

They can be bucketed, preserving health, age, and name.

## Growth

Takes 20 minutes (1 Minecraft day) to mature. The biome at the time of maturation determines the frog variant:

- **Temperate frog** -- most overworld biomes: forests, plains, swamp, ocean, caves, and similar
- **Cold frog** -- frozen and snowy biomes, The End, Deep Dark
- **Warm frog** -- jungle, desert, savanna, Mangrove Swamp, Warm Ocean, and all Nether biomes

To reliably produce a specific frog variant, bucket the tadpole and carry it to a biome of the correct temperature before it matures. If golden dandelion aging-pause is used, the tadpole can be held indefinitely and released at a chosen location.

## Drops

None.

# Rabbit

A passive mob found in snowy, desert, taiga, and meadow biomes. Fragile but notably can jump over fences, making containment non-trivial.

## Spawning

Spawns in groups of 2-3 above grass, snow blocks, or sand. High-density biomes: desert (92%), snowy plains/ice spikes (91%), grove (62%). Wolves and foxes in taiga and mountain biomes actively hunt rabbits, reducing their population there.

## Stats

- 3 HP (1.5 hearts)
- Passive

## Behavior

Rabbits flee from players not holding their food items. They can **jump over fences and fence gates** (hitbox is 0.5 blocks tall). Baby rabbits follow adults.

## Variants

Six natural coat types (brown, white, black, white splotched, gold, salt) -- biome-dependent, cosmetic only. Naming a rabbit "Toast" with a name tag gives it a unique black-and-white skin (cosmetic only; Toast offspring revert to normal). The Killer Bunny is a Java Edition command-only hostile variant with 8 armor points that attacks players and wolves.

## Drops

- 0-1 rabbit hide
- 1 raw rabbit (cooked if killed by fire)
- 0-1 rabbit's foot (rare; used for Leaping potions)
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf
- 1-7 XP on successful breeding

## Breeding

Breed with carrots, golden carrots, or dandelions. Baby rabbits take 20 minutes to mature; each feeding reduces growth by 10%. Offspring variant: 47.5% chance of each parent's coat, 5% chance of the biome-default coat.

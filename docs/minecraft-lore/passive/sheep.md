# Sheep

A passive mob found in most grassy Overworld biomes. Primary renewable source of wool and a secondary food source. Wool color varies by biome; sheep can be dyed.

## Spawning

Spawn in groups of 4 on grass blocks (light level 9+) in most biomes except pale gardens. May spawn in village shepherd houses, butcher houses, and animal pens. Color distribution by biome:
- **Temperate:** White (81.8%), Light Gray/Gray/Black (15% split), Brown (3%), Pink (0.164%)
- **Cold:** Black (81.8%), White/Light Gray/Gray (15% split), Brown (3%), Pink (0.164%)
- **Warm:** Brown (81.8%), White/Light Gray/Gray (15% split), Black (3%), Pink (0.164%)

## Stats

- 8 HP (4 hearts)
- Passive

## Behavior

Sheep wander in small flocks of 2-4. They avoid cliffs and hazardous areas. They flee briefly when attacked but make **no special attempt to avoid wolves**.

**Grazing:** Sheep eat short grass, short dry grass, tall dry grass, and ferns -- converting grass blocks to dirt. They can eat grass through blocks shorter than a full block (honey blocks, slabs, etc.) and even from inside minecarts. Sheared sheep regrow wool after grazing. If no grass is available, wool does not regrow. Baby sheep graze ~20x more often than adults and grow 1 minute faster from grazing. With `mobGriefing=false`, grass blocks are not consumed but wool still regrows.

Sheep follow players holding wheat within 6 blocks. Adult sheep cannot fit through the 90- corner of two fences when the corner piece is removed.

**Evoker interaction:** An evoker not in combat within 16 blocks of a **blue sheep** changes its wool to red with a "wololo" sound effect (Age of Empires reference). Works even on jeb\_ sheep.

## Shearing

Yields 1-3 wool per sheep; sheep take no damage. Not affected by Fortune or Looting.

## Dyeing

Right-click with any dye to change wool color. Color is retained through shearing and regrowth. A sheared sheep cannot be dyed until wool regrows. Breeding two sheep with compatible dye colors produces a baby with the mixed color (e.g., blue + white = light blue).

**jeb\_:** Naming a sheep "jeb\_" with a name tag makes its wool cycle through all colors visually. The dropped wool matches whatever color it was when sheared -- the rainbow effect is cosmetic only.

## Drops

- 1-2 raw mutton (cooked mutton if killed by fire)
- 1 wool if not sheared before death
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf
- 1-7 XP on successful breeding

## Breeding

Breed with wheat. 5-minute cooldown. Baby takes 20 minutes to mature; each wheat feeding cuts 10% off remaining time. Baby sheep can also self-accelerate growth by grazing.

# Glow Squid

A passive aquatic mob found in dark underground water. The only source of glow ink sacs, used to make glowing text on signs and glowing item frames.

## Spawning

Spawns in schools of 4-6 in complete darkness (light level 0) in water below layer 30 in the Overworld. Spawns in nearly all biomes with underground water -- does **not** spawn in the deep dark or sulfur cave biomes. Unlike most aquatic mobs, does not require specific block conditions above or below the spawn space. 5% chance to spawn as a baby.

## Stats

- 10 HP (5 hearts)
- Passive
- 85% knockback resistance

## Behavior

Fully passive, never attacks. Swims aimlessly. When hit, releases dark ink particles that briefly darken the surrounding water visually -- this is a cosmetic effect only, it does not apply status effects or blind the player.

Their luminescent texture makes them visible in dark underwater environments, but they do **not** emit actual light to surrounding blocks.

Cannot breed.

## Cross-Mob Interactions

Axolotls hunt glow squid on sight.

## Drops

- 1-3 glow ink sac
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf

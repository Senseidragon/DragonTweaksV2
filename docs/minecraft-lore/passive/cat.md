# Cat

A passive mob found in villages and swamp huts. Tamed cats repel creepers and phantoms and bring the player gifts after sleeping. They are one of the most practically useful companions in the game despite having no combat capability.

## Spawning

Cats spawn in villages once per minute (1200 ticks) -- one at a time, 8-32 blocks from a random player, provided there are at least 5 claimed beds within 48 blocks and fewer than 4 cats in a 97x17x97 area. Village cat spawning is independent of the passive mob cap. Black cats spawn only in swamp huts, and during full moons 50% of village cats will also be black. Cats spawned at village creation do not despawn; additional village cats may despawn.

## Stats

- 10 HP (5 hearts)
- Passive; attacks only rabbits and baby turtles (3 HP)

## Behavior

### Stray Cats

Untamed cats flee from players within 7 blocks and avoid players within 16. They slowly approach a player holding raw cod or salmon within 10 blocks -- if the player moves too fast or stops holding fish within 6 blocks, the cat flees and won't approach again briefly. Stray cats hunt rabbits and baby turtles within 15 blocks, stalking to within 4 blocks before chasing.

### Creeper and Phantom Deterrence

Creepers keep at least **6 blocks** distance from any cat (tamed or wild). A creeper that has already begun its detonation countdown does not flee.

Phantoms keep at least **16 blocks** distance from any cat. Cats hiss at phantoms pursuing a player.

### Tamed Cat Behavior

Tamed cats follow their owner and teleport to them when more than 12 blocks away. **Teleportation is completely silent.** Cats do not teleport when: ordered to sit, lying on a bed, in a minecart or boat, leashed to a fence, in an unloaded chunk, or when the player is in another dimension.

Tamed cats autonomously sit on top of chests (the foot of beds, or active furnaces) within a 4-block radius at the same Y-level. **A chest with a cat sitting on it cannot be opened.** The cat can be moved by ordering it to sit and then stand, holding a raw fish nearby, breaking the block, or attacking it.

Cats are immune to fall damage but still avoid falling off cliffs.

Cats can see players with the Invisibility effect.

If a sitting cat is struck by lightning, it will teleport to the owner **on fire** -- this almost always kills the cat.

### Morning Gifts

When the player sleeps at night, tamed cats approach the bed and sleep alongside the player. On waking, there is a **70% chance** the cat brings a gift. Gifts are not given if: the player slept during a thunderstorm in the daytime, or the cat was commanded to sit. Gift loot table includes: string, rabbit's foot, rabbit hide, rotten flesh, feather, raw chicken, and **phantom membrane** (3.22% chance).

## Taming

Feed raw cod or raw salmon to a stray cat. Each fish has a **1/3 chance** of taming. Once tamed, the cat no longer despawns or fears the player.

## Drops

- 0-2 string (adult)
- 1-3 XP when killed by player or tamed wolf
- Nothing from baby cats

## Breeding

Tamed cats breed with raw cod or salmon (both must enter love mode). Baby inherits color from one parent and collar color from a mix of both parents' collars. 5-minute breeding cooldown. Baby matures in 20 minutes (each fish feeding cuts 10%).

## Notes

- Keep a tamed cat near your base to passively deter creepers within 6 blocks and phantoms within 16 blocks.
- A cat sitting on a chest blocks all chest access -- useful to know if a cat goes missing near your storage.
- The morning gift phantom membrane is the only renewable source of membranes without hunting phantoms directly.
- 11 visual variants; only black cats spawn in swamp huts. No behavioral differences between variants.

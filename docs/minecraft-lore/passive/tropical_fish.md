# Tropical Fish

**Tropical fish** are passive aquatic mobs found in warm/lukewarm oceans, mangrove swamps, and lush caves. They have over 2,700 naturally occurring visual variants but no behavioral differences between them.

## Spawning

Tropical fish spawn in groups of 8 at Y-level 50-63 in warm oceans, lukewarm oceans, deep lukewarm oceans, and mangrove swamps, and in lush caves at any Y-level. 90% of spawns use one of 22 named common varieties; 10% have fully random patterns, colors, and shapes.

## Behavior

Fully passive. Tropical fish cannot survive out of water. They are vulnerable to the Impaling enchantment. They can be bucketed.

## Drops

- Bone Meal, Bone

## Cross-Mob Interactions

Axolotls hunt tropical fish on sight. Any body of water containing both axolotls and tropical fish will see the fish killed -- they are explicitly listed as axolotl prey.

Axolotls are bred and lured using **buckets of tropical fish** (not raw tropical fish items). A player holding a bucket of tropical fish causes nearby axolotls to follow within 10 blocks. Each bucket fed to a baby axolotl reduces its remaining growth time by 10%.

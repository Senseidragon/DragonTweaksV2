# Squid

A common passive aquatic mob found in rivers and oceans. Primary source of ink sacs. Has unusually high knockback resistance (85%) for a passive mob.

## Spawning

Spawns in groups of 2-4 in water at Y-level 50-63 in ocean and river biomes. Block above and below spawn space must contain water. Shares the water creature mob cap with dolphins and nautiluses. 5% chance to spawn as a baby. Cannot breed.

## Stats

- 10 HP (5 hearts)
- Passive
- 85% knockback resistance

## Behavior

Fully passive, swims aimlessly. When attacked, squirts ink that creates a brief darkening area effect in surrounding water. Cannot survive out of water.

## Cross-Mob Interactions

Axolotls hunt squid on sight.

## Drops

- 1-3 ink sac
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf

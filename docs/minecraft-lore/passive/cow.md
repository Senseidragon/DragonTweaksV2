# Cow

A passive mob found in most grassy biomes. Primary source of leather, beef, and milk. Three cosmetic variants (temperate, cold, warm) with no behavioral differences. Shearing a mooshroom converts it into a cow.

## Spawning

Spawns in herds of 4 on grass blocks with at least two blocks of space above, in most Overworld grassy biomes. Does not spawn in snowy plains, meadows, cherry groves, mangrove swamps, or pale gardens. Cows may also spawn pre-placed inside village animal pens, stables, and butcher house backyards at world generation.

## Stats

- 10 HP (5 hearts)
- Passive

## Behavior

Cows wander aimlessly. They actively avoid water (unless following a player), avoid rails, and avoid falls that would cause damage. They flee briefly when hurt. Cows follow players holding wheat within 6 blocks.

An adult cow can be milked with a bucket at any time -- infinite, no cooldown.

## Drops

- 1-3 raw beef (steak if killed by fire)
- 0-2 leather
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf

## Breeding

Breed with wheat. Both parents enter a 5-minute cooldown. Baby cows take 20 minutes to mature; each wheat feeding cuts 10% off the remaining time. Offspring inherit the variant of one parent regardless of biome.

Baby cows prefer to follow adult cows over players holding wheat. Feeding a baby cow a golden dandelion freezes its growth and resets the timer to 20 minutes; feeding it a second golden dandelion resumes growth.

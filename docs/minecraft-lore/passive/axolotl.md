# Axolotl

A passive aquatic mob found only in lush caves. Axolotls actively hunt most hostile and passive aquatic mobs and reward players who fight alongside them with Regeneration and the removal of Mining Fatigue. They can be carried in buckets and permanently tamed by releasing them from a bucket.

## Spawning

Lush caves only. Requires a clay block within 5 blocks below the spawn point. Groups of 4-6. Blue axolotls cannot spawn naturally -- they only appear as rare offspring from breeding (1 in 1200 chance).

## Stats

- 14 HP (7 hearts)
- Passive toward players; aggressive toward aquatic mobs

## Behavior

Axolotls hunt the following mobs on sight: cod, drowned, elder guardian, glow squid, guardian, pufferfish, salmon, squid, tadpole, tropical fish. They deal 2 HP per attack and prioritize hostile mobs over passive ones. After killing a passive aquatic mob, they have a 2-minute cooldown before hunting another passive mob.

When a player kills a mob that an axolotl is fighting, the axolotl grants the player **Regeneration I** for 5 seconds (stacks per axolotl in the fight, up to 2 minutes) and removes any **Mining Fatigue** effect.

Axolotls also cause nearby pufferfish to inflate.

### Play Dead

When damaged underwater, an axolotl may play dead -- lying still for 10 seconds while regenerating 4 HP. Hostile aquatic mobs ignore a playing-dead axolotl. This triggers: a 1/3 base chance, increased if incoming damage is significant or the axolotl is below 50% health.

### Out of Water

Axolotls can leave water and wander up to 8-9 blocks from the water's edge. They require at least 2-block-deep water within 16 blocks to find their way back. If no such water is within range, they wander randomly and die after 5 minutes on land. They do not die on land in rain or thunderstorms (unlike fish or squid), but they **can** die out of water as passengers in a boat.

Axolotls cannot be pushed by flowing water but are still moved by bubble columns.

A splash or lingering water bottle restores 90 seconds of air supply to an axolotl.

## Drops

- No item drops
- 1 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf

## Breeding

Bred with buckets of tropical fish. Follow players holding tropical fish buckets within 10 blocks. After breeding, 1-7 XP drops; parents have a 5-minute cooldown. Baby has a 1/1200 chance to be the rare blue variant; otherwise inherits one parent's color. Baby matures in 20 minutes (each tropical fish bucket speeds growth by 10%).

An axolotl loses breeding willingness if it is bucketed and released after being fed but before reaching its mate -- must be fed again.

## Notes

- Axolotls picked up in a water bucket and re-released do not despawn -- this is how to permanently keep one.
- Naming a bucket of axolotl in an anvil names the axolotl inside; the name persists if transferred to another bucket.
- Blue axolotls are purely cosmetic -- no behavioral difference from other colors.
- The Impaling enchantment deals extra damage to axolotls (they are aquatic mobs).

# Sniffer

A large ancient passive mob that hatches from sniffer eggs. Only source of torchflower seeds and pitcher pods. One of the largest passive mobs (1.9 blocks wide, 1.75 tall).

## Spawning

Does not spawn naturally. Hatches from sniffer eggs found in suspicious sand from warm ocean ruins. Breeding two sniffers with torchflower seeds produces a new sniffer egg item dropped on the ground.

## Stats

- 14 HP (7 hearts)
- Passive
- Speed: 0.1 (very slow)

## Behavior

Sniffers wander aimlessly and occasionally smell the ground, tracking ancient seeds. When they locate a seed location, they sploot and dig with their snout, producing either a torchflower seed or pitcher pod (equal chance).

**Digging constraints:**
- Can only dig on solid ground; cannot dig in water, when leashed, when tempted with a torchflower seed, while panicking, or in a boat/minecart.
- Dig target is 2.25 blocks in front of the sniffer's center.
- Diggable blocks: coarse dirt, dirt, grass block, moss block, mud, muddy mangrove roots, pale moss block, rooted dirt, podzol. **Cannot dig mycelium.**
- Remembers its last 20 dig locations and will not re-dig them.
- **8-minute cooldown** after each dig.

## Snifflets (Babies)

- Take **40 minutes** to mature (twice the normal 20 minutes for most babies).
- Do **not** follow adult sniffers.
- Cannot dig.
- Growth accelerated 10% per torchflower seed feeding.

## Breeding

Feed torchflower seeds to two sniffers to activate love mode. Produces a sniffer egg item on the ground.

## Drops

- No drops on death
- 1-7 XP on successful breeding

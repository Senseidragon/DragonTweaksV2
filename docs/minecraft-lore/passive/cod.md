# Cod

A passive aquatic mob found in ocean biomes. Common source of raw cod and a reliable way to fill a bucket for portable fish transport.

## Spawning

Spawns in groups of 3-6 underwater in normal, cold, and lukewarm oceans (and their deep variants). Does not spawn in warm oceans or frozen oceans.

## Stats

- 3 HP (1.5 hearts)
- Passive

## Behavior

Cod swim passively and do not flee from players. They form schools of up to 9 fish.

A cod can be collected with a water bucket -- the resulting bucket of cod does not despawn naturally. Placing the bucket releases the cod into the water at that location. Cod cannot be bred.

## Weaknesses

Cod die outside water: they flop for 10 seconds, then take suffocation damage. They cannot survive in cauldron water or in waterlogged blocks (waterlogged slabs, stairs, etc. do not count as full water for fish survival). Cod are vulnerable to the Impaling enchantment.

## Cross-Mob Interactions

Axolotls hunt cod on sight.

Feeding **raw cod** (or raw salmon) to a dolphin causes it to swim toward and guide the player to the nearest shipwreck or ocean ruins. This is the primary practical use of raw cod beyond food. Dolphins cannot be bred, but baby dolphin growth is accelerated by feeding them fish -- each feeding reduces remaining growth time by 10%.

Ocelots approach players holding raw cod within 10 blocks. Each piece of raw cod fed to a wild ocelot has a 1/3 chance of gaining its trust, causing it to stop fleeing. Trusting ocelots can be bred with raw cod.

## Drops

- 1 raw cod (cooked if killed by fire)
- 5% chance of 1 bone meal
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf

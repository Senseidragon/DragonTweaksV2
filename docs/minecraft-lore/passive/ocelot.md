# Ocelot

A passive mob exclusive to jungle biomes. Cannot be tamed but can be "trusted" through feeding. Provides passive deterrence to creepers and phantoms.

## Spawning

Spawns above grass at sea level in jungle, bamboo jungle, and sparse jungle biomes. Groups of 1-3 adults (95%) or babies (5%). Uses the hostile mob spawn cap on Java Edition -- does not spawn on Peaceful difficulty. Trusting ocelots do not despawn.

## Stats

- 10 HP (5 hearts)
- Passive to players; attacks chickens and baby turtles
- Immune to fall damage

## Behavior

Ocelots sprint away from players in Survival/Adventure mode. They can be approached by holding raw cod or raw salmon -- ocelot approaches within 10 blocks. Each feeding has a 1/3 chance of gaining trust (hearts appear). Do not move or turn quickly when within 6 blocks or the ocelot flees and resets.

Once trusted, the ocelot no longer flees. Trusted ocelots do not despawn.

**Mob deterrence:**
- **Creepers** stay 6 blocks away. A creeper already in detonation does not flee unless the player also leaves its blast radius.
- **Phantoms** stay 16 blocks away.

Ocelots attack chickens and baby turtles within 15 blocks, stalking within 4 blocks before chasing. They can attack through fences, fence gates, and doors.

## Breeding

Trusting ocelots breed with raw cod or salmon. After initiating love mode, the player must switch to a different item -- holding fish causes them to keep looking at the player instead of each other.

## Drops

- No drops on death
- 1-7 XP on successful breeding

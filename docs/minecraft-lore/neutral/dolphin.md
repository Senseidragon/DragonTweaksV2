# Dolphin

Dolphins are neutral aquatic mobs that live in non-frozen ocean biomes. They are known for granting a swimming speed boost to nearby players.

## Spawning

Dolphins spawn in groups of 1-5 in all ocean biomes except frozen and cold oceans, exclusively between Y levels 50 and 64. They continuously respawn as long as conditions are met and despawn if no players are nearby. 10% of dolphins spawn as babies (babies are passive).

Spawn biomes: Lukewarm Ocean, Deep Lukewarm Ocean, Ocean, Deep Ocean, Warm Ocean.

## Behavior

Dolphins follow and swim with players who are swimming nearby, granting the **Dolphin's Grace** status effect -- a significant swimming speed increase. They are playful and will interact with items floating in water, bouncing them into the air.

**Weaknesses:**
- Out of water in dry weather: takes suffocation damage after 2 minutes, dies shortly after. Rain prevents this damage.
- Submerged too long (~4 minutes): begins drowning. Water Breathing prevents this.
- Cannot ride boats.

Dolphins actively seek water when on land (unless pursuing a target in a hostile state).

## Combat

Dolphins are neutral -- they attack only if provoked. Attack strength: 2.5HP (Easy), 3HP (Normal), 4.5HP (Hard). Baby dolphins are passive and will not attack.

## Drops

- Raw Cod (may cook to Cooked Cod if killed by fire)

## Breeding

Dolphins cannot be bred by the player. Baby dolphins only appear through natural spawning in the wild.

## Notes

- Dolphins cannot survive in frozen or cold oceans.
- Towing with a lead is possible.
- The Impaling enchantment deals extra damage to dolphins (aquatic mob).

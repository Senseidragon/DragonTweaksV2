# Wolf

Wolves are neutral mobs that can be tamed with bones. Tamed wolves defend their owner, assist in combat, and can wear wolf armor. There are 9 color variants, each spawning in a different biome.

## Spawning

Wild wolves spawn in packs on grass, coarse dirt, podzol, or snow. Nine color variants each spawn in a specific biome:

| Variant | Biome | Pack size |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Pale | Taiga | 4 |
| Ashen | Snowy Taiga | 4 |
| Black | Old Growth Pine Taiga | 2-4 |
| Chestnut | Old Growth Spruce Taiga | 2-4 |
| Rusty | Sparse Jungle | 2-4 |
| Snowy | Grove | 1 |
| Spotted | Savanna Plateau | 4-8 |
| Striped | Wooded Badlands | 4-8 |
| Woods | Forest | 4 |

Wolves also have 7 sound variants (independent of color variant) with no behavioral effect.

## States

**Untamed (wild):** Neutral to players. Hostile toward sheep, rabbits, foxes, baby turtles, skeletons, wither skeletons, strays, and bogged. Attack these mobs on sight. Do not despawn.

**Angry:** Becomes hostile when attacked (not killed in one hit) or when they see a prey mob. Eyes go red, tail sticks out. All wolves within 33x21x33 blocks become hostile to the attacker. Angry wolves can track attackers even under Invisibility.

**Tamed:** Red collar (dyeable). 40HP (vs 8HP wild). Sits/follows on command. Attacks what the owner attacks, and defends owner against attackers. Does not attack creepers or ghasts. Teleports to owner if more than 12 blocks away (unless sitting, angry, in a vehicle, or on a lead). Kills by tamed wolves grant the owner XP and loot credit.

## Taming

Feed bones -- each bone has a 1/3 chance of taming. No limit to number of tamed wolves. Cannot tame a hostile or already-tamed wolf.

## Feeding / Healing

Feed tamed wolves any meat (raw or cooked) or fish to restore health. Key values: Rotten Flesh = 8HP, Cooked Salmon = 10HP, Cooked Chicken/Mutton = 12HP, Cooked Porkchop/Steak = 16HP, Rabbit Stew = 20HP. Wolves do not get Hunger from rotten flesh or raw chicken.

## Wolf Armor

Equip using wolf armor item on a tamed wolf. Absorbs all damage until durability runs out except: drowning, freezing, suffocating, magic, thorns, Wither effect, void, and world border damage. Wolf does not produce a hurt sound when armor absorbs damage. Wolves wearing armor always drop their armor on death. Baby and untamed wolves cannot wear armor.

## Combat

- Health: Wild 8HP / Tamed 40HP
- Attack: 3HP (Easy), 4HP (Normal), 6HP (Hard) -- always 4HP vs non-player mobs

## Drops

- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf (adult)
- 1-7 XP upon successful breeding
- Wolf armor (if wearing it)

## Breeding

Tamed wolves breed with any meat item. Baby wolf color has a 50% chance of matching either parent's variant. Offspring collar color is a blend of both parents' collar colors (or randomly picks one parent's if they can't be mixed). Baby wolves behave like tamed adults and are owned by the same owner as the parents; if parents have different owners, ownership is randomly assigned.

## Notes

- Wolves vs Llamas: wolves attack or flee (within 24 blocks) from llamas; a llama that spits on a wolf causes that wolf to always attack the llama.
- Tamed wolves teleport silently to owner; cannot teleport across dimensions.
- Skeletons (and variants), foxes, and baby turtles actively flee from both tamed and wild wolves.

# Polar Bear

Polar bears are large neutral mobs found in frozen and icy biomes. Adults are protective of cubs -- they attack players who come too close to a baby or who attack any polar bear nearby. Their swimming speed matches the player's, making water a dangerous place to fight them.

## Spawning

Polar bears spawn above ice in groups of up to 2. If a group of 2 spawns, the second is always a baby. Baby polar bears are passive; the adult accompanying them is not.

Spawn biomes: Frozen Ocean, Deep Frozen Ocean (100% spawn chance), Snowy Plains, Ice Spikes, Frozen River, Snowy Slopes, Jagged Peaks, Frozen Peaks.

## Behavior

Adult polar bears are neutral when no cubs are present. They become hostile toward players who:
1. Directly attack any polar bear
2. Come too close to a baby polar bear nearby

If a baby is attacked (and not killed in one hit), **all adults within a 41x21x41 area** become hostile regardless of difficulty. On Peaceful difficulty they still aggro but deal no damage. Attacking an adult near a cub also provokes other nearby adults within a 21x21x21 area of the attacked adult.

Adults also attack foxes on sight.

Polar bears attack by rearing onto their hind legs and mauling down. Their **swimming speed equals the player's** -- escaping them in water is not possible by swimming alone.

Polar bears are immune to freezing damage from powder snow.

## Breeding

Polar bears cannot breed. Baby polar bears only appear through natural spawning. They grow into adults after 20 minutes and their growth cannot be sped up. Growth can be stopped permanently with a golden dandelion.

## Combat

- Health: 30HP
- Attack: 4HP (Easy), 6HP (Normal), 9HP (Hard)
- Knockback resistance: 60%
- Baby polar bears are completely passive and never attack

## Drops

- Raw Cod (0-2) **or** Raw Salmon (0-2) -- one of the two is selected randomly; Cooked Cod/Salmon if killed by fire
- 1-3 XP when killed by player or tamed wolf
- No experience from baby polar bears

## Notes

- Lead can be attached to a polar bear.
- 60% knockback resistance makes melee combat slow and dangerous.
- Never fight a polar bear near water -- they match your swimming speed and there is no escape.

# Enderman

Endermen are tall neutral mobs (2.9 blocks) found in the Overworld at night, the Nether, and the End. They are provoked by eye contact or attack and are capable of teleporting.

## Spawning

**Overworld:** Spawn at night on solid blocks with at least 3 blocks of vertical space. Common in the End and Nether.

**The End:** Spawn abundantly on End Stone. The Ender Dragon boss arena has heavy Enderman density.

**Nether:** Spawn most commonly in Warped Forests, uncommonly in Nether Wastes, and rarely in Soul Sand Valleys.

## Behavior

Endermen are neutral unless:
1. A player looks directly at their upper body (face/torso) for ~0.25 seconds -- triggers aggro
2. The Enderman is attacked

When aggro is triggered, the Enderman opens its mouth and screams. If you hold eye contact with an aggro'd Enderman from within 16 blocks, it freezes -- it will not advance while being stared at. Breaking eye contact resumes the chase. Approaching within 4 blocks of a frozen Enderman causes it to teleport away.

Endermen are also hostile toward endermites and will attack any within 64 blocks -- a fact exploited in pearl farms.

Endermen take damage from water (1HP per contact), rain, and water bottles. They will teleport away from water. They are immune to most projectiles (arrows, tridents, snowballs -- dodge by teleporting).

Wearing a pumpkin on the head prevents Endermen from being provoked by eye contact.

Endermen pick up and move certain blocks: grass, dirt, sand, gravel, clay, flowers, mushrooms, cactus, pumpkins, TNT, and others. They will place carried blocks nearby.

## Combat

- Health: 40HP
- Melee attack: 7HP (Easy), 10HP (Normal), 15HP (Hard)
- Teleports to dodge projectiles (arrows, etc.)
- Teleports away when set on fire or hit with water
- Can teleport to avoid lava

## Drops

- 0-1 Ender Pearl (common drop, looting increases yield)
- 5 XP when killed by player or tamed wolf

## Notes

- Endermen are the only source of Ender Pearls in Survival.
- The End is filled with Endermen -- a sword with Looting is recommended for pearl farming.
- Endermen will not pursue players through a 2-block-high gap (too tall to fit).
- Endermen do not take damage from the sun unlike undead mobs.

# Goat

Goats are neutral mobs found in mountain biomes. They jump very high, occasionally ram stationary targets, and can be milked. Unlike most neutral mobs, goats do not retaliate when attacked.

## Spawning

Goats spawn in groups of 2-3 above stone, gravel, packed ice, or snow in snowy slopes, jagged peaks, and frozen peaks biomes. 5% spawn as babies. 2% of naturally spawned goats are **screaming goats** -- they look identical but make different sounds and ram far more often.

Adult goats have a 10% chance to spawn missing one horn (randomly left or right).

## Behavior

Goats wander upward, preferring hills and high ground. They avoid powder snow and take 10HP less fall damage than other mobs. They don't take lethal damage from pointed dripstone if falling from under 10 blocks.

**Jumping:** When crossing an obstacle, goats lunge up to 10 blocks high and 5 blocks laterally. 30-60 second cooldown per jump. Goats never voluntarily jump more than 5 blocks downward.

**Ramming:** Every 30 seconds to 5 minutes, a goat targets a single stationary mob or player within 4-16 blocks, locks on, lowers its head, then charges. On hit: 2HP (Easy/Normal) or 3HP (Hard) damage + 9 blocks of knockback. If the target moves, the ram misses. Goats **do not retaliate** when attacked.

- **Screaming goats** ram every 5-15 seconds instead of 30s-5min.
- Mobs generally don't retaliate to being rammed (exceptions: piglins, piglin brutes, hoglins, zoglins, "Johnny" vindicators, and wardens). Ramming a zombified piglin causes all nearby zombified piglins to converge on the goat.
- Players can hold wheat to stop a charging goat.

**Horn drops:** If a goat with at least one horn rams certain naturally-occurring solid blocks (stone, coal ore, copper ore, iron ore, emerald ore, logs, packed ice), it loses one horn, which drops as a **goat horn** item. Normal goats drop "Ponder," "Sing," "Seek," or "Feel" horns. Screaming goats drop "Admire," "Call," "Yearn," or "Dream" -- otherwise unobtainable variants.

## Drops

- 1-7 XP upon successful breeding
- No item drops on death (besides goat horns if rammed off before death)

## Breeding

Breed with wheat. Baby growth accelerated by feeding wheat (10% per feeding). Standard x screaming goat offspring has ~50% chance of screaming. Two screaming goats produce a 100% screaming offspring.

## Milking

Adult goats can be milked with a bucket to produce a milk bucket. Screaming goats make a distinct sound when milked.

## Notes

- Goats will not ram players in Creative mode or on Peaceful difficulty.
- Each goat can drop at most 2 horns (one left, one right); horns do not regrow.
- Goats cannot be tamed or equipped with saddles/chests.

# Nether Fortress

The Nether Fortress is why you came here.

It generates as a sprawling elevated structure of Nether brick — bridges, corridors, towers, open courtyards — suspended above the Nether's terrain on columns or resting directly on netherrack depending on where it spawned. Finding one requires either luck or moving in the right direction. Fortresses tend to generate in bands running north-south; moving east or west along a consistent Z coordinate increases the likelihood of eventually crossing one.

Blazes spawn inside and around it. This is the primary draw — Blaze Rods are necessary for brewing and for Eyes of Ender, and the Fortress is the only reliable source. The Blazes spawn from spawner blocks in specific interior rooms. Locating the spawners rather than hunting Blazes room to room is more efficient and allows for farm construction if you're planning to return.

Wither Skeletons spawn here too. Tall black Skeletons that inflict the Wither effect on hit, carrying stone swords, moving through the Fortress's high corridors with the specific menace of something that has always lived here. They drop coal, bones, and rarely their skull. Three skulls make a Wither. The skull drop rate is low enough that farming Wither Skeletons is a significant time investment, which is the point — the Wither is meant to require effort to summon.

Nether Wart grows in the stairwell gardens. The first Nether Wart you find is almost certainly here, growing in soul sand planters on the staircases. It's the base ingredient for almost every potion. Take all of it and replant some at your base.

Chests appear in the corridors. Saddles, horse armor, gold, obsidian, nether wart, diamonds. The chest contents are worth having though the Fortress is primarily a mob source rather than a loot destination.

The Fortress has a feel. The open bridges over lava lakes, the Ghast cries from outside, the dark corridors with Wither Skeletons emerging from doorways — it is unambiguously a hostile place and it is unambiguously worth the trip.

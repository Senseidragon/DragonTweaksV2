**Role:** Reliable early loot cache; portal-building material source; Nether entry point starter.

## Generation

- Generates in ALL Overworld biomes EXCEPT the deep dark, and in ALL Nether biomes
- Only structure in the game that generates in more than one dimension
- One ruined portal per 640x640 block area (portal can appear anywhere in a 400-block zone; 240-block buffer between zones)
- 5% chance to be a giant ruined portal (3 giant designs); 95% chance to be normal (10 normal designs)
- Placement varies by biome: underground with air pocket, on surface, partially buried (2-8 blocks above surface), on ocean floor, inside windswept hills
- Terrain immediately surrounding the portal converts to netherrack; netherrack mass generates underneath the structure

## Biome Variants

- Jungle and swamp: overgrown with vines
- Ocean: placed on ocean floor; lava replaced by magma blocks
- Cold biomes (temperature below 0.15): lava replaced entirely by netherrack or magma blocks -- no exposed lava hazard
- Nether: stone brick components replaced by polished blackstone variants; iron bars replaced by chains

## Key Notes

- 15-20% of obsidian frame blocks are randomly replaced by crying obsidian on generation
- Crying obsidian in the portal frame prevents activation -- must replace with regular obsidian before lighting
- Most normal portals contain enough obsidian to complete a frame, but crying obsidian substitutions on smaller variants may leave you short; count before assuming
- Chest always generates in Java Edition; in Bedrock, terrain (commonly lava) may overwrite the chest

## Loot

Portal-building materials: flint and steel, fire charge, obsidian, flint

Gold items (common): iron nuggets, gold nuggets, golden apple, enchanted gold tools and armor (axe, hoe, pickaxe, shovel, sword, helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots)

Gold items (uncommon): gold ingots, clock, glistering melon slice, golden carrots, golden horse armor, light weighted pressure plate

Rare: block of gold, bell, enchanted golden apple, lodestone (actually the most consistent find -- roughly 2-in-3 chance per chest)

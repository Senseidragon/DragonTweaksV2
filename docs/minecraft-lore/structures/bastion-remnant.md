# Bastion Remnant

The Bastions belong to the Piglins, and the Piglins make this clear immediately.

They generate in four variants — bridge, hoglin stables, housing units, treasure room — each with a different layout and a different distribution of loot and hostiles. All of them have Piglins, Piglin Brutes, and the understanding that you are in someone else's space and they didn't invite you.

Gold armor makes regular Piglins neutral. It does nothing for Piglin Brutes. The Brutes are black-armored, axe-carrying, and attack regardless of what you're wearing. Every Bastion has them positioned near the important areas — near the treasure room, near the high-value chests. They're the Bastion's immune response to intrusion and they cannot be reasoned with or bribed.

The treasure room variant contains the most valuable loot in the Nether. Ancient Debris — the ore that processes into Netherite — appears in the chests. Lodestones, which anchor a compass to a specific location. Enchanted golden tools and armor. The chest platform in a treasure room Bastion is surrounded by Piglin Brutes and often magma blocks and void drops, because the game has decided that the best loot should be the hardest to reach.

Ancient Debris also generates in the Nether's netherrack at specific depth ranges, but Bastion chests guarantee some quantity without the mining. For a first Netherite upgrade, a Bastion treasure room run may be faster than mining.

Breaking gold blocks, gold ore, or gilded blackstone within a Piglin's line of sight makes them hostile regardless of your gold armor. The Bastion is full of gold blocks. Navigate carefully.

The architecture is heavy and low — dark stone bridges, magma floors, cage structures, the specific oppressive density of a space built by something that didn't prioritize your navigability. It is impressive in a way that makes you feel small.

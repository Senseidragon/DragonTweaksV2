**Role:** The only way in or out of [the Nether](../dimensions/nether.md) under normal play, aside from finding a [Ruined Portal](ruined_portal.md) already built.

## Building One

- Frame: obsidian, 4x5 minimum up to 23x23 maximum, corner blocks optional
- Light the inside with flint and steel, a fire charge, a fireball, or a lightning strike -- the frame must already be complete before you light it
- Cannot be activated in the End

## Crossing

- 4 seconds standing inside in Survival (instant in Creative); step out early to cancel
- 15-second cooldown after arriving -- you cannot be pulled straight back through right away
- Withers and the Ender Dragon can never use one
- Distance is scaled 1:8 -- one block of travel in the Nether covers eight Overworld blocks, which is why portal-hopping is the fast way to cross long Overworld distances

## Linking

- Using a portal with no linked partner on the other side builds one automatically near the matching coordinates -- it can land you somewhere bad (inside a wall, over lava, off a ledge), so be ready to act the moment you arrive
- A portal built wider than 18 blocks splits and links to two separate portals on the other side instead of one

## What It Does To Mobs

- [Hoglins](../hostile/hoglin.md) refuse to walk into an active portal
- A [zombified piglin](../neutral/zombified_piglin.md) occasionally spawns at the base of an active portal frame on the Overworld side; it arrives with its return cooldown already running, so it can't immediately step back through
- No other mob spawns from a portal directly

## While Standing In One

- Your inventory won't open
- Any open chest or villager trade window slams shut the moment you step in

## Notes

- Carry a way to handle whatever's on the other side of a new link before you step through -- the destination is not guaranteed to be safe ground

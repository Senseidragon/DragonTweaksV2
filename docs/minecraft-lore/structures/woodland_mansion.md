**Role:** High-danger one-time dungeon; primary source of totems of undying; rare loot cache.

## Generation

- Generates rarely in dark forests (1.21.1; pale garden support added in 1.21.4 -- not applicable here)
- Located via woodland explorer maps purchased from cartographer villagers -- the map may NOT point to the nearest mansion
- Also locatable with `/locate structure mansion` (requires cheats enabled)

## Mobs

- Vindicators spawn on all 3 floors and rarely in corridors
- Evokers spawn from floor 2 upward in large rooms only; floor 1 rooms contain vindicators only
- Allays imprisoned in jail room cells, often with a vindicator guard outside
- CRITICAL: vindicators and evokers DO NOT respawn after being killed -- the mansion can be permanently cleared; unlike pillager outposts where pillagers constantly respawn
- Interior lighting is low enough for other Overworld hostile mobs to spawn inside rooms and enter through the open front entrance at night

## Structure

- 3 floors; top floor is smaller than the lower two
- Cobblestone foundation generates underneath the entire structure to fill any terrain gaps
- Large foyer on entry with a main staircase to floor 2; the foyer is dark and mobs can spawn under the staircase
- Room layout is randomly generated; not every room type appears in every mansion
- Some rooms have sealed or hidden entrances; secret rooms have NO entrance at all

## Rooms

The interior is a maze of randomly assembled chambers connected by dark corridors -- no two mansions are laid out the same. Expect to find:

- **Farming rooms** -- wheat fields lit by torches, mushroom patches on coarse dirt, a two-tiered pumpkin and melon farm, rows of saplings on dirt shelves, even a room with an artificial dark oak tree and a sealed entrance hiding a chest with an iron axe
- **Libraries** -- at least one small study with bookshelves; often a larger lounge version with armchairs and walls lined floor to ceiling with books
- **Dining halls** -- one or two large halls with long tables, chairs, and chandeliers; smaller sitting rooms with a single table are common throughout
- **Bedrooms** -- several, ranging from a simple cot to a four-post master bed; one has a closet below and a loft with a chest above, reached by ladder
- **Jail cells** -- cobblestone cage rooms with iron doors, cauldrons inside, and a vindicator stationed outside; allays are sometimes found imprisoned here
- **A forge** -- polished stone arches around a damaged anvil and a contained lava pool; a vindicator is always present
- **A ritual room** -- altar draped in black carpet with black banners overhead; always has two vindicators and an evoker -- one of the most dangerous rooms in the building
- **Wool statue rooms** -- oversized sculptures built entirely from colored wool: a cat, a chicken, and a giant illager figure; no loot, just unsettling decor
- **A nature room** -- artificial dark oak tree in one corner, stone water feature in another; no mobs, no loot
- **An arena** -- raised stone platform ringed by dark oak fencing; loot chest concealed in a loft overhead

Large rooms on floors 2 and 3 frequently contain a pair of vindicators and an evoker. The ritual room and the large meeting halls are consistently the most dangerous rooms in the building.

## Secret Rooms

Some rooms have no entrances at all -- you find them by spotting gaps between walls, peering through windows from outside, or cutting through the roof. Worth hunting for:

- **A room decorated with cobblestone X patterns** -- nothing threatening, just a chest
- **A web-choked room** -- packed floor to ceiling with cobwebs, spider spawner in the center; dangerous to enter blind
- **A room built around a strange obsidian structure** -- worth breaking into; there is something valuable inside
- **A room with lava sealed behind glass walls** -- something glints inside; getting to it is the challenge
- **A room made to look like an End portal** -- do not open the chest; it is rigged with explosives and the walls are full of silverfish
- **A plain room with a lone chest** -- nothing else, no tricks; found on the ground floor
- **An attic space** tucked above other rooms with a pair of chests on the upper tier; the most common secret room and usually the easiest to reach
- **Occasionally just four pillars** -- nothing in it; the mansion has a sense of humor

## Loot (Chests)

Notable items from mansion chests:
- Vex armor trim smithing template (common -- roughly coin-flip per chest)
- Lead, golden apple, diamond hoe, music disc (13), music disc (cat) (moderate)
- Enchanted book, chainmail chestplate (moderate)
- Diamond chestplate (uncommon)
- Enchanted golden apple (rare)

Totems of undying are NOT in chests -- they drop directly from evokers on kill.

# Ruined Portal

Someone was trying to go somewhere and didn't make it.

Ruined portals are everywhere once you start noticing them — slumped in fields, half-submerged in rivers, jutting out of cliffsides, scattered across the Nether's ashfields with the same abandoned air. Obsidian frames, some standing, some tipped flat on the ground like they fell over mid-construction. Crying obsidian where the regular kind should be, weeping purple in a way that feels less decorative than *wrong*. Gold blocks scattered around the base, chests nearby with flint and steel and more gold, as though whoever was working here left in a hurry and expected to come back.

They didn't come back. The portals sit incomplete.

What I've never resolved is whether these are ruins of portals that worked and then broke, or portals that never finished being built. The ones lying flat suggest collapse — something that stood and fell. But some of them look more like an interrupted project, the frame only half-assembled, materials still staged nearby. A civilization's worth of failed attempts at crossing between worlds, or one civilization's repeated tries at the same crossing?

Crying obsidian is the clue no one's properly explained to me. It appears when a portal anchor is lost — when you die in the Nether without a set respawn, the portal you return through is framed in crying obsidian, weeping light. Someone died near these portals. Many someones, maybe. The gold was left as tribute or salvage and no one came to collect it.

Repair one if you have the obsidian and the flint. The portal works fine once completed. Whatever broke the original connection doesn't seem to hold a grudge.

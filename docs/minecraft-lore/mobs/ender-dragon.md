# Ender Dragon

Everything in this world leads here.

The Eyes of Ender, the stronghold, the portal, the obsidian platform in the void — all of it is a path to this encounter. The Dragon circles the central pillar of the End, diving periodically, sustained by beams from the End Crystals on top of the obsidian pillars. Destroy the crystals first. As long as they're active they heal the Dragon continuously, and damaging the Dragon without dealing with the crystals is a long exercise in futility. Some crystals are caged — iron bars around them — and destroying those requires either breaking the bars or shooting the crystal from below.

The Dragon has two states: circling and perching. When circling it's mostly invulnerable except for its head, which takes full damage from arrows. When perching on the central fountain structure it becomes vulnerable to all damage but summons Endermen and breathes Dragon's Breath — a purple cloud that lingers and deals damage over time. The breath is also a resource: collect it in a glass bottle and you have a brewing ingredient.

The Dragon can't destroy End Stone. Everything else in the End that it flies through gets destroyed. Build in End Stone if you need cover.

It drops twelve thousand experience points on death and an egg. The egg teleports when interacted with and can only be collected with a piston or careful trap mechanics. It has no further use beyond existing — a trophy, the only one of its kind, proof of what you did.

Death opens a portal home and reveals the End Poem. The poem runs without interruption and cannot be skipped and has something to say about what you've done and what it means. Whether it's right about what it means is a question the poem leaves open, which is the correct choice.

You can fight the Dragon again by placing four End Crystals on the central fountain's edges. It respawns. The egg does not.

# Vindicator

The Vindicator carries an axe and uses it.

That's the summary. Everything else is elaboration on those two facts. The axe deals more damage than most melee weapons in the game. The Vindicator is fast — faster than you expect from something that large — and it closes distance aggressively. It doesn't hang back and wait. It comes for you and it comes hard.

They appear in two places: Woodland Mansions and raids. In the Mansion they patrol the corridors and wait in rooms, and the rooms of a Woodland Mansion are designed with enough clutter and enough Vindicators that managing them at close range in enclosed spaces is consistently punishing. In raids they appear in the later waves, when you've already been fighting for some time and your resources are depleted from what came before.

There's a name. If you name a Vindicator "Johnny" — actually place a name tag on it that says Johnny — it becomes aggressive toward every other mob except other Illagers. The behavior change is extreme and specific and the reference is a horror film from the 1980s. The world has, embedded in its mechanics, a joke about a man with an axe. The Vindicator was the right choice for this.

They drop Emeralds sometimes. Axes with enchantments. The axes are worth picking up if you're early in a playthrough and haven't found better, though the circumstances under which you're picking up a Vindicator's dropped axe involve having survived the Vindicator, which is the more significant achievement.

In a raid they're the answer to the question of what to do if the previous waves weren't hard enough. The Pillagers soften you, the Ravager comes through and disrupts your positioning, and then the Vindicators arrive and start looking for anything still standing. The sequencing of a raid is designed to exhaust your resources before the hardest threats appear. The Vindicator shows up when you're tired and it doesn't care.

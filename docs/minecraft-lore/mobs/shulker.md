# Shulker

The Shulker is a box that shoots at you.

It attaches to surfaces in End Cities — walls, floors, ceilings — disguised as a purple block until something gets close enough for it to open. When it opens, it fires a homing projectile: a small shell that tracks you, moves slowly but persistently, and on hit applies Levitation. Levitation is a status effect that pushes you upward regardless of your intention. In a tall End City tower, Levitation from a Shulker hit sends you up toward the ceiling and then, when the effect ends, drops you however many blocks you've risen. The fall kills you. Your items fall into the void. This is the primary Shulker threat — not the direct damage of the projectile but what the Levitation does to you in the specific environment where Shulkers live.

The shell can be deflected. It tracks slowly enough that you can sidestep it, and if it misses and hits a wall it pops harmlessly. Blocking with a shield stops it entirely. The skill expression in Shulker fights is keeping track of multiple shells — Shulkers fire multiple projectiles, several Shulkers in the same area fire many — and managing the Levitation windows when they land.

They close their shells when they take damage and become temporarily invulnerable. The vulnerability window is only when the shell is open, which means you're timing attacks to the brief moments when the shell is exposed rather than maintaining sustained pressure.

Shulker Shells drop on death. Two shells make a Shulker Box — a portable storage container that retains its contents when broken. Shulker Boxes are the inventory expansion endgame. A double chest worth of Shulker Boxes, each full of resources, carried in your inventory: that's what the Shulker makes possible, and it's why End City runs are worth doing beyond the elytra.

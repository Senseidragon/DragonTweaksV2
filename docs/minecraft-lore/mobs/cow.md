# Cow

The Cow is the most useful passive mob in the game and it knows nothing about this.

It wanders. It eats grass, or doesn't, since the eating is mostly aesthetic. It follows wheat when held in front of it with a patience that suggests it has assigned special significance to wheat and found everything else wanting. It makes the low sound cows make. It exists in fields and forests and anywhere that isn't too cold or too dry, a large placid presence in the landscape that registers your approach with mild curiosity and then returns to whatever it was doing.

Leather, beef, and milk. That's the entire economy. Leather for early-game armor and books and item frames and the hundred other things leather touches. Beef cooked over a fire for one of the better hunger sources available without farming. Milk from a bucket, which clears status effects — poison, blindness, the Wither effect, Mining Fatigue from Elder Guardians, anything the game has applied to you that shouldn't be applied to you. The bucket of milk is a universal antidote and the Cow is the only source of it.

Breeding with wheat produces calves that grow into adults and expand the herd. This is how early resource problems get solved — find two Cows, feed them wheat, wait for calves, wait for calves to grow, have a sustainable supply of everything Cows provide. The Cow is one of the first systems you understand and one that remains relevant at every stage of the game.

Mooshrooms are the mushroom biome variant. Red or brown, covered in mushrooms, occupying one of the rarest biomes in the world. They can be sheared for mushrooms, milked normally, or milked with a bowl for Mushroom Stew. They convert to normal Cows if you shear off the mushrooms. The specificity of this suggests someone had a vision for the mushroom biome that the Mooshroom is the central expression of.

There's something grounding about the Cow. In a world that contains Wardens and Evokers and the deep dark and the End, the Cow is a large gentle animal standing in a field, waiting for wheat, asking nothing of you that you aren't willing to give.

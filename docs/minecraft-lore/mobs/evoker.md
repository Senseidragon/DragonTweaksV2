# Evoker

The Evoker is the most dangerous thing in a Woodland Mansion and one of the most dangerous things you'll find anywhere in the overworld.

It doesn't move much. It stands in its room in its dark robes and it summons. Vexes, first — small winged creatures that phase through walls and hit hard relative to their size. Three at a time, summoned in waves, flying through whatever obstacles you've positioned yourself behind. The Vexes are a problem that requires immediate management because they don't stop and they ignore terrain. While you're dealing with Vexes, the Evoker has begun something else.

The fangs. Two rows of spectral teeth emerging from the ground beneath your feet, tracking your position, dealing damage in sequence. They can be dodged with careful movement. They cannot be blocked or absorbed. They appear under you regardless of what you're standing on. The combination of Vexes harrying you from above and fangs tracking you from below leaves very little comfortable space to occupy during an Evoker fight.

The Totem of Undying is why you're there. The Evoker carries one and drops it on death — a one-time get-out-of-death item that triggers when you would otherwise be killed, restoring health and granting brief regeneration and absorption. It's the only source of the Totem in the game. You have to come here, to this mansion, to this room, to this specific mob, to get it. The Evoker is the gatekeeper of the one item that lets you survive something that would otherwise kill you, which is a design decision worth respecting.

They have one additional trick. If a Blue Sheep is nearby, the Evoker will convert it to red. The purpose of this is unclear, the trigger condition unusual, and the practical effect minor. It's one of those details that exists in the world without explanation, like the Evoker needed something to do during the moments when it wasn't actively killing you.

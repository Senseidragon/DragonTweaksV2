# Frog

Frogs are the result of tadpoles growing up in different biomes, and the biome determines the color.

Temperate biomes produce orange Frogs. Cold biomes produce green Frogs. Warm biomes produce white Frogs. The color is the only cosmetic difference — the behavior is the same. They hop around on land and in water, they eat small Slimes and small Magma Cubes, and the eating produces something: Slime drops a Slimeball, Magma Cube drops a Frogligh block in a color matching the Frog's variant. White Frog eats Magma Cube, produces Ochre Froglight. Orange Frog produces Pearlescent. Green Frog produces Verdant.

Froglight is a full-brightness light block with a specific aesthetic value — warm, organic, nothing like glowstone or shroomlight. It's available only through this interaction, which means producing it intentionally requires having the right color Frog in proximity to a live Magma Cube small enough to eat. The Magma Cube must be small — large and medium variants are too big. Farming Froglight is an operation with specific requirements and a specific result.

They lay eggs. Frog eggs — Frogspawn — are laid on water, hatch into tadpoles, and the tadpoles grow into Frogs with the color determined by the biome they're in when they mature. Moving a tadpole to a different biome before it matures changes the resulting Frog's color. This is how you get non-native color variants into a biome — you bring the tadpoles there rather than the adult.

Frogs jump high relative to their size. They take reduced fall damage. They make the sound frogs make.

They're in swamps and mangrove swamps primarily. The mangrove swamp variant feels like home — the Frog in dense aerial roots and warm water, surrounded by the specific humid complexity of that biome, is a combination that works.

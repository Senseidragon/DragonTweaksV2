# Zombie

The Zombie is the most common thing that will try to kill you and probably the least interesting.

It walks toward you. It hits you. It takes damage until it stops. There's no archery, no explosion, no special mechanic to navigate. Just a slow walk and a hit that does modest damage and a moan that you'll learn to recognize and then stop hearing consciously because it's just the background noise of any night outside.

They burn in sunlight. They wear armor sometimes. They can pick up items off the ground. In groups they have a mechanic for alerting nearby Zombies when one is hurt, which means a cave that seems lightly populated can suddenly produce a crowd. This is the only interesting thing about them tactically — the social alarm — and it's learned after the first time it happens and then managed around.

Villages complicate things. Zombies will hammer at doors until they break them during hard difficulty, which converts peaceful-looking settlements into siege scenarios if you're not managing the perimeter. Villagers that get caught by Zombies become Zombie Villagers, which are curable but remain a problem until cured. The calculus of a village after dark involves patrolling, lighting, and the specific frustration of watching a Zombie eat a librarian you were cultivating for good trades.

Baby Zombies are faster. Much faster. They fit through small spaces, they juke unpredictably, and they ride Chickens, which is one of those details that seems like a joke until a Chicken Jockey appears out of the dark and closes the distance before you've processed what you're seeing. The game is occasionally absurdist about its threats.

Drowned are what Zombies become underwater — converted by drowning, which implies the Zombie has retained just enough biology for this transition to be possible. They carry tridents sometimes. Tridents are rare. The Drowned therefore becomes a specific target rather than background noise, which is the pattern: everything in this world eventually becomes either a resource or a hazard and the Zombie progresses from nuisance to material in the usual way.

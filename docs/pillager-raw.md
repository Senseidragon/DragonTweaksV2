pillager spawned by a player using a spawn egg\n or commands\n can be a captain, which has an ominous banner\n in their head slot\n, which displays as attached to the back of their head.\n\n### Patrols\n\nIn _Java Edition_\n, the number of pillagers spawning\n in each patrol\n varies from one to five and depends on the regional difficulty\n, with one spawning as a patrol captain\n. Patrols occur after 51⁄2 in-game days, any time and independently of structures, which means they still spawn in worlds without generated structures\n. A patrol spawns 24 to 48 blocks away from a random player, on the highest solid block. The individual pillagers of a patrol can spawn only at block light\n level less than 9, regardless of sky light.\n\nIn _Bedrock Edition_\n, each patrol spawns two to five pillagers with one spawning as a patrol captain. After 51⁄2 in-game days, patrols appear 24 to 48 blocks away from the player\n, at a light level of 0-7.\n\n### Pillager outposts\n\nPillagers continually spawn in a 72×54×72 block\n volume centered on the top floor in a pillager outpost\n. Some pillagers spawn as outpost captains. They may spawn on any valid opaque block if the block light level is less than or equal to 8.\n\nIn _Java Edition_\n, there can be unlimited pillagers that spawn in outposts naturally, limited only by the natural spawning rules.\n\nIn _Bedrock Edition_\n, pillagers continually spawn at or below a particular location in the outpost, choosing the highest opaque block with a non-solid block on top and spawning on the northwest corner. A maximum of eight pillagers spawn naturally in an outpost.\n\n### Raids\n\nIn a raid\n, pillagers are more common in the earlier waves and decrease in number as the waves progress. Despite this, they still constitute the majority of raiders in total.\n\nOne pillager spawns riding a ravager\n in the fifth wave.\n\nIn _Bedrock Edition_\n, one of the ravagers is ridden by a pillager instead of a vindicator\n in the seventh wave.\n\nIn _Java Edition_\n, pillagers spawn during bonus waves and have the largest chance of being the raid captain in the first wave, but in the second wave and onward, vindicators have a greater chance than pillagers of being the raid captain\n.\n\nDrops\n-----\n\n### On death\n\n_Java Edition\n_:\n\n*   Decimal\n    \n*   Fraction\n    \n*

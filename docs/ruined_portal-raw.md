\\]\n}\n\nA **ruined portal** is a [structure](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Structure \"Structure\")\n resembling a damaged [Nether portal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_portal \"Nether portal\")\n, which generates commonly in both [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether \"The Nether\")\n and the [Overworld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Overworld \"Overworld\")\n. It contains some decoration and a loot chest around it.\n\nGeneration\n----------\n\n[](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Ruined_Portal_underside_in_water.png)\n\nUnderside of a ruined portal\n\nRuined portals are the only structures that generate in more than one dimension; they generate in all [biomes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Biome \"Biome\")\n in both the Nether and the Overworld, except the [deep dark](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Deep_dark \"Deep dark\")\n. They can spawn underground, underwater, or exposed to the air. If they generate underground, they have air pockets around them. Natural terrain around ruined portals generates as [netherrack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherrack \"Netherrack\")\n. They also generate a mass of netherrack underneath them (including \"stalactite\"-like shapes, and this may contain [blackstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blackstone \"Blackstone\")\n deposits in the Nether). Giant ruined portals have 3 distinct designs, and normal ruined portals have 10 designs. When a ruined portal generates, it has a 5% chance to be a giant ruined portal, for about a 1.67% chance per giant portal design. This gives normal ruined portals a 95% chance, for a 9.5% chance per normal ruined portal design.\n\nRuined portals generate in a grid of squares 25 chunks (400 blocks) wide with 15 chunks (240 blocks) of buffer space between the squares. In other words, a ruined portal can generate at X and Z coordinates between 0 and 399 mod 640. [↑](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ruined_portal#cite_ref-netherrack_1-0 \"Jump up\")\n     This includes netherrack generated as part of the netherrack \"spread\" through nearby terrain.\n\nStructure\n---------\n\nMain article: [/Structure](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ruined_Portal/Structure \"Ruined Portal/Structure\")\n\nRuined portals generate damaged portal frames composed of [obsidian](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Obsidian \"Obsidian\")\n, sometimes along with [crying obsidian](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crying_obsidian \"Crying obsidian\")\n, although Nether portals cannot be activated with crying obsidian in the frame. Some frames generate flat on the ground, as if they toppled over. Others are free-standing separately, as if still being assembled/attached.\n\nAll ruined portals generate with a [chest](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chest \"Chest\")\n in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition \"Bedrock Edition\")\n, but in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition \"Java Edition\")\n, the chest may be replaced if terrain overrides it (often lava). Each chest contains various gold items and items used to build portals, such as [obsidian](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Obsidian \"Obsidian\")\n and [flint and steel](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Flint_and_steel \"Flint and steel\")\n.\n\nAny ruined portal generated in the [Overworld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Overworld \"Overworld\")\n is surrounded with structures made of [stone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stone \"Stone\")\n, [stone bricks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stone_bricks \"Stone bricks\")\n, and [iron bars](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_bars \"Iron bars\")\n; in the Nether, it is surrounded by [blackstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blackstone \"Blackstone\")\n variants and [chains](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chain \"Chain\")\n. However, both the jungle and swamp variants of `ruined_portal/portal_6` may spawn without their stone brick slab foundations.\n\nThere are 13 variants: 10 normal size portals and 3 giant portals in varying states of decay.\n\nIn [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition \"Java Edition\")\n, ruined portals can be generated by the player by loading `ruined_portal/portal_<1 to 10>` or `ruined_portal/giant_portal_<1 to 3>` with a [structure block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Structure_block \"Structure block\")\n. These ruined portals generate as they are stored, meaning they are not modified as detailed in the Generation section above. All portals generated this way create air pockets if generated in other blocks.\n\n### Overworld\n\nNote that some blocks can be replaced with others (such as [crying obsidian](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crying_Obsidian \"Crying Obsidian\")\n) upon generation.

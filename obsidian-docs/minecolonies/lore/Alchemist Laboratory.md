The [[Alchemist Laboratory]] is where the Alchemist works. The Alchemist brews potions, harvests Nether Wart and Mistletoe.

> **Note:** Upgrading the [[Alchemist Laboratory]] lets you teach it more recipes. So:

| Building Level | Number of Recipes |
| -------------- | ----------------- |
| 1              | 10                |
| 2              | 20                |
| 3              | 40                |
| 4              | 80                |
| 5              | 160               |

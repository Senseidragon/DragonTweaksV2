Colony connections allow you to link your colony to neighboring colonies, forming a network that serves as the foundation for inter-colony features like trading and diplomacy. Each colony involved must have a [[Gatehouse]], which acts as the anchor point for all connections.

To establish a connection, interact with your own [[Gatehouse]] hut block to start the process, then walk toward the other colony placing colony sign blocks as you go — each sign must be within 50 blocks of the previous one, and the entire path must be fully walkable by colonists. When close enough to the other colony's [[Gatehouse]], interact with their hut block to finalize the link. Gatehouse hut blocks are the only hut blocks that can be interacted with regardless of colony ownership, which is what makes this handshake possible.

Established connections are listed in the [[Gatehouse]]'s Connections tab. Direct connections are colonies you have linked to yourself; indirect connections are colonies visible through your allies' networks. Diplomatic standing — neutral, allied, or feuding — is set in the Alliances tab of the [[Town Hall]], and both sides must independently agree for any status change to take effect. Neutral colonies cannot be travelled to; allied colonies can be reached via teleport directly from the [[Gatehouse]] Connections tab.

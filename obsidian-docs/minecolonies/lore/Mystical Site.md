The [[Mystical Site]] is a simple building that increase a colony's overall happiness level. It has no worker and increases colonists' happiness just by existing.

The happiness bonus does not stack - only the highest level of [[Mystical Site]] in the colony will provide a bonus. While there is no benefit to building multiple Mysical Sites, one is not prevented from doing so should they wish.

The [[Mystical Site]] also gives a bonus to the maximum resurrection chance of the Undertaker

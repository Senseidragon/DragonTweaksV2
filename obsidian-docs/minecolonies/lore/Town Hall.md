The [[Town Hall]] is the heart of any colony. Placing its block with the Scepter of Gold establishes the colony and sets a permanent protected center — the chosen location cannot be changed, so it should be chosen carefully. The protected area extends roughly four chunks in every direction by default, from bedrock to sky limit, encompassing whatever terrain, caves, and structures fall within that radius.

Within the protected area, no one outside the colony can place or break blocks, ignite explosives, or pour liquids. If another colony's territory is too close, placement will be refused entirely.

The [[Town Hall]] block can be moved within the established protected area by breaking and re-placing it without affecting the colony boundary itself. Removing or relocating the block does not dissolve the colony — only a command from an operator or administrator can do that. To intentionally delete a colony, place a new [[Town Hall]] block outside the current protected border and interact with it; this will prompt colony deletion.

The [[Town Hall]] GUI is the central management panel for the colony: renaming it, reviewing and adjusting worker assignments, managing build order priorities, setting permissions for other players, and handling diplomacy with connected colonies.

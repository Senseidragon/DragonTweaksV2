The quality of tools, weapons, and armor a worker can use is determined by the level of their work hut. A worker in a low-level hut is limited to basic materials with little or no enchantment; upgrading their building unlocks progressively better materials and higher enchantment levels. A fully upgraded hut places no restrictions at all.

For most workers, the progression runs from wood or gold tools at the lowest levels up through stone, iron, diamond, and netherite as the building improves. Enchantments follow the same pattern — a worker in a level-1 hut can use lightly enchanted tools, while a level-5 hut removes all limits. Bows and fishing rods follow a similar rule: enchantment slots unlock one at a time as the hut level rises.

Guards follow their own version of this system for armor. A low-level [[Guard Tower]] or [[Barracks Tower]] limits guards to leather or gold armor with no enchantments. Each level up allows heavier materials — chainmail, iron, diamond, and eventually netherite — as well as more powerful enchantments on whatever armor they wear. Upgrading guard buildings is therefore a direct investment in how well your guards can protect the colony.

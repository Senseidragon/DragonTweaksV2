The [[Flowershop]] is where your Florist will grow flowers for your colony, if given Compost and an axe.

> **Note:** The higher the level of the building, the more daily output the Florist can handle. So:

| Building Level | Number of Plants |
| -------------- | ---------------- |
| 1              | 4                |
| 2              | 8                |
| 3              | 12               |
| 4              | 16               |
| 5              | 20               |

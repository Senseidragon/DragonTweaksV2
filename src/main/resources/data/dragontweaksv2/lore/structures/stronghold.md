**Role:** Hard progression gate; End Portal source; secondary enchanted book cache.

## Generation

- Generates underground in any Overworld biome; prefers above sea level but will generate underwater if needed
- 128 strongholds per world in 8 concentric rings around world origin (X=0, Z=0)
- Nearest 3 strongholds: 1,280–2,816 blocks from origin; subsequent rings push out to ~24,000 blocks maximum
- Each ring spaces its strongholds at roughly equal angles from the center
- Can be partially overwritten by caves, mineshafts, ocean monuments, ancient cities, or geodes; the End Portal itself is rarely destroyed, but if it is, another stronghold must be found (all 12 frames required for activation)

## Locating

Throw an eye of ender; it rises and drifts toward the nearest stronghold, then falls. Follow the direction repeatedly. When the eye falls and returns downward at your feet, the stronghold is below. Eyes of ender have a 20% chance to shatter on use. Digging down typically reveals a stone brick wall or corridor.

## Structure

- Stone brick construction: ~45% regular, 30% mossy, 20% cracked, 5% infested (contain silverfish)
- Begins with a spiral staircase leading down to a five-way crossing; rooms branch randomly from there
- Layout can extend up to 50 rooms; no two strongholds have identical layouts

**Key rooms:**
- **Portal room** -- always generates; never within 5 rooms of the entrance; ringed by lava, one silverfish spawner, iron bar gate entrance; 12 end portal frames each with 10% pre-filled eye (~1.2 average pre-filled)
- **Libraries** -- 0–2 per stronghold; large (2-floor, 2 chests) or small (1-floor, 1 chest) variant; cobwebs throughout; guaranteed enchanted book per chest
- **Large rooms** -- up to 6; variants include empty, fountain, pillar, and storeroom (2-floor room with loot chest on upper level)
- **Loot altars** -- up to 4; chest on a raised stone platform in a corridor
- **Jail cells** -- iron-barred cells; no loot, no mobs beyond normal stronghold spawns
- **Staircases** -- spiral and straight variants; connect floors

## Mobs

Silverfish only. They emerge from infested stone brick blocks when struck. The portal room always contains a silverfish spawner. Silverfish call for help from nearby infested blocks when attacked, potentially triggering cascading block breaks. Fighting near walls is risky.

## Activating the End Portal

The portal room has 12 frames arranged in a ring over a lava pool. Each frame must contain an eye of ender to activate. Average ~1.2 frames come pre-filled; the player must supply the rest. Pre-filled eyes can be in any position. The chance of a fully pre-activated portal is roughly 1 in 1 trillion.

## Loot Summary

**Altar chests:** Ender pearl (~23%), iron gear (~12% each piece), diamond (~7%), eye armor trim (~10%), music disc (otherside) (~2.5%), golden apple (~2.5%), enchanted book (~2.5%)

**Library chests:** Eye armor trim (100% guaranteed), enchanted book (~68–73%), paper (~89%), book (~89%), compass (~11%)

**Storeroom chests:** Apple, bread, coal, iron ingot (common); enchanted book (~4%)

## Notes

- The nearest stronghold to world origin is the most accessible; most players will find one of the 3 in the first ring
- Silverfish summoning makes clearing the portal room dangerous -- use a sword, kill quickly, avoid prolonged fights near walls
- Libraries are secondary value: enchanted books with treasure enchantments can include Mending
- Strongholds can overlap with ancient cities (both underground) -- if an ancient city is nearby, approach carefully

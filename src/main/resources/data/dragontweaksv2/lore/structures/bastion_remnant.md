**Role:** Piglin stronghold and the only reliable, repeatable source of netherite upgrade smithing templates; also gates several decorative/cosmetic-exclusive items.

## Generation

- Generates in all Nether biomes except basalt deltas -- a Nether fortress generates instead if a bastion would have spawned there
- The Nether is divided into large regions (368x368 to 416x416 generatable blocks after border spacing); each region rolls either a fortress or a bastion, never both (though the two structures may overlap near a region border)
- Chance of a bastion winning that roll over a fortress is roughly 60% in older versions, 66.6% in current versions
- All 4 structural variants are equally likely to generate: Bridge, Hoglin Stables, Housing Units, Treasure Room
- Piglins and piglin brutes spawn permanently on structure generation; hoglins also spawn on generation in the Bridge and Hoglin Stables variants. None of these despawn naturally, piglins don't fight each other, and once killed they do not respawn (same rule as End City shulkers)

## Structure

- **Bridge** -- large ruined rampart with a piglin-face carving; multi-level walkways over a lava sea; a damaged bridge span with a support pillar
- **Hoglin Stables** -- three-part rampart with damaged stable wings on either side; stable-themed loot chests
- **Housing Units** -- several ruined two-part ramparts surrounding a central courtyard where nether wart grows naturally
- **Treasure Room** -- rampart on buttresses connected by a bridge to the treasure chamber; the chamber floor has a magma cube monster spawner, an open lava floor, and a gold-block island holding 1-2 treasure chests. Ghasts can spawn inside the chamber in biome-compatible Nether biomes, turning the treasure pull into a real fight

All variants are built primarily from basalt, polished basalt, blackstone (plus slabs/stairs/walls), gilded blackstone, polished/cracked/chiseled blackstone bricks, gold blocks, iron chains, and lava, with glowstone/lanterns for light and monster spawners (magma cube) in the Treasure Room.

## Why It Matters

- **Netherite upgrade smithing template** -- bastion remnants are the only source; the Treasure Room's treasure chest guarantees one every time it's opened, while the other 3 chest types only have a ~10% chance per chest
- **Gilded blackstone, Pigstep music disc, snout banner pattern, snout armor trim smithing template** -- exclusive to bastion remnant loot; the Pigstep disc only drops from Generic-type chests
- **Ancient debris / netherite scrap / netherite ingot** -- bastion chests are a viable supplementary source alongside mining, especially the Treasure chest

## Loot By Chest Type

All figures are per-chest probabilities (Java Edition; Bedrock is similar within a few points).

**Bridge chest** -- guaranteed Lodestone (100%); common rolls (~48%) of arrow, iron/gold nugget, string, leather; mid rolls (~11%) of spectral arrow, gilded blackstone, iron/gold ingot, crying obsidian, a damaged enchanted crossbow, block of gold, golden sword, or an enchanted golden armor piece/axe; netherite upgrade smithing template (10.0%); snout armor trim smithing template (8.3%).

**Generic chest** -- the most varied table and the only source of the Pigstep music disc; arrow/magma cream/gilded blackstone (~44%); iron chain/obsidian/string/nuggets/bone block/cooked porkchop (~24%); iron/gold ingot, block of iron/gold, damaged enchanted iron sword (~19%); golden carrot and ancient debris x1 (13.5% each); spectral arrow, enchanted book (possible Soul Speed) (~11%); snout banner pattern, golden apple (~10%); netherite upgrade smithing template (10.0%); crossbow, golden sword, enchanted golden axe, golden armor pieces, enchanted golden boots w/Soul Speed (~9.8%); snout armor trim smithing template (8.3%); damaged enchanted crossbow, diamond shovel, enchanted diamond pickaxe (~6.7%); **Pigstep music disc (5.6%, exclusive to this chest)**; netherite scrap (4.5%).

**Hoglin Stable chest** -- arrow, string, gold nugget, crimson fungus/nylium/roots, glowstone, soul sand, cooked/raw porkchop, gilded blackstone, crying obsidian, leather, enchanted golden axe (~23% each); block of gold (16.0%); damaged enchanted diamond shovel (15.0%); ancient debris x1, saddle, enchanted diamond pickaxe (12.0% each); golden carrot, golden apple, netherite upgrade smithing template (10.0% each); snout armor trim smithing template (8.3%); netherite scrap (8.0%); ancient debris x2 (5.0%).

**Treasure chest** -- **netherite upgrade smithing template is guaranteed, 100% of every chest opened**; netherite ingot (35.0%); spectral arrow, nether quartz, gilded blackstone, iron/gold ingot, magma cream, crying obsidian, block of iron/gold (~33.7% each); ancient debris x1 (24.5%); netherite scrap (19.9%); damaged enchanted diamond gear (spear/sword/helmet/chestplate/leggings/boots, ~15.2% each); diamond (2-6) and undamaged diamond armor pieces (~12.8% each); ancient debris x2 (10.3%); snout armor trim smithing template (8.3%); enchanted golden apple (5.3%).

## Notes

- The Treasure Room is the single best farming target for netherite upgrade smithing templates -- every other source in the game is far less reliable than this guaranteed chest roll
- Ghasts spawning in the Treasure Room (where biome-compatible) make full diamond gear and explosive knockback a real hazard around the open lava floor
- Generation-spawned piglins/piglin brutes/hoglins are a one-time population -- clearing a bastion of mobs once means it stays cleared

**Role:** Hard progression gate; only source of blaze rods (brewing, end portal) and wither skeleton skulls (wither boss).

## Generation

- Generates in all Nether biomes
- Competes with bastion remnants for generation slots per ~432×432 region; 40% chance fortress, 60% bastion (Java Edition)
- Exception: basalt deltas biome always produces a fortress instead of a bastion -- the most reliable biome for guaranteed fortress generation
- Two structures never share a region, but may overlap near region borders
- May generate buried inside netherrack; interiors always clear, but external walkways may tunnel through netherrack walls

## Structure

Two distinct zones connected by a lava well room:

- **Exterior bridges** -- open walkways above the lava sea; straight spans and four-way crossings; up to 4 arch crossings; up to 2 blaze spawner platforms (raised fenced platform, blaze spawner in center)
- **Interior corridors** -- enclosed Nether brick hallways; up to 20 turns (1-in-3 chance each of a loot chest in the corner); up to 2 Nether wart staircases (soul sand gardens at the base with Nether wart growing on it); stairways and dead ends

The structure can generate any size -- small fortresses do exist and may have none or only one of the landmark rooms.

## Mobs

Fortress uses its own spawn list, overriding the biome. Mobs spawn on any Nether brick floor (not slabs) within the fortress bounding box.

- **Blaze** -- most common (~36%); spawn in groups of 2–3; found throughout but cluster near spawner platforms
- **Wither Skeleton** -- second most common (~29%); spawn in groups of 5; carry stone swords; rare skull drop
- **Zombified Piglin** -- present (~18%); wander in; neutral unless provoked
- **Magma Cube** -- present (~11%)
- **Skeleton** -- least common (~7%)

Mobs spawn at a higher rate if surrounding biome is soul sand valley or warped forest, as those biomes suppress general hostile spawning and push the cap into the fortress.

## Why It Matters

- **Blaze rods** drop from blazes; needed to craft brewing stands and blaze powder, which is required to activate eyes of ender and reach the End Portal
- **Wither skeleton skulls** drop rarely (~2.5% base) from wither skeletons; 3 skulls needed to summon the Wither boss
- **Nether wart** only grows naturally here (on soul sand at the staircase gardens); required for almost every potion base

Without a fortress, there is no brewing and no path to the End.

## Loot (Corridor Chests)

- Gold ingot (~47%)
- Saddle (~33%)
- Diamond (~18%), iron ingot (~18%), nether wart (~18%), golden sword (~18%), golden chestplate (~18%), flint and steel (~18%)
- Diamond horse armor (~11%)
- Rib armor trim smithing template (~7%)
- Obsidian (~8%)

## Notes

- Blaze spawner platforms can be farmed; building a simple kill chamber around the platform is a reliable blaze rod source
- Wither skeleton skulls are rare per kill -- expect to clear many wither skeletons per skull
- Fortresses can generate with fewer than the maximum number of special rooms; a fortress with no blaze spawners is possible but uncommon
- Blazes deal fire damage and at range with fireballs; fire resistance is strongly recommended before entering

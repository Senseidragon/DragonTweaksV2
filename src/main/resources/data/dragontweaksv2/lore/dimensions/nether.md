**Role:** The hostile dimension on the other side of every [Nether Portal](../structures/nether_portal.md); home to the [Nether Fortress](../structures/nether_fortress.md), Bastion Remnants, and a fixed roster of native hostile mobs found nowhere else.

## Water Does Not Work Here

There is no way to place a water source anywhere in the Nether, with one exception: a cauldron can still hold water normally. Empty a water bucket against any block and you just get a hiss of steam and an empty bucket back -- the water never appears.

Two things every traveler needs to know before stepping through a portal:
- **A water bucket cannot put out fire on you here.** If you're burning, get clear of the fire source and ride it out, or carry Fire Resistance -- there is no water trick to fall back on.
- **You cannot make obsidian here.** Pouring water next to lava is how obsidian is made in the Overworld; without placeable water, that recipe does not exist in the Nether. Bring obsidian with you if you'll need more.

## Fire and Lava

Fire here never burns itself out. Lava is far worse than in the Overworld -- it spreads about twice as far and three times as fast. Pockets of it are buried in netherrack throughout every biome, so digging blind is a real way to break into one unannounced.

## Light, Tools, and Navigation

There's no day or night -- light only comes from fire, lava, portals, glowstone, and shroomlights. Even total darkness here is brighter than a pitch-black Overworld night. Compasses spin uselessly unless bound to a lodestone, clocks just spin, and maps render as static -- none of them function normally here.

## Beds

Do not sleep here. A bed explodes instead of letting you rest.

## Biomes

- **Nether Wastes** (most common, ~36%) -- open netherrack terrain, glowstone clusters; [ghasts](../hostile/ghast.md), [zombified piglins](../neutral/zombified_piglin.md), and [magma cubes](../hostile/magma_cube.md) are common
- **Crimson Forest** (~22%) -- tall crimson fungi, weeping vines, shroomlights; home to [hoglins](../hostile/hoglin.md) and [piglins](../neutral/piglin.md)
- **Soul Sand Valley** (~17%) -- soul sand, basalt, bone fossils, soul fire under blue fog; ghasts and [skeletons](../hostile/skeleton.md) are common, [endermen](../neutral/enderman.md) rare
- **Basalt Deltas** (~16%) -- volcanic basalt and blackstone field; very high magma cube spawn rate
- **Warped Forest** (~9%, safest biome) -- endermen and striders are the only mobs that spawn here

## Structures

- **Nether Fortress** -- holds [blazes](../hostile/blaze.md) and [wither skeletons](../hostile/wither_skeleton.md); see [full entry](../structures/nether_fortress.md)
- **Bastion Remnant** -- blackstone fortification holding [piglins](../neutral/piglin.md) and [piglin brutes](../hostile/piglin_brute.md); generates in every biome except Basalt Deltas (no advisor entry yet -- known gap)
- **Nether Fossils** -- bone-block formations unique to Soul Sand Valley
- **Ruined Portal** -- a broken, half-buried portal frame with a loot chest nearby; see [full entry](../structures/ruined_portal.md)

A given region generates either a fortress or a bastion, never both.

## Native Mobs

Blazes, wither skeletons, skeletons, ghasts, hoglins, magma cubes, piglins, piglin brutes, zombified piglins, and endermen are all native here. Striders are the only fully passive Nether-native mob (no advisor entry yet -- known gap).

## Size

128 blocks tall, capped by roughly 5 layers of bedrock at both the top and the bottom -- you cannot dig or build past either.

## Other Hazards

Snow golems take rapid fire damage and die almost immediately here unless protected by Fire Resistance. Never bring one through a portal unprotected.

# Llama

Llamas are tameable neutral mobs that can carry chests for item storage and wear decorative carpets. They spit at mobs that attack them and can form caravans when leashed.

## Spawning

Llamas spawn in herds:
- **Windswept Hills, Windswept Forest, Windswept Gravelly Hills:** herds of 4-6
- **Savanna Plateau, Savanna, Windswept Savanna:** herds of 4

Four coat colors: brown, cream, white, gray. 10% of llamas spawn as babies.

## Taming

Tame by repeatedly mounting (press use while holding nothing) until hearts appear. Each ride attempt checks if a random number 0-29 is less than the llama's `Temper` value (starts at 0, max 30). On failure, Temper increases by 5. Feeding wheat (+3 Temper) or hay bales (+6 Temper) speeds taming.

Tamed llamas do not spit at mobs attacking their owner, but still spit at anything attacking the llama itself (including the owner).

## Behavior

Llamas spit when attacked, dealing 1HP (Easy/Normal) or 1.5HP (Hard) damage. Spit is not flammable.

**Caravans:** Leashing one llama causes up to 9 nearby unleashed llamas to follow it, forming a caravan of up to 10. Multiple leashed llamas can each lead their own caravan. No limit on number of caravans a player can lead.

## Storage

Tamed llamas accept a chest (press use while holding chest). Inventory size depends on strength (1-5):

| Strength | Chest Slots | Wild Spawn Chance |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 | 3 | 32.8% |
| 2 | 6 | 32.8% |
| 3 | 9 | 32.8% |
| 4 | 12 | 0.8% |
| 5 | 15 | 0.8% |

Access chest contents by sneaking + use, or opening inventory while riding. The chest cannot be retrieved without killing the llama.

## Carpets

Tamed llamas can wear wool carpets (placed in carpet slot). Each color displays a unique patterned rug. Useful for color-coding storage llamas. Carpet remains visible even under Invisibility effect.

## Feeding

| Food | Heals | Growth Speed | Temper |
|------|-------|-------------|--------|
| Wheat | 2HP | +10s | +3 |
| Hay Bale | 10HP | +1:30 min | +6, triggers love mode |

## Breeding

Breed tamed llamas with hay bales. Offspring coat color is randomly selected from parents. Offspring strength is random between 1 and the stronger parent's strength, with 3% chance of +1 (capped at 5).

Breeding a trader llama with a normal llama always produces a normal llama. Two trader llamas always produce a trader llama.

## Drops

- 1-7 XP upon successful breeding
- Leather on death

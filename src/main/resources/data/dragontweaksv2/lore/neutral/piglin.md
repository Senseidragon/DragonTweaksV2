# Piglin

Piglins are neutral Nether mobs. They are hostile to players who are not wearing at least one piece of golden armor. They can be bartered with using gold ingots, and hunt hoglins for food.

## Spawning

Piglins spawn in groups of 3-4 (Crimson Forest) or 4 (Nether Wastes) at light level 11 or below. 20% chance to spawn as a baby. Also spawn as part of Bastion Remnant generation. Each adult spawns with a weapon: golden sword (45%), crossbow (50%), or golden spear (5%). Adults may also spawn with 1-4 pieces of golden armor (each slot 10% independent).

## Behavior

**Gold armor = safety:** Adult piglins are neutral toward players wearing at least one piece of golden armor. Without it, they attack on sight (short-range aggro). Babies are always passive.

**Aggravation triggers:**
- Not wearing gold armor (light aggravation -- can be calmed by equipping gold or breaking line of sight)
- Attacking a piglin or piglin brute (heavy aggravation -- cannot be distracted by gold)
- Opening/breaking a chest, trapped chest, shulker box, barrel, ender chest, minecart with chest, or boat with chest (medium aggravation)
- Breaking a gold block, gold ore, gilded blackstone, nether gold ore, block of raw gold, or deepslate gold ore (medium aggravation)

Opening a hopper does NOT provoke piglins.

Piglins remain hostile for 30 seconds after provocation. They can be distracted by dropping gold ingots during medium/light aggravation. Heavy aggravation (from a direct hit) cannot be distracted.

**Zombification:** Piglins in the Overworld or the End zombify into Zombified Piglins after 15 seconds.

**Hoglin hunting:** Piglins occasionally attack hoglins for food. All nearby piglins join the hunt. After a hoglin kill, piglins pick up porkchops. 10% chance of a victory dance after any kill.

**Retreat:** Idle piglins flee from zombified piglins, zoglins, soul fire, soul torches, soul lanterns, and lit soul campfires.

## Bartering

Drop a gold ingot near an adult piglin or use one while looking at them. The piglin "examines" it for 6-8 seconds, then drops a random item (gravel, leather, iron boots, potions, splash potions, nether quartz, glowstone dust, magma cream, ender pearl, string, fire charge, cry ingot, iron nugget, nether brick, spectral arrow, etc.). Baby piglins take the ingot and run without giving anything.

## Combat

- Health: 16HP
- Attack (sword): varies by weapon and difficulty
- Crossbow attack: 2-5HP (Easy/Normal), 3-5HP (Hard) -- backs away from close targets
- Spear: charge attack while moving -- especially dangerous
- Cannot swim and can drown

## Drops

- 5 XP (adult or baby)
- 8.5% chance to drop each naturally held/worn equipment piece
- Always drops any picked-up items (except gold ingots, raw porkchops, cooked porkchops)

## Notes

- Baby piglins do not grow up.
- Piglins are not immune to fire or lava (unlike zombified piglins).
- The Piglin Head reduces piglins' detection range of the wearer by 50%.
- Piglins are hostile toward wither skeletons and withers without provocation.
- Piglins that spawned as part of a bastion remnant, and any piglin that has picked up an item, do not despawn naturally.
- When a piglin equips better gear it picks up, its old item drops on the ground -- players can bait a swap to recover equipment.
- Only gold-related items from the `piglin_loved` tag qualify as distractions. Non-loved items (even gold-adjacent ones) cannot distract or calm piglins.

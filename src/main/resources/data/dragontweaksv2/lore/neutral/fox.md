# Fox

Foxes are neutral mobs found in taiga biomes. Wild foxes flee from players unless approached while sneaking. A fox bred by a player will trust that player and defend them.

## Spawning

Foxes spawn in groups of 2-4 on grass, coarse dirt, podzol, snow, or snow blocks.

- **Red fox:** Taiga, Old Growth Pine Taiga, Old Growth Spruce Taiga
- **Snow fox:** Grove, Snowy Taiga

Foxes may spawn holding an item (emerald, rabbit's foot, rabbit hide, egg, wheat, leather, or feather).

## Behavior

Foxes are nocturnal -- they sleep during the day in shaded areas and become active at night. They occasionally screech at night unless a trusted player is nearby. Baby foxes follow adult foxes.

Foxes flee from wolves, polar bears, and non-trusted players unless the player sneaks. They take no damage and don't slow in sweet berry bushes.

**Attacking:** Foxes hunt chickens, rabbits, cod, salmon, tropical fish, and baby turtles. Red foxes prefer land prey; snow foxes prefer aquatic prey. Foxes pounce (up to 4 blocks high) to attack, jumping over fences if needed. Foxes can safely fall 5 blocks (vs the normal 3-block fall threshold).

**Holding items:** Foxes pick up any item off the ground, preferring food. Food is eaten after 30 seconds with applicable effects (e.g., chorus fruit teleports the fox). A held sword increases the fox's attack damage; a held totem of undying activates on fatal damage.

## Combat

- Health: 10HP
- Attack: 2HP (Easy/Normal), 3HP (Hard)
- Does not retaliate when attacked by the player
- Trusting foxes will attack mobs that harm their trusted player

## Drops

- 1-3 XP (adult killed by player or tamed wolf)
- Drops any held items with 100% chance

## Breeding

Bred with sweet berries or glow berries. Baby fox from two different-color parents has 50% chance of either color. The baby trusts the player who bred it and will not flee from them.

## Notes

- Trusting foxes will attack phantoms, zombies, and other mobs that attack their trusted player (but not wolves, polar bears, dolphins, iron golems, bees, slimes, or magma cubes).
- Trusting foxes retreat from polar bears and wolves, but not from tamed wolves. A tamed wolf whose owner attacks a trusting fox will also attack that fox.
- Foxes that have not been bred always sit while in the Nether.
- Foxes do not pick up items when `mobGriefing` is false.

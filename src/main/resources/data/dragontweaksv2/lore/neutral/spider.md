# Spider

Spiders are neutral arthropod mobs that are passive in bright light but hostile in darkness. They can climb walls and detect players through blocks. Their neutrality is light-dependent and resets if they pursue into a lit area -- but they always retaliate when attacked regardless of light.

## Spawning

Spiders spawn at light level 0 on solid opaque blocks in virtually every Overworld biome except mushroom fields and deep dark.

Additional sources: Monster Rooms (25% of spawner types), Woodland Mansion cobweb rooms, Trial Chambers (1/3 chance for melee mob slot).

**Spider jockeys:** 1% chance for a spider to spawn with a skeleton riding it. The skeleton controls movement; the spider can still climb walls but the skeleton suffocates in low ceilings. Separated by water.

## Behavior

Spiders are **hostile when the light level immediately around them is 11 or less**. If a spider chases its target into an area where the light level around the spider rises above 11, it returns to neutral. However, a spider always pursues in retaliation to an attack regardless of light level.

Spiders have a **16-block detection range** and pounce at close range.

**Wall climbing:** Spiders can climb any solid vertical surface. If a wall blocks their path to a player, they climb it to the top -- even after losing aggression, they continue moving forward blindly for 2 seconds, which means they often end up on top of walls they didn't need to climb.

**Block detection:** A pursuing spider can detect a player through solid blocks and will path toward them through walls.

Spiders **flee from armadillos** that are not in a rolled-up state.

Spiders ignore villagers and wandering traders at all light levels.

Spiders are **immune to cobweb slowing** -- they move through cobwebs at full speed. They are also immune to Poison.

**Hard difficulty status effects (Java Edition):** When spawned under regional difficulty, a spider may receive Speed (40%), Strength (20%), Regeneration (20%), or Invisibility (20%) permanently. Eyes remain visible even under Invisibility.

## Combat

- Health: 16HP
- Attack: 2HP (Easy/Normal), 3HP (Hard)
- Immune to Poison damage and cobweb slowing

## Drops

- String: 0-2
- Spider Eye: 0-1 (only when killed by player or tamed wolf)
- 5 XP when killed by player or tamed wolf

## Notes

- Spiders can climb boats, boats with chests, and shulkers.
- Cannot climb magma blocks.
- Wide hitbox (1.4 blocks) -- cannot fit through 1-block-wide horizontal gaps.
- Cobweb traps do not slow spiders -- do not rely on webs as a spider countermeasure.

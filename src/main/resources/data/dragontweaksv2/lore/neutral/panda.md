# Panda

Pandas are rare neutral mobs found in jungle biomes. They have 7 distinct personality types that affect their behavior. Adults are neutral -- they attack once when hit but do not continue pursuing.

## Spawning

Pandas spawn in groups of 1-2 on grass blocks in jungle, bamboo jungle, and sparse jungle biomes. 5% spawn as babies. Bamboo jungles have much higher panda spawn rates. Pandas do not spawn on podzol.

**Personality probability (at spawn):**
| Personality | Chance |
|-------------|--------|
| Normal | 63.7% |
| Weak | 9.8% |
| Aggressive | 6.3% |
| Lazy | 6.3% |
| Worried | 6.3% |
| Playful | 6.3% |
| Brown | 1.6% |

## Personalities

- **Normal:** Standard frown. No unique behaviors.
- **Lazy:** Smiling, slower than all other land mobs. Lies on back and ignores bamboo-holding players while lying down (Java: resumes following).
- **Worried:** Avoids players and most monsters. Shakes and covers face during thunderstorms. Won't eat bamboo or cake on their own.
- **Playful:** Tongue sticking out. Rolls and jumps as adults -- may accidentally roll off cliffs.
- **Aggressive:** Thick eyebrows, tight frown. Attacks continuously until target dies or escapes (not just once). Nearby aggressive pandas also turn hostile when any panda is attacked. Slower but long reach.
- **Weak:** Teary eyes, snotty nose. Only 10HP (half normal health). Baby weak pandas sneeze far more often (1/500 chance per tick vs 1/6000).
- **Brown:** Brown/white coloring instead of black/white. No unique behaviors.

## Behavior

Pandas follow players carrying bamboo within 16 blocks. Adult pandas seek out bamboo and cake to eat. Baby pandas occasionally roll over and jump around. They whimper during thunderstorms.

Pandas cannot be leashed normally. Unlike most neutral mobs, attacking a panda causes it to retaliate only once (except Aggressive type).

Baby pandas have a 1/6000 chance per tick to sneeze, briefly making nearby adult pandas jump. Sneezing rarely drops 1 slimeball.

## Combat

- Health: 20HP (10HP for Weak pandas)
- Attack: 2HP (Easy/Normal), 3HP (Hard); Aggressive: 4HP (Easy), 6HP (Normal), 9HP (Hard)

## Drops

- 1-7 XP upon breeding
- Bamboo on death

## Breeding

Pandas require at least one bamboo plant (not shoots) within a 7x7x3 area to breed -- feeding hay bale alone is not sufficient. Otherwise they shake their heads and refuse to breed. Breed with bamboo -- cake cannot be used for breeding even though pandas eat it. Baby panda personality is determined by genetics (dominant/recessive gene system).

## Notes

- Cannot be tamed or leashed by normal means.
- Pandas are the only source of slimeballs from sneezing (baby pandas only).

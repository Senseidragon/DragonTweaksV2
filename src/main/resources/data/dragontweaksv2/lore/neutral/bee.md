# Bee

Bees are neutral arthropod insects that live in bee nests and beehives. They are passive unless provoked -- attacking one bee or harvesting a hive without a campfire underneath will anger all nearby bees.

## Spawning

Bees nest naturally in bee nests found in trees in flower forests, plains, sunflower plains, cherry groves, meadows, mangrove swamps, and jungles. They spawn in groups of 3 per nest. A bee nest generated with an oak or birch tree has a 5% chance of spawning with bees.

## Behavior

Bees venture up to 22 blocks from their home hive searching for flowers. They return home at night or during rain. Bees pollinate flowers, carrying pollen back to their hive, which slowly increments the honey level (0-5). A hive at honey level 5 can be harvested with a bottle (honey bottle) or shears (honeycomb). Harvesting without a campfire lit within 5 blocks below the hive aggros all bees currently inside that hive.

Bees sleep inside their nest at night and will not leave during rain. They avoid water -- bees take damage in water (1HP every half second after the first second of contact).

Bees are attracted to wither roses like other flowers and will try to gather nectar from them, but doing so inflicts the Wither effect on the bee. Bees can die this way.

In the Nether and End where there is no daylight cycle, bees work continuously without resting.

## Combat

When angered, bees attack the player and any nearby players or mobs that also angered them. An angry bee will sting once, inflicting **Poison I** for 10 seconds (Normal) or 18 seconds (Hard). After stinging, the bee loses its stinger and dies within 60 seconds. Baby bees cannot sting. Bees deal 2HP on Easy/Normal and 3HP on Hard.

Group aggro is triggered by:
- Attacking any bee in the swarm (unless killed in one hit)
- Harvesting a hive/nest without a calming campfire below it
- Destroying a beehive (even an empty one that was just placed)

Bees also swarm any mob that hits them -- if a skeleton accidentally shoots a bee, the bees attack the skeleton.

If a player deflects a bee's sting with a shield, the bee continues attacking until it lands a hit. A bee that fails to hit its target for 20-39 seconds returns to neutral on its own.

## Drops

- Up to 3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf
- No item drops

## Breeding

Adult bees can be bred with any flower. After breeding, bees have a 5-minute cooldown. Baby bees take 20 minutes to mature; feeding flowers speeds growth by 10% per flower. Baby bees can follow adult bees.

## Notes

- Placing a Silk Touch tool on a bee nest/beehive preserves any bees inside when the block is broken.
- Bees become permanently hostile to a player who destroys their hive without calming them first.
- Campfire smoke (within 5 blocks below) prevents harvest aggro.
- Bees can enter and exit through any face of their hive except the bottom.

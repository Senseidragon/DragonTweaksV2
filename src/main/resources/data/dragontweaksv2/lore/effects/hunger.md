# Hunger

The hunger bar has 20 points (displayed as 10 drumstick icons). Maintaining food is essential — below half hunger (9 or fewer points) you cannot sprint, and at zero you take starvation damage (0.5 HP per 4 seconds on Normal, lethal on Hard). Cooked meats, bread, and golden carrots are the most efficient foods. Rotten flesh from zombies is edible but causes the Hunger effect which drains the food bar faster — only eat it in emergencies. Chorus Fruit teleports you randomly but restores some food. Always carry food in your hotbar.

## Food Efficiency

High-saturation foods delay hunger drain: golden carrots (6 food, 14.4 saturation), cooked porkchop (8 food, 12.8 saturation), cooked beef (8 food, 12.8 saturation). Bread (5 food, 6.0 saturation) is cheap and reliable early-game.

## Starvation

When food bar is at zero, starvation damage begins. On Easy it stops at 10 HP. On Normal it stops at 1 HP. On Hard it continues until death. Sprint is disabled at 6 food or below.

# Fire and Lava

Fire and lava both deal continuous damage while in contact. Lava deals more damage per tick (4 HP every 0.5 sec) than fire (1 HP per second). After leaving a fire or lava source you continue burning briefly.

## Fire Resistance

Fire Resistance potions provide complete immunity to fire, lava, and magma block damage. They are essential for Nether exploration. Brewed from Magma Cream + Awkward Potion. Duration: 3 minutes base, 8 minutes extended. Fire Resistance (Splash) protects mobs too, including the Wither boss.

## Extinguishing

- **Water** extinguishes fire on your body instantly.
- Rain also extinguishes burning.
- Stepping into a water source removes fire immediately.

## Netherite

Netherite items and armor do not burn in lava (they float and are fireproof as items). However, **you still take lava damage** wearing Netherite armor without Fire Resistance — the armor is fireproof, not you.

## Practical Notes

Keep a Fire Resistance potion when exploring the Nether. Lava lakes generate frequently in the Nether and Overworld underground. Ghast fireballs set terrain on fire; netherrack burns indefinitely once lit.

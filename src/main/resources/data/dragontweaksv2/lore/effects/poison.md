# Poison

Poison is a status effect that drains health over time but cannot kill you — it stops at 1 HP. Damage ticks every 1.25 seconds (Poison I) or every 0.6 seconds (Poison II). Sources include cave spiders (Normal: 7 sec, Hard: 15 sec), witches (Splash Potion of Poison, up to 45 sec), and certain foods like spider eyes and suspicious stew with the wrong ingredient.

## Cures

- **Milk** cures all status effects including Poison immediately and is the most accessible cure.
- **Honey Bottles** specifically cure Poison and stack to 16. Honey Bottles do NOT cure other effects.
- Waiting out Poison is safe if you have more than 1 HP — it will not kill you.

## Prevention

The Turtle Master potion provides resistance but not Poison immunity. Fire Resistance potions do not help with Poison. Being on Hard difficulty means cave spider Poison lasts 15 seconds instead of 7 — prioritize killing them before they bite.

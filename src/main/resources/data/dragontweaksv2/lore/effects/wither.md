# Wither Effect

The Wither status effect deals damage over time and, unlike Poison, CAN kill you — it has no health floor. It also turns the health bar black, making your current HP difficult to read. Damage ticks every 2 seconds (Wither I) or every 1 second (Wither II).

## Sources

- **Wither Skeletons** inflict Wither I for 10 seconds on melee hit.
- **The Wither boss** projectiles inflict Wither II.
- Certain potions and arrows of decay.

## Cures

- **Milk** cures Wither immediately and is the only reliable field cure.
- There is no potion that cures Wither directly. Milk is the primary counter.
- Keep your health high when fighting Wither Skeletons — the combined melee damage plus Wither ticks can kill quickly.

## Nether Fortress Caution

Wither Skeletons spawn in Nether Fortresses in groups. Engaging multiple at once in tight corridors is extremely dangerous. Fight in doorways to limit how many can reach you simultaneously.

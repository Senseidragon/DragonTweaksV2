# Mooshroom

A mushroom-covered cow variant exclusive to mushroom fields biomes. Significant renewable resource: provides beef, leather, milk, mushroom stew, and the brown variant produces suspicious stew on demand.

## Spawning

Red mooshrooms spawn in herds of 4-8 on mycelium blocks in mushroom fields only (100% spawn weight). 5% chance to spawn as a baby. Brown mooshrooms **never spawn naturally** -- created only by striking a red mooshroom with lightning (and vice versa).

## Stats

- 10 HP (5 hearts)
- Passive

## Behavior and Resources

- **Milk:** Right-click with a bucket for a milk bucket (infinite, no cooldown).
- **Mushroom stew:** Right-click with a bowl to get mushroom stew directly (renewable food).
- **Shearing:** Shearing a red mooshroom drops 5 red mushrooms and converts it to a regular cow. Shearing a brown mooshroom drops 5 brown mushrooms and converts it to a regular cow.
- **Suspicious stew (brown only):** Feed a small flower to a brown mooshroom; its next bowl-milking produces suspicious stew with an effect based on the flower. Key effects: Regeneration (oxeye daisy), Fire Resistance (allium), Jump Boost (cornflower), Night Vision (poppy/torchflower), Saturation (blue orchid/dandelion), Poison (lily of the valley), Wither (wither rose). Returns to normal mushroom stew until fed another flower.
- Baby mooshrooms have no mushrooms on their back -- mushrooms only appear once they grow to adult.

## Breeding

Breed with wheat. Cannot breed with regular cows. Two red mooshrooms have a 1/1024 chance to produce a brown baby; red x brown produces 50/50 variant split. Baby growth time cut 10% per wheat feeding.

## Drops

- 1-3 raw beef (steak if killed by fire)
- 0-2 leather
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf
- 1-7 XP on successful breeding

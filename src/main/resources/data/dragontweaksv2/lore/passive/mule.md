# Mule

A passive rideable mob created exclusively by breeding a horse with a donkey. Mules cannot breed. They function identically to donkeys for item transport but may have better stats if bred from quality parents.

## Spawning

Mules do not spawn naturally. Produced only by cross-breeding a tamed horse with a tamed donkey.

## Stats

- **Health:** 15-30 HP (tends toward average ~22-23)
- **Speed:** Fixed at 0.175 for spawned mules; bred mules inherit averaged horse/donkey speed (0.1125-0.3375)
- **Jump strength:** Fixed at 0.5 (clears ~1.5 blocks) for spawned mules; bred mules can range 0.4-1.0
- Passive

## Taming

Same mechanic as donkeys: mount repeatedly with empty hand. Temper starts at 0/100; each mount attempt: random 0-99 < temper - tamed, else temper +5 and bucked off. Feeding raises temper. Baby mules cannot be tamed until grown.

## Equipment and Usage

- **Saddle:** Required for riding; removable with shears.
- **Chest:** Provides 15 inventory slots. Cannot be removed without killing the mule; chest and contents drop on death.
- Cannot wear horse armor.
- Baby mules cannot be equipped with anything.
- Rider clearance: 2.75 blocks minimum. Cannot fit through 1-block-wide gaps.
- Floats on water when ridden.
- Not affected by Jump Boost beacons or potions.

## Healing

Hay bale restores 20 HP. Golden apple restores 10 HP. Wheat restores 2 HP.

## Drops

- Leather
- Saddle and chest contents if equipped
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf

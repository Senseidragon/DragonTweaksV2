# Parrot

A tameable passive mob found in jungle biomes. Primary utility: passive early-warning system -- imitates the sounds of nearby hostile mobs within 20 blocks.

## Spawning

Spawns in groups of 1-2 in jungle, bamboo jungle, and sparse jungle biomes above logs, leaves, grass, or air. Cannot be bred.

## Stats

- 6 HP (3 hearts)
- Passive

## Taming

Feed any seed type (wheat, beetroot, melon, pumpkin, torchflower, pitcher pod) until heart particles appear. Each seed has a chance to tame.

**Cookies are toxic and kill parrots instantly** -- do not feed.

## Behavior

**Hostile mob detection:** Parrots imitate the idle sound of nearby hostile mobs within 20 blocks at a higher pitch (matching baby pitch). Provides an auditory alert before the mob is visible. Imitated mobs include: Blaze, Breeze, Cave Spider, Creeper, Drowned, Elder Guardian, Enderman, Evoker, Ghast, Guardian, Hoglin, Husk, Magma Cube, Phantom, Piglin, Piglin Brute, Pillager, Ravager, Shulker, Silverfish, Skeleton, Slime, Spider, Stray, Vex, Vindicator, Warden, Witch, Wither, Wither Skeleton, Zombie, Zombie Villager, Zombified Piglin, and others. May occasionally imitate mobs not currently nearby.

**Shoulder perching:** A tamed parrot walks into the player to perch on their shoulder (left preferred). Up to one per shoulder. Parrot dismounts when the player: steps up -- block, drops off a ledge >- block, takes damage, touches water or powder snow, enters creative flight, uses an elytra, sleeps on a bed, or submerges their head in lava -- **the parrot dismounts and burns even if the player has Fire Resistance**. Parrots teleport with the player on ender pearl use.

**Dancing:** Parrots dance within 3 blocks of a jukebox playing a music disc, including while on a shoulder.

## Drops

- 1-2 feathers
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf

# Chicken

A passive mob found in most grassy biomes and the primary source of raw chicken, feathers, and eggs. Chickens are the **only Overworld mob that can repopulate without breeding** -- adult chickens lay eggs automatically, which can hatch new chicks.

## Spawning

Spawns in groups of 4 on grass blocks in most grassy biomes. Does not spawn in snowy plains, meadows, cherry groves, mangrove swamps, or pale gardens. 5% of spawns are baby chicks. More common in sparse jungles.

Baby zombie variants (and baby zombified piglins) have a 5% chance to spawn riding a nearby chicken as a jockey; if no chicken is present, an additional 5% chance spawns one on a new adult chicken. Chicken jockeys can appear in the Nether via zombified piglins.

## Stats

- 4 HP (2 hearts)
- Passive

## Behavior

Chickens wander aimlessly and follow players holding any seed type (wheat, beetroot, melon, pumpkin, torchflower, pitcher pod) within 10 blocks.

Adult chickens lay an egg every 5-10 minutes automatically. A thrown egg has a 1/8 chance to spawn a chick; if a chick spawns, there is a further 1/32 chance to spawn three additional chicks (four total). Eggs can be loaded into and fired from dispensers -- automated chicken farms are possible. Throwing an egg at a glass pane can spawn the chick on the far side of the pane.

Three cosmetic variants (temperate, cold, warm) lay different egg types: temperate lay regular eggs, warm lay brown eggs, cold lay blue eggs. No behavioral differences between variants.

Chicks are smaller than a half-block and can slip through sub-full-block openings unintentionally (but will not actively walk into half-block-tall gaps). A chick inside a minecart cannot be hit -- it is fully within the minecart's hitbox.

## Drops

- 1-2 raw chicken (cooked chicken if killed by fire)
- 0-2 feathers
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf
- No drops from baby chicks

## Breeding

Breed with any seed type. Both parents enter a 5-minute cooldown after breeding. Baby chicks take 20 minutes to mature; seeds accelerate growth by 10% per feeding. Offspring inherit the variant of one parent regardless of biome.

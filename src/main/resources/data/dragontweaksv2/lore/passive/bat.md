# Bat

A purely atmospheric flying mob with no combat role, no drops, and no XP. Bats exist solely as ambience and are the only mob in the ambient mob cap category -- they do not compete with passive or hostile mob spawning limits.

## Spawning

Spawns in groups of 8 anywhere in the Overworld (except the deep dark) at light level 3 or below, on stone, granite, diorite, andesite, tuff, or deepslate that is not directly sky-exposed. Requires at least 1.5 blocks of air above the spawn point. Spawns at any y-level.

## Behavior

Bats fly aimlessly and squeak. When idle, they hang upside down from the underside of solid blocks -- they cannot hang from transparent or non-solid surfaces. A hanging bat flies away if any player approaches, **even an invisible one**. Breaking the block it hangs from also disturbs it.

Bats despawn beyond 32 blocks from the player. They can fly into lava and die. They can be slowed by cobwebs. They cannot be leashed, placed in boats, or transported by any conventional means. They cannot breed and have no baby form.

## Drops

- Nothing -- no items, no XP

## Notes

- Bats do not count toward the passive mob cap; they have their own ambient cap.
- Invisibility does not prevent disturbing a hanging bat.

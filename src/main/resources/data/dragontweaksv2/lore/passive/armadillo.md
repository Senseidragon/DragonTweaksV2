# Armadillo

A passive mob found in badlands and savanna biomes. Armadillos are the only source of armadillo scutes, which are used to craft wolf armor. They roll up when threatened, reducing incoming damage and passively repelling spiders.

## Spawning

Armadillos spawn in savannas, savanna plateaus, and windswept savannas (groups of 2-3) and in badlands, eroded badlands, and wooded badlands (groups of 1-2). Light level 9 or higher required. They do not spawn in other biomes.

## Stats

- 12 HP (6 hearts)
- Passive

## Behavior

When standing, armadillos passively repel spiders and cave spiders -- any spider within 6 blocks flees from them. This repelling effect is disabled while rolled up.

### Rolling Up

An armadillo rolls up when:
- It takes damage
- An undead mob comes near
- A player is sprinting or mounted nearby

While rolled up, it takes reduced damage: `(damage - 1) / 2`. It cannot walk, eat, or be bred while rolled up. It occasionally peeks out to check for threats and unrolls if no threat is detected for 3 seconds.

Immediate unroll is forced by: water contact or being attached to a lead.

The armadillo does **not** roll up while fleeing, in water, in the air, or being led -- but it **does** roll up on campfires (being on fire and being on a campfire are treated differently).

## Drops

- 1 armadillo scute every 5-10 minutes (passively shed, similar to chicken egg timing)
- 1 armadillo scute when brushed with a brush (costs 16 durability per scute; an unenchanted brush yields 4-5 scutes before breaking)

## Breeding

Bred with spider eyes. Armadillos follow players holding spider eyes within 10 blocks. After breeding, 1-7 XP is dropped and a 5-minute cooldown applies. Baby armadillos take 20 minutes to mature; spider eyes accelerate growth by 10% each.

An armadillo loses its willingness to breed if it rolls up after being fed but before reaching its mate -- it must be fed again to become willing.

## Notes

- Wolf armor is crafted from armadillo scutes -- the primary reason to seek these mobs out.
- A lead will force an armadillo to unroll immediately, which can be useful for resuming breeding or item collection.
- Spider repelling only functions when the armadillo is standing, not rolled up.

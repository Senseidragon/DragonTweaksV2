# Pig

A passive mob found in most grassy biomes. Food source and rideable with saddle + carrot on a stick. Three cosmetic variants (temperate, cold, warm) with no behavioral differences.

## Spawning

Spawns in groups of 4 on grass blocks in most Overworld grassy biomes. Does not spawn in snowy plains, meadows, mangrove swamps, or pale gardens. May spawn in village animal pens, stables, and butcher house backyards at world generation. Pigs never spawn with saddles.

## Stats

- 10 HP (5 hearts)
- Passive

## Behavior

Wanders aimlessly. Follows players holding carrots, potatoes, or beetroot within 10 blocks.

**Riding:** Apply a saddle to an adult pig (baby pigs cannot be saddled). Saddle is retrievable with shears. With a carrot on a stick, the pig is controllable at 2.42 blocks/sec. Using the carrot triggers a boost (costs 7 durability) peaking at 5.2 blocks/sec; average during boost ~4.19 m/s. Pigs cannot float on water or lava when ridden but can be controlled through 1-block-deep water. Fit through 1-block-high gaps.

**Lightning:** A pig struck by lightning transforms into a zombified piglin (saddle is lost). The player remains mounted if riding it.

## Breeding

Breed with carrots, potatoes, or beetroot. 5-minute breeding cooldown. Baby pigs take 20 minutes to mature; each food feeding cuts remaining growth by 10%. Golden dandelion freezes baby pig growth and resets the timer; a second feeding resumes growth.

## Drops

- 1-3 raw porkchop (cooked porkchop if killed by fire)
- 1-3 XP when killed by a player or tamed wolf
- 1-7 XP on successful breeding

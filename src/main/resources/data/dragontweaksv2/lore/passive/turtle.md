# Turtle

A **turtle** is a passive aquatic mob found on beaches. It has the highest HP of any passive mob (30 HP) and is the only source of turtle scutes, which drop when baby turtles mature into adults and can be crafted into a Turtle Shell helmet.

## Spawning

Turtles spawn in groups of 2-5 on sand near beaches (Y-level -4 above sea level). They do not spawn on snowy beaches or stony shores. 5% chance to spawn as babies.

## Stats

- **Health:** 30 HP (very tanky for a passive mob)
- **Size:** Adult 1.2 x 0.4 blocks (very flat); baby 0.36 x 0.12 blocks (smallest mobs in game)

## Behavior

Turtles move slowly on land but swim quickly. On land they move toward the nearest water source at least 2 blocks deep. They cannot be attached to leads but follow players holding seagrass within 10 blocks.

**Home position:** Each turtle permanently remembers its spawn block as its home. After breeding, the pregnant turtle always returns to its home beach to lay eggs, regardless of distance.

**Lightning:** Turtles take essentially instant death damage from lightning strikes.

## Breeding and Eggs

Feed seagrass to two turtles. One becomes pregnant (slightly taller). The pregnant turtle returns to its home beach, digs, and lays 1-4 eggs in a cluster. Cooldown: 90 seconds.

Turtle eggs hatch over 4-5 in-game days (faster at night). Eggs crack 3 times before hatching. Eggs can be placed on any block but only hatch on sand, red sand, or suspicious sand. Eggs can be harvested with Silk Touch.

**Egg predators (targeted deliberately):** Zombies, husks, drowned, and zombie villagers trample eggs and kill babies (zombie detection range for eggs: 47x7x47 blocks). Zombified piglins target eggs only. Baby turtles are also hunted by skeletons, bogged, strays, wither skeletons, foxes, stray cats, and wild wolves.

Wither, Warden, Vindicator named "Johnny", and Zoglin attack both babies and adults. Goats attack babies and adults randomly. Tamed wolves only attack when their owner does.

**Baby turtles do not flee from stray cats** even while being attacked -- they make no attempt to escape.

**Silk Touch relocation:** Harvesting eggs with Silk Touch and placing them elsewhere causes hatchlings to imprint on the new location as their home beach. This is the only reliable way to establish a turtle farm away from where the turtles originally spawned.

**Own eggs:** Turtles can walk on their own eggs without breaking them. Other living entities that fall or walk on eggs trample and break them one at a time.

**Baby growth:** Takes 20 minutes (1 Minecraft day). Seagrass accelerates growth by 10% per feeding. A golden dandelion pauses growth; a second resumes it.

## Drops

**Adult death:** Seagrass (0-1), Bowl (0-1)

**Baby maturing to adult:** 1 Turtle Scute (the primary reason to breed turtles)

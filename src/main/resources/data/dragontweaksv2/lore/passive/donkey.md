# Donkey

A passive mob that can be tamed, saddled, ridden, and equipped with a chest for item transport. All naturally spawned donkeys have fixed stats unlike horses.

## Spawning

Donkeys spawn in meadows (20% chance, groups of 1-2), plains and sunflower plains (~2%, groups of 1-3), and savannas (~1.5-2%, groups of 1).

## Stats

- **Health:** 15-30 HP (average ~22.5); varies per individual
- **Speed:** Fixed at 0.175 (7.56 m/s) for all naturally spawned donkeys
- **Jump strength:** Fixed at 0.5, clearing approximately 1.5 blocks
- Passive

Bred donkeys can inherit varied speed (0.1125-0.3375) and jump strength (0.4-1.0) from parent averages.

## Taming

Mount the donkey repeatedly. Starts with 0 temper (out of 100). Each mount attempt picks a random 0-99 value; if it's less than current temper, the donkey is tamed; otherwise temper increases by 5 and the player is bucked off. Feeding also raises temper. Baby donkeys cannot be tamed until they grow into adults.

## Equipment and Usage

- **Saddle:** Required for riding. Can be removed with shears.
- **Chest:** Provides 15 inventory slots. Cannot be removed without killing the donkey -- chest and all contents drop on death.
- Donkeys cannot wear horse armor.
- Cannot fit through 1-block-wide gaps; rider risks suffocation below ~2.75-block clearance.
- Cannot use nether or end portals while riding.
- Automatically steps up 1-block slopes while ridden.

## Healing

Hay bale restores 20 HP. Golden apple restores 10 HP. Wheat restores 2 HP. Feed food directly to heal or speed up baby growth.

## Breeding

Tamed donkeys breed with golden apples or golden carrots. Cross-breeding a donkey with a horse produces a mule; mules cannot breed.

## Drops

- Leather
- Saddle and chest contents drop if equipped
- 1-7 XP on successful breeding

# Frog

A passive mob found in swamps and mangrove swamps. Jumps, swims, and hunts small slimes and small magma cubes using its tongue. Passive to players and cannot harm them.

## Spawning

Spawns in groups of 2-5. Mangrove swamp spawns are guaranteed (100% weight); swamp spawns are 20% weight. Cold frogs can appear near swamps bordering cold biomes; a tadpole grown in a cold biome becomes a cold frog.

## Stats

- 10 HP (5 hearts)
- Passive (to players); attacks small slimes and small magma cubes
- Tongue attack is instant kill, ignores target health

## Variants

Three variants with different froglight outputs:
- **Temperate** (swamp): drops Ochre Froglight
- **Warm** (mangrove swamp): drops Pearlescent Froglight
- **Cold** (cold biome border/growth): drops Verdant Froglight

Variant is determined by the biome the **tadpole grows up in**, not parental variant -- a unique mechanic. Tadpoles are treated as a completely separate mob from frogs.

## Behavior

Frogs jump frequently and swim faster than they move on land. They stay near water surfaces and do not drown. They occasionally croak and inflate their vocal sac.

Frogs use their tongue to instantly eat (despawn) small slimes and small magma cubes -- no death animation. Eating a small slime drops a slimeball; eating a small magma cube drops a froglight of the frog's color. Frogs are **immune to all damage from slimes and magma cubes** regardless of size.

## Breeding

Frogs follow players holding a slimeball within 6 blocks. Feeding two frogs slimeballs causes one to become pregnant; it seeks a water block with air above to lay frogspawn. After 3-10 minutes, frogspawn hatches into 2-6 tadpoles. Tadpoles take 20 minutes to grow into frogs; the biome of growth (not parents) determines their variant.

## Drops

- No drops on death
- 1-7 XP on successful breeding

# Magma Cube

A Nether version of the slime with 3 natural sizes. Key differences from slimes: ALL sizes including small deal damage, all sizes have significant armor, fire immune, and small magma cubes can be eaten by frogs to produce froglight. Splits into smaller sizes on death. Found in basalt deltas (dominant), Nether fortresses, and Nether wastes.

## Sizes and Stats

| Size | HP | Armor | Attack (Easy/Normal/Hard) |
|------|----|-------|--------------------------|
| Large | 16 HP | 12 | 4 / 6 / 9 HP |
| Medium | 4 HP | 6 | 3 / 4 / 6 HP |
| Small | 1 HP | 3 | 2.5 / 3 / 4.5 HP |

On death: large splits into 2-4 medium, medium into 2-4 small, small drops magma cream and XP.

Armor formula: size x 3. HP formula: size squared. Attack: size + 2.

## Spawning

Basalt deltas: dominant, groups of 2-5. Nether fortresses: 10.7% chance. Nether wastes: rare (1.2%). All light levels in the Nether. Require a 2.08x2.08x2.08 clear space (same check as large magma cube regardless of final size -- anything blocking the 2.08 cube prevents any size from spawning).

Monster spawners in treasure bastion remnant rooms also produce magma cubes.

Regional difficulty affects size distribution: ranges from 33% each at low to 50% large / 33% medium / 16% small at high difficulty.

## Behavior

- Attacks by jumping and landing on targets; deals damage on contact
- Damage applies continuously to any entity in contact (~twice per second in practice)
- Large magma cubes can deal up to 12 HP/sec on Normal difficulty due to contact damage
- Fire immune -- can spawn and walk on magma blocks
- Jumps higher than slimes: large jumps 4 blocks high, medium 2 blocks, small 1 block
- **Froglight source**: a frog that eats a small magma cube produces a froglight (color varies by frog type)

## Drops

- Magma cream (small size only, 0-1)

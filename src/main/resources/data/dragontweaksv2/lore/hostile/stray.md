# Stray

A skeleton variant found in snowy biomes with 20 HP. Identical to the skeleton in combat behavior except for two traits: strays fire Slowness II tipped arrows instead of normal arrows, and they do NOT freeze in powder snow. Spawns replacing 80% of skeletons in snowy plains, ice spikes, frozen peaks, and other cold biomes.

## Stats

- Health: 20 HP
- Attack (bow): Easy 2-4 HP, Normal 3-5 HP, Hard 4-8 HP
- All arrows inflict Slowness II for 30 seconds

## Spawning

Replaces 80% of skeletons in snowy plains and ice spikes. Also spawns in frozen rivers, frozen/deep frozen oceans, snowy slopes, jagged peaks, and frozen peaks. Does not spawn from monster spawners (only natural spawning). 1/3 chance selected as "ranged" mob for trial chambers.

**Conversion source**: A regular skeleton kept inside powder snow for 7 seconds begins shaking; after another 15 seconds it becomes a stray. Converted strays retain any gear from the original skeleton and spawn with full health.

## Behavior

Same as skeleton: shoots at targets within 15 blocks, strafes and retreats to 4 blocks minimum, flees wolves (turns to shoot if attacked by wolf), arrows provoke neutral mobs against the stray. Strays do NOT freeze in powder snow -- they are immune to the conversion that creates them.

## Drops

- Bone
- Arrow (regular arrow -- the Slowness effect is not carried by dropped arrows)

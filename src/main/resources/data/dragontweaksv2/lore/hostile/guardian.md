# Guardian

An aquatic hostile mob with 30 HP found exclusively in ocean monuments. Guardians attack with a 4-second laser that cannot be dodged and deal retaliatory spike damage when hit in melee. They target players, axolotls, squid, and glow squid but do NOT retaliate against other mobs that attack them.

## Stats

- Health: 30 HP
- Attack (laser): Easy 4 HP, Normal 6 HP + 2 HP magic, Hard 9 HP + 3-5 HP magic
- Attack (spikes): Easy/Normal 2 HP, Hard 3 HP (triggered when hit by melee while spikes extended)
- Laser range: 15 blocks
- Laser charge time: 4 seconds

## Spawning

Ocean monuments only. Require water (flowing or stationary). On Java, spawn attempts mostly fail in open-sky water above sea level (95% fail rate in exposed columns) -- guardians naturally concentrate inside and under the monument.

## Behavior

Guardians swim in abrupt charges, retracting their spikes while moving. While stationary they sink slowly with spikes extended and quivering. They congregate loosely around sea lantern blocks.

When out of water, guardians do not suffocate and survive indefinitely -- they squeak loudly and hop erratically.

### Laser Attack

Guardian laser starts purple and fades to yellow over 4 seconds. When fully charged it flashes green, then fires. The laser:
- Cannot be dodged -- target must escape range or break line of sight
- A shield reduces damage by 50%
- Blocked by solid blocks including glass (any solid block breaks line of sight)
- Maximum range: 15 blocks

After firing, the guardian swims for 3 seconds before targeting again. If the target approaches while charging, the guardian retreats to comfortable range and resumes charging.

### Spike Defense

When hit by melee while spikes are extended (i.e., not swimming), the guardian deals retaliatory damage. At point-blank range, guardians extend spikes and laser simultaneously.

### Target Limitations

Guardians target players, axolotls, squid, and glow squid. They do NOT retaliate against mobs (e.g., iron golems) that attack them -- only the spike thorns apply passively.

Guardians do NOT follow players who move out of sight -- they simply resume swimming until line of sight is regained.

## Invisibility Exploit

A guardian's eye follows any nearby player. A fully invisible player (Invisibility potion + no armor pieces) is completely undetected -- the guardian cannot see them and will not laser them. Wearing even one piece of armor while invisible makes the player visible to guardians again.

## Drops

- Prismarine shards (primary)
- Prismarine crystals
- Raw cod (sometimes cooked if on fire when killed)
- 10 XP per kill

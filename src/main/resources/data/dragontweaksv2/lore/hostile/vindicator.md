# Vindicator

A melee illager with 24 HP and an iron axe. One of the hardest-hitting common hostile mobs -- 13 HP on Normal, 19.5 HP on Hard -- with a sprint speed that makes it dangerous in open terrain. Found in woodland mansions and raids.

## Stats

- Health: 24 HP
- Attack (iron axe): Easy 7.5 HP, Normal 13 HP, Hard 19.5 HP
- Attack (unarmed): Easy 3.5 HP, Normal 5 HP, Hard 7.5 HP
- Speed: 5.612 blocks/sec (can only sprint; always at sprint speed)
- Shield break: axe attack disables player's shield for 5 seconds (even when vindicator is mounted on a ravager)

## Spawning

**Woodland mansions**: Groups of 1-3 in specific rooms, sometimes with an evoker. All 3 floors. Do not respawn after initial spawn. Mansion-spawned vindicators do not despawn.

Mansion vindicators are sometimes neutral on first spawn -- they don't attack until provoked. This is not guaranteed.

**Raids**: Appear in later waves with higher frequency. Raid vindicators may spawn with Sharpness I (or Sharpness II in waves 6+) on their axe. Can spawn riding a ravager.

**Patrols**: Vindicators can join patrols if near a patrol captain, but do not naturally spawn in patrols or seek them out.

## Behavior

Vindicators sprint at all times, even when idle. They charge toward targets and attack with their axe.

When attacked by a mob (except illagers and goats, or if killed in one hit), they call for help from other nearby vindicators. They do NOT call other illager types.

Raid vindicators can open wooden and copper doors. They can also break doors (like zombies on Hard).

Bell ringing within 32 blocks gives all vindicators the Glowing effect for 3 seconds.

## "Johnny" Behavior

A vindicator named "Johnny" (via name tag) becomes universally hostile -- it attacks every mob except agents, evokers, ghasts, illusioners, other vindicators, and pillagers. This includes baby villagers, creepers, ravagers, vexes, witches, and iron golems. Phantoms and the ender dragon are excluded only because they target only players. Goats are excluded (though they may still ram it). Slimes, magma cubes, ravagers, guardians, and elder guardians will not retaliate.

A "Johnny" vindicator also targets ravagers, and if the ravager has no other target, the ravager attacks itself until dead.

The "Johnny" tag persists through renaming and is not the same as the name tag text -- it is a separate internal flag.

## Drops

- Emerald (rare), enchanted book (rare), iron tools/armor (rare)
- Naturally-spawned axe: 8.5% drop chance

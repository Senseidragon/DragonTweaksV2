# Zombie Villager

A zombie variant with 20 HP that retains the profession and appearance of the villager it came from. The defining feature: zombie villagers can be cured back into living villagers, yielding permanent trading discounts. They do NOT convert to drowned when submerged.

## Stats

- Health: 20 HP (leaders: 40-100 HP on Hard)
- Armor: 2 points
- Attack: Easy 2.5 HP, Normal 3 HP, Hard 4.5 HP

## Spawning

5% of naturally spawned zombies in most biomes; 20% in old growth pine taiga. Also appear in abandoned villages (persistent, no despawn) and igloo basements (persistent, locked in cages). On Hard: reinforcements (0-10% per hit; leaders get 50-75% boost). Not spawned in zombie sieges.

If a zombie (husk, drowned, zombified piglin, or zombie villager) kills a villager: Easy 0%, Normal 50%, Hard 100% chance of conversion to zombie villager. A converted zombie villager does not despawn if the player had traded with the original villager.

## Curing

1. Apply Weakness to the zombie villager (splash/lingering potion, arrow of Weakness, witch, etc.)
2. Feed it a (non-enchanted) golden apple while it has Weakness
3. Wait 3-5 minutes (180-300 sec) -- zombie villager shudders and gains Strength during this time

**Caution**: The zombie villager gains Strength during curing and is more dangerous than normal.

**Accelerants**: Iron bars and beds within a 9x9x9 cube centered on the zombie villager speed up the cure. Up to 14 individual objects are checked; each has a 30% chance per tick (1% check rate) to reduce the countdown. Maximum average speedup is ~4.2% with 14+ blocks.

**Result**: Villager recovers its former profession and all prior trades. The player who administered the cure receives permanent trading discounts. Nearby villagers also receive smaller discounts. If it was a baby, it cures back into a baby villager.

## Behavior

Behaves identically to a zombie: hostile to players, villagers, iron golems, baby turtles, wandering traders. Unlike zombies, zombie villagers do NOT convert to drowned when submerged.

Spear-wielding zombie villagers back away from their target to maintain range for charge attacks.

## Drops

- Rotten flesh
- Naturally-spawned equipment (8.5% drop, +1% per Looting)

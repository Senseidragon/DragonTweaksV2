# Elder Guardian

The largest aquatic mob in the game with 80 HP. Three spawn inside each ocean monument -- one in the top room, one in each wing -- and they do NOT respawn. Unlike regular guardians they do not retreat from approaching players. The defining threat is Mining Fatigue III, which they inflict on all players within 50 blocks every minute.

## Stats

- Health: 80 HP
- Laser: Easy 5 HP, Normal 8 HP + 4 HP magic, Hard 12 HP + up to 8 HP magic
- Spikes: Easy/Normal 2 HP, Hard 3 HP (passive retaliation when struck)
- XP: 10 per kill

## Mining Fatigue Curse

Immediately upon spawning and once per minute afterward, the elder guardian afflicts every player within a 50-block sphere with Mining Fatigue III for 5 minutes. The effect:
- -30% attack speed
- Drastically reduced mining speed
- Passes through solid blocks -- underground players are not safe
- A potion of Invisibility provides no defense
- Player sees a ghostly elder guardian image and hears a bell sound when afflicted

This is not classified as an attack and does not trigger the Thorns enchantment.

## Laser Attack

Charges over 3 seconds (purple -- yellow -- green flash on firing). Once fired:
- Cannot be dodged -- player must escape range or break line of sight
- A shield stops the beam entirely
- Any solid block (including glass) breaks line of sight
- Maximum range: ~14 blocks

The elder guardian does NOT swim away when a player closes in, unlike a regular guardian.

## Spikes Defense

When struck while spikes are extended (not swimming), the elder guardian deals retaliatory damage. If a player or axolotl corners it, it extends spikes and fires the laser simultaneously at point-blank range.

## Behavior

- Pathfinds back toward the monument if displaced outside it
- Attacks players, squid, glow squid, and axolotls (unless the axolotl is playing dead)
- Does NOT retaliate against mobs that attack it -- only passive spike damage applies
- Its eye follows any nearby player even through Invisibility or Spectator mode (though it does not attack Spectator players)
- Does not suffocate out of water

## Drops

- Wet sponge (always, 1)
- Tide armor trim (20%)
- Prismarine shards, prismarine crystals, raw cod (variable)

# Vex

A small flying hostile mob summoned by evokers with 14 HP. The defining traits: vexes can pass through any solid block (including bedrock), water, and lava without damage or slowing, and they typically attack from behind, making shield blocks unreliable. Iron sword does not drop on death.

## Stats

- Health: 14 HP
- Armed (iron sword): Easy 5.5 HP, Normal 9 HP, Hard 13.5 HP
- Unarmed: Easy 3 HP, Normal 4 HP, Hard 6 HP

## Spawning

Summoned only by evokers. Each summon produces 3 vexes near the evoker. The evoker can resummon even if some vexes from the previous wave are still alive. Blocks at the spawn point prevent vexes from appearing at that location.

## Behavior

- Phases through all solid blocks, water, lava, and bedrock -- cannot be walled off
- Cobwebs do not slow vexes; honey blocks DO slow them
- Attacks from behind, making it hard to block with a shield
- Glows red while attacking and lunges at its target
- If any mob attacks a vex, ALL nearby vexes become hostile toward that attacker -- even "Johnny" vindicators, despite both being illagers
- Not counted as raiders during raids (no bossbar contribution)
- Can fall into the void and die
- A vex with no item in either hand still targets players and villagers but deals no damage

## Drops

- Iron sword does NOT drop (HandDropChances set to 0; Looting has no effect)
- 5 XP when killed by player or tamed wolf

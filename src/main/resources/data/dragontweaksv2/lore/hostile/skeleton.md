# Skeleton

A common undead ranged hostile mob with 20 HP. Skeletons shoot arrows from up to 15 blocks and strafe to avoid attacks. They burn in daylight, flee from wolves, and can accidentally provoke neutral mobs into fighting them.

## Stats

- Health: 20 HP
- Attack (bow): Easy 2-4 HP, Normal 3-5 HP, Hard 4-8 HP
- Fire rate: once every 3 seconds (Easy/Normal), once every 2 seconds (Hard)
- Shoot range: 15 blocks (line of sight required)
- Pursuit range: 16 blocks

## Spawning

Groups of 4 at light level 0. Not in mushroom fields, deep dark, or trial chambers (via regular spawn). Also spawn in soul sand valleys and Nether fortresses (light level 7 or less). In Nether fortresses, 20% of wither skeleton spawns are replaced by regular skeletons.

Skeleton horse trap: triggers during thunderstorms, spawning 4 skeleton horsemen with enchanted bows and iron helmets.

Variants: strays in snowy biomes, bogged in swamps/mangrove swamps, parched in deserts, wither skeletons in Nether fortresses. A skeleton exposed to powder snow for 22 seconds converts to a stray.

Monster room spawners: 25% of rooms.
Trial chamber spawners: 1/3 ranged slot.

## Behavior

Skeletons chase players, baby turtles, and iron golems within 16 blocks. They do NOT target villagers or wandering traders.

When within 15 blocks with clear line of sight, they shoot. Skeletons strafe in circular patterns to dodge attacks and retreat if player closes to within 4 blocks. They sprint toward a player at 14+ blocks. If the player exits the 15-block range, skeletons hold their bow drawn and fire the instant the player re-enters range.

Unlike zombies, skeletons actively seek shade during the day. They burn in direct sunlight unless under shelter, wearing a helmet, in water, or in cobwebs. Helmets take 50% damage per fire tick. Glass and ice do not prevent burning. Skeletons pathfind to water when not wearing a helmet.

Skeletons sink in water but do not drown. They can still shoot underwater, but arrows slow significantly.

## Wolf Interaction

Skeletons flee from wolves. A skeleton runs away from a pursuing wolf until the wolf lands a hit, at which point the skeleton turns and shoots the attacking wolf.

## Friendly Fire Risk

If a skeleton's arrow hits a neutral or hostile mob that hasn't been attacked by the player, that mob turns to attack the skeleton instead of the player. This can pull creepers, spiders, endermen, and other mobs into fighting the skeleton -- a useful diversion.

## Detection Reduction

Player detection range is halved (8 blocks) when wearing a skeleton skull, and this stacks with sneaking and Invisibility.

## Powder Snow Conversion

A skeleton inside powder snow for ~7 seconds begins shaking; after 22 total seconds it converts to a stray with full health. The stray retains all worn gear.

## Drops

- Bone, Arrow, Skeleton Skull (rare, via charged creeper)
- Naturally-spawned gear: 8.5% drop chance (bow; armor if wearing)

# Husk

A desert zombie variant with 20 HP and 2 armor points. The key difference from regular zombies: husks do NOT burn in sunlight and inflict Hunger on unarmed melee hits. Spawns in deserts replacing 80% of zombies. Converts to a regular zombie after 30 seconds submerged in water (then may further convert to a drowned).

## Stats

- Health: 20 HP (leaders: 40-100 HP on Hard)
- Armor: 2 points
- Attack: Easy 2.5 HP, Normal 3 HP, Hard 4.5 HP + Hunger based on regional difficulty
- Speed: 0.23 (adult)
- Pursuit range: 40 blocks (vs 16 for most hostiles)

## Hunger Effect

Unarmed melee hits apply Hunger for 7 * floor(regional difficulty) seconds. A shield block or the husk holding any item in its main hand prevents this effect.

## Spawning

Desert biome only, at light level 0, replacing 80% of zombies. Groups of 4. 10% chance to spawn as a camel husk jockey (mounted on a camel husk with a parched passenger, wielding an iron spear). 5% chance to spawn as a baby; baby husks have a chance to be chicken jockeys.

On Hard: reinforcements (0-10% chance per hit, leaders get 50-75% boost). Reinforcement husks require direct sky exposure to spawn.

1/3 chance selected as "melee" mob for trial chambers.

## Behavior

- Does not burn in sunlight -- remains active all day
- Avoids water; only enters if there is no other path to its target
- After 30 seconds submerged, converts to a regular zombie (then may convert to drowned if still submerged)
- Hard difficulty: up to 10% of husks can break wooden/copper doors
- Seeks out and stomps turtle eggs
- Can pick up items (armor, weapons) if mobGriefing is enabled; prevents despawn
- Spear-wielding husks use a charge attack, backing up to maintain distance between lunges

## Undead Properties

- Does not burn in sunlight (unique among undead)
- Damaged by Instant Health, healed by Instant Damage
- Smite enchantment effective
- Ignored by the Wither

## Drops

- Rotten flesh
- Naturally-spawned equipment (8.5% drop, +1% per Looting level)

# Silverfish

A small hostile arthropod with 8 HP that hides inside infested stone blocks. Breaking an infested block releases it; attacking one alerts nearby silverfish to burst out of their own infested blocks. Found in strongholds, mountain caves (stone and deepslate), igloo basements, and woodland mansions.

## Stats

- Health: 8 HP
- Hitbox: 0.3 blocks tall, 0.4 blocks wide
- Attack: Easy/Normal 1 HP, Hard 1.5 HP
- Speed: 0.25

## Spawning

**Infested blocks**: Silverfish hide in infested stone, cobblestone, stone bricks, mossy stone bricks, cracked stone bricks, and chiseled stone bricks. Breaking these with Silk Touch does NOT spawn a silverfish. Infested blocks look identical to normal blocks.

Natural infested block locations: strongholds, underground in mountains/windswept hills biomes, igloo basements, woodland mansion false portal rooms.

**Spawner**: Stronghold end portal rooms contain a silverfish spawner. Spawns at light level 11 or lower, or on compatible stone types at any light level -- but cannot spawn within 5 blocks of any player.

**Trial chambers**: 25% chance selected as the "small melee" mob. During ominous trials, any trial spawner has a 1-in-7 chance to dispense lingering potions of Infestation.

**Infested effect**: 10% chance to spawn 1-2 silverfish when an entity with the Infested effect takes damage.

## Behavior

When a silverfish takes damage, nearby silverfish that are hidden inside infested blocks burst out and join the attack. This makes fighting in silverfish-dense areas (stronghold end portal rooms) dangerous -- each hit can spawn a wave of additional silverfish.

Silverfish that cannot find a block to re-enter will quickly despawn.

## Drops

None.

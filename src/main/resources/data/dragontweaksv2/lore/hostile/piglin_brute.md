# Piglin Brute

A larger, more dangerous variant of the piglin with 50 HP. Found only in bastion remnants, spawning once at world generation (not renewable). Wields a golden axe and is permanently hostile to players -- gold armor does NOT pacify it. Does not barter, retreat, or get distracted by gold items.

## Stats

- Health: 50 HP
- Golden axe: Easy 7.5 HP, Normal 13 HP, Hard 19.5 HP
- Detection range: 16 blocks (halved to 8 blocks if player wears piglin head)

## Spawning

A small number spawn in certain rooms of bastion remnants at world generation. Not renewable -- they do not respawn. No baby variant exists. Spawn with a golden axe (sometimes enchanted on Java).

## Behavior

- Attacks players on sight regardless of gold armor
- Does NOT barter when gold ingots are thrown
- Does NOT retreat from soul fire items, zoglins, or zombified piglins
- Does NOT perform victory dances
- Does NOT hunt hoglins
- Does NOT join hunts
- Picks up golden axes encountered on the ground; ignores all other gold items
- Calls nearby piglins and piglin brutes when attacked
- Can open wooden and copper doors
- Returns to spawn point when not in combat
- Prefers attacking players over wither skeletons and withers, but attacks all three

**Zombification**: In the Overworld or End, converts to a zombified piglin after 15 seconds. Keeps its axe after conversion.

## Drops

- Golden axe (8.5% chance, +1% per Looting level, max 11.5% with Looting III)

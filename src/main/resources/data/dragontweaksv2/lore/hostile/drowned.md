# Drowned

An undead aquatic zombie variant with 20 HP and 2 armor points. Found in all ocean biomes, rivers, and dripstone caves -- or created when a zombie's head is submerged for 30 seconds (converts after an additional 15 seconds; cannot be stopped once started). Husks convert to a regular zombie first, then to a drowned.

## Stats

- Health: 20 HP (leaders: 40-100 HP on Hard)
- Armor: 2 points
- Melee: Easy 2.5 HP, Normal 3 HP, Hard 4.5 HP
- Trident melee: Easy 6.5 HP, Normal 11 HP, Hard 16.5 HP
- Trident ranged: Easy 5 HP, Normal 8 HP, Hard 12 HP
- Speed: 0.23 (adult)

## Spawning

Highest spawn rate in rivers and dripstone caves; low rate in most ocean biomes. Spawn in water 2+ blocks deep at light level 0 (ocean/dripstone: Y < 58; rivers have no Y restriction). Cannot spawn with armor -- only Overworld zombie variant without it.

Natural equipment: 6.25% trident, 3.75% fishing rod (main hand, mutually exclusive), 3% nautilus shell offhand (8% Bedrock). Adult drowned holding a trident: 50% chance to spawn riding a zombie nautilus jockey.

On Hard, drowned can call reinforcements (0-10% chance per hit). Up to 5% spawn as leaders (50-75% boost to reinforcement chance). Reinforcements require water 2+ blocks tall.

## Behavior

**Daytime neutrality**: Drowned ignore players on land or in boats during the day. They only attack if the player enters the water. At night or during thunderstorms, they become hostile and attack any player or baby turtle on sight.

**Trident combat**: A trident-armed drowned throws every 1.5 seconds, up to 20 blocks. Thrown tridents cannot be picked up. The drowned keeps throwing even after the player leaves the water or moves far away. Enchantments (Loyalty, Impaling, Channeling) work as for players; Riptide has no effect.

**Door breaking**: Up to 10% of drowned can break wooden and copper doors on Hard difficulty.

**Item pickup**: Some drowned can pick up dropped armor, weapons, and tools. Unlike zombies, they prefer tridents above all other items.

**Turtle eggs**: Drowned seek out and stomp turtle eggs within 24 blocks. They can jump a 1-block gap to reach eggs 1 block higher.

**Immune to guardian/elder guardian laser attacks.**

## Conversion Source

Only normal zombies convert to drowned -- zombie villagers and zombified piglins cannot. Converted drowned spawn with full health regardless of the zombie's prior health state. Converted drowned never gain tridents (Java), and any equipment the zombie had is kept but not supplemented.

## Drops

- Rotten flesh
- Copper ingot
- Trident (8.5% if held, +1% per Looting level; max 11.5% Looting III)
- Fishing rod (8.5% if held, same Looting scaling)
- Nautilus shell (100% if held in offhand)

## Undead Properties

- Damaged by Instant Health, healed by Instant Damage
- Smite enchantment effective
- Ignored by the Wither
- Cannot drown

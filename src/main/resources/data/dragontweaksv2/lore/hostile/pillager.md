# Pillager

A crossbow-wielding illager with 24 HP found in patrols, outposts, and raids. Pillagers are the most common illager type and the source of the Bad Omen effect that triggers raids. Patrol captains wear an ominous banner; killing one starts a raid.

## Stats

- Health: 24 HP
- Attack (crossbow): Easy 2-3 HP, Normal 3-5 HP, Hard 5-7 HP
- Attack (melee, no crossbow): Easy 2.5 HP, Normal 3 HP, Hard 4.5 HP
- Shoot range: up to 8 blocks to begin loading; pursues and fires within 64 blocks
- Fire rate: once every 3 seconds

## Spawning

**Patrols**: Groups of 1-5 spawn after 5.5 in-game days, 24-48 blocks from a random player on the highest solid block. Spawn at block light below 9. One member is always a patrol captain (ominous banner on head). Patrols occur independently of world structure generation.

**Pillager outposts**: Continuously spawn in a 72x54x72 volume centered on the outpost. Uncapped -- outpost pillager count is limited only by normal mob rules. Some are outpost captains. Block light level 8 or below.

**Raids**: Wave compositions vary by difficulty (see Raid entry). One pillager rides a ravager in wave 5 (Normal/Hard).

## Behavior

Pillagers move slowly when wandering or reloading. They always point their crossbow at the player even before firing. Detection range: 32 blocks.

Patrol members do not attack immediately -- they turn to track the player. Attacking or approaching within 10 blocks of patrol members triggers combat.

When a pillager is attacked by a mob (not illagers, not goats), it alerts all nearby pillagers to attack the attacker, regardless of distance.

A pillager whose crossbow breaks (after 465 shots without Unbreaking) becomes passive toward all entities. Unarmed pillagers still frighten villagers and iron golems still attack them.

Pillagers flee from creakings within 8 blocks.

Pillagers can spawn with firework rockets in their off-hand, which they fire at targets in the same arc as arrows (even though rockets are unaffected by gravity).

A player in Creative mode or under Invisibility still causes pillagers to stare and track movement. If the invisible player attacks, the pillager retaliates normally.

## Outpost Behavior

Pillagers at outposts spawn continuously and indefinitely. There is no way to "clear" an outpost permanently -- they respawn. Caged iron golems can be freed at outposts; they turn hostile toward the pillagers.

## Drops

- Ominous bottle (patrol/outpost captains)
- Crossbow (8.5% base, may be loaded or enchanted, random durability)
- Arrow, emerald (rare), iron gear (rare)
- Patrol captain always drops ominous banner

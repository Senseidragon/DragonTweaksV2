# Cave Spider

A smaller, more dangerous variant of the spider with 12 HP (vs 16 for normal spiders). Spawns exclusively from monster spawners in mineshafts and trial chambers. The defining difference: cave spiders inflict Poison on Normal and Hard difficulty, and their smaller hitbox (0.5 blocks tall) lets them squeeze through gaps that block regular spiders.

## Stats

- Health: 12 HP
- Hitbox: 0.5 blocks tall, 0.7 blocks wide
- Melee: Easy 2 HP, Normal 2 HP + Poison 7 sec (6 HP total), Hard 3 HP + Poison 15 sec (12 HP total)
- Speed: 0.3

## Spawning

Spawner-only mob on current releases. Monster spawners in mineshaft corridors at light level 0 (surrounded by cobwebs). 25% chance to be selected as the "small melee" mob for trial chambers trial spawners.

1% chance to spawn a cave spider jockey (skeleton riding). The skeleton has 80% chance to be replaced by a stray, bogged, or wither skeleton in applicable biomes. Cave spider jockeys fit through smaller gaps than regular spider jockeys.

15% of nearby baby zombies, husks, or drowned can mount a cave spider (not one already spawned as a jockey) to form their own jockey.

## Behavior

Shares spider behavior:
- Neutral above light level 11, hostile below (including all caves, at night)
- Climbs any solid vertical surface
- Immune to Poison damage
- Immune to cobweb movement slowdown

The Poison on Normal/Hard does not kill -- Poison stops at 1 HP. However on Hard the 15-second duration ticks down 12 HP total, which is lethal if combined with melee hits.

## Drops

- String
- Spider eye

The Alchemist Laboratory is where the alchemist works. The alchemist brews [potions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion), harvests Nether Wart and minecolonies/mistletoe.

> **Note:** Upgrading the Alchemist Laboratory lets you teach it more recipes. So:

| Building Level | Number of Recipes |
| -------------- | ----------------- |
| 1              | 10                |
| 2              | 20                |
| 3              | 40                |
| 4              | 80                |
| 5              | 160               |

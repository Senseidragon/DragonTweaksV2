The Swineherd's Hut is where the swineherder will breed and butcher pigs for food. You will have to capture and bring in two pigs to the Swineherd's Hut, as the swineherder will not catch and bring in any pigs.

> **Note:** The swineherder will only keep two pigs alive per hut level, so at level 5 they will have 10 pigs in their holding pens to breed and butcher. This means they will be faster at producing and collecting meat. So:

| Building Level | Pigs Housed |
| -------------- | ----------- |
| 1              | 2           |
| 2              | 4           |
| 3              | 6           |
| 4              | 8           |
| 5              | 10          |

## Interface

- **Breeding:** On by default. Here you can choose if the swineherder will breed (and consequently kill) pigs.
- **Feeding:** On by default. Here you can choose if the swineherder will feed baby animals, to let them grow up faster.

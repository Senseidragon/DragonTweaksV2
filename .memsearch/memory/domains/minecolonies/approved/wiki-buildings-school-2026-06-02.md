The School is where the teacher will level up the pupil (children) their Intelligence skill. Paper will increase the leveling speed of the pupil. 

The level of the School determines how many pupil can be taught at a time.

| Building Level | pupil Taught |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 1              | 2                                             |
| 2              | 4                                             |
| 3              | 6                                             |
| 4              | 8                                             |
| 5              | 10                                            |

The Gatehouse stations two guards at fixed positions in the structure to defend your colony's entrance. Unlike the guardtower or barracks, guards at the Gatehouse do not patrol. They remain at their designated positions at all times but will fight back when engaged by hostile mobs.

You may hire any combination of two guards: two knight, two archer, or one of each.

> **Note:** The Gatehouse can only be upgraded to level 3, not level 5.

The Gatehouse also enables colony connections. By placing colony signs in the world, you can create connections between your colony and other colonies. These connections are the foundation for future trading, diplomacy, and inter-colony mechanics.

## Interface

- **Retreat on low health:** Here you can choose if the guards will attempt to retreat when they have low health.
- **Hire Trainee:** If there is a vacancy at the Gatehouse, a new knight or archer can be hired from the respective training facility (combatacademy for knight and archery for archer) instead of an unemployed colonist. This setting only matters if Assign Colonists to Jobs is turned to Automatic in the townhall GUI.

This tab shows all active colony connections to your colony. Connections are established by placing colony signs in the world.

- **Direct connections** are your immediate neighbouring colonies.
- **Indirect connections** are colonies connected to yours through the connections of your allied colonies.

All listed colonies show their distance from yours and their current diplomatic status: allied, neutral, or enemy. Allied colonies have a travel button that teleports you directly to their Gatehouse.

The Smeltery is where the smeltery will smelt ores into ingots.

The primary skill level of the smeltery will determine how many furnaces they will be able to use, up to a maximum of 10.
The smeltery applies fortune to the ores they smelt. The fortune level is building level -1 (for example, a level 3 smeltery, will apply fortune II).

The higher the level of the Smeltery, the more furnaces the smeltery will be able to use.
A higher level will also have a higher chance to double and even triple the ingot output per block of ore. 

## Interface

Listed here are all ore blocks the Smeltery can smelt. Turn off any ores you do not want smelted.
All coded ores (even if they come from other mods) are automatically added to this list.
If you have ores that are not on the list, they can be added to the [[configfile.mdoc]].

The Fisher's Hut is where the fisherman will catch fish. The fisherman requires a fishing rod and a body of water of a minimum of  to be able to work, and the water must be near the hut.

Upgrading the Fisher's Hut will expand the range at which the fisherman can fish, and the higher the level of the Fisher's Hut, the more loot the fisherman will be fishing out (instead of fish). This includes prismarine and sponges!

> **Note:** The fisherman will only catch fish and junk in most biomes. If the fisherman is in an ocean biome, items from treasure category will also be caught.

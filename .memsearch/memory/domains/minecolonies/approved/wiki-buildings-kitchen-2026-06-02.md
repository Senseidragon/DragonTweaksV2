The Cookery is where the kitchen will create food for your citizens.
Food will be cooked when buildings request is, use this conjunction with your cook to automatically keep your cook stocked with food.

> **Note:** Upgrading the Cookery lets you teach it more recipes. So:

| Building Level | Number of Recipes |
| -------------- | ----------------- |
| 1              | 10                |
| 2              | 20                |
| 3              | 40                |
| 4              | 80                |
| 5              | 160               |

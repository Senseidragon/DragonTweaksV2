Snow Golem
==========

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For other uses, see [Snow (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snow_(disambiguation) "Snow (disambiguation)")
 and [Golem](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Golem "Golem")
.

A **snow golem** is a buildable [passive mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Passive_mob "Passive mob")
 that throws [snowballs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snowball "Snowball")
 at monsters, which provokes them into attacking it. Depending on the [biome temperature](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Biome_temperature "Biome temperature")
, it also either produces a trail of [snow](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snow "Snow")
 or takes heat [damage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage "Damage")
 and dies.

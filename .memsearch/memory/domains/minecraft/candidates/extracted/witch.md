Witch
=====

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For other uses, see [Witch (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Witch_(disambiguation) "Witch (disambiguation)")
.

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) "Wizard" redirects here. For the spell-casting illager, see [Evoker](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Evoker "Evoker")
. For the other unimplemented spell-casting illager, see [Illusioner](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illusioner "Illusioner")
. For the mob in _[Minecraft Dungeons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
_, see [Dungeons:Soul Wizard](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Soul_Wizard "Dungeons:Soul Wizard")
.

**Witches** are [hostile mobs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hostile_mob "Hostile mob")
 that use potions in [combat](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Combat "Combat")
, using various [splash potions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Splash_Potion "Splash Potion")
 for offense and [drinkable potions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion "Potion")
 for defense.

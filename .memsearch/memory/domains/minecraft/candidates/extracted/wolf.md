Trivia
------

*   If a tamed wolf is named with a [name tag](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Name_tag "Name tag")
    , its name is shown in the chat to the owner when killed.
*   Despite being unused at the time, one of the two howling sounds can be heard in an official Mojang [YouTube](https://minecraft.wiki/w/YouTube "YouTube")
     video.[\[16\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#cite_note-17)
     The other one is heard twice in _[A Minecraft Movie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/A_Minecraft_Movie "A Minecraft Movie")
    _. A potentially different howling sound can also be heard in the announcement trailer for [Minecraft Dungeons II](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons_II "Minecraft Dungeons II")
    .[\[17\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#cite_note-18)
    
*   The different wolf variants were initially made just for fun, until they were deemed the perfect addition to the [armadillos](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Armadillo "Armadillo")
     and [wolf armors](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf_armor "Wolf armor")
    .[\[18\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#cite_note-19)
    
*   In the [April Fools'](https://minecraft.wiki/w/April_Fools%27 "April Fools'")
     snapshot [22w13oneBlockAtATime](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_22w13oneBlockAtATime "Java Edition 22w13oneBlockAtATime")
     naming a wolf "Mars" gives it a unique texture.
*   In the April Fools' snapshot [23w13a\_or\_b](https://minecraft.wiki/w/23w13a_or_b "23w13a or b")
    , naming a wolf "[kingbdogz](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brandon_Pearce "Brandon Pearce")
    " using a [name tag](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Name_tag "Name tag")
     causes the wolf to be given a crown.
*   A wild baby wolf's head grows significantly when it becomes angry.
*   In [singleplayer](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Singleplayer "Singleplayer")
    , if the player punches a wild wolf and leaves its field of vision, it stares at the player and does not move at all. Going back into its range causes it to continue pursuing the player.
*   If the player attacks a wolf, but then moves a large distance away (e.g. 70 blocks), the wolf still appears hostile, but exhibits neutral behavior.‌\[_[Java Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
     only_\]
*   With wild, tame, and angry states, baby and adult states, 9 variants, whether it is [named "Dinnerbone"/"Grumm"](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Name_Tag#Easter_eggs "Name Tag")
     or not, and specifically for tamed wolves sitting and standing states, 40 tail positions, 16 collar colors, whether it is wearing [wolf armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf_armor "Wolf armor")
     and whether that armor is [dyed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dye#Dyeing_armor "Dye")
     or not, and 16,777,216 dyed wolf armor colors, there are 1,642,824,992,160 possible visually distinct wolves. Because dyeing armor is limited to about 5,439,691 colors in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
     without the use of [commands](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Command "Command")
    , 532,654,544,160 of those are obtainable through normal means.

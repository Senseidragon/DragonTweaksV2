Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_City?action=edit&section=22 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   Some End city rooms are structured similarly to [traditional Asian pagodas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pagoda "wikipedia:Pagoda")
    . Jeb had visited the [Summer Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer_Palace "wikipedia:Summer Palace")
     before the release of [15w31a](https://minecraft.wiki/w/15w31a "15w31a")
    .[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_City#cite_note-11)
    [\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_City#cite_note-12)
    
*   The banners atop the towers have a magenta and black pattern that resembles the [missing texture](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Missing_texture "Missing texture")
    : [![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Missing_Texture_JE4.png?71c27)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Missing_Texture_JE4.png)
    
*   End cities can be over 100 blocks tall.
*   On the End ship, there is a hole in the crow's nest atop of the mast, which is directly above a piece of magenta [stained glass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stained_glass "Stained glass")
     on the ship's deck. This is a relic feature from the early days of the ship's development, when the main loot inside the ship was a [beacon](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Beacon "Beacon")
     that shoots its beam through the magenta glass and the hole in the crow's nest, creating a magenta beam. Although the beacon was replaced with the [elytra](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elytra "Elytra")
    , the magenta glass and the hole remained unchanged.
*   The reason on why End cities have [beetroot seeds](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Beetroot_seeds "Beetroot seeds")
     in their chests is because on [Legacy Console Editions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Legacy_Console_Edition "Legacy Console Edition")
     of the game, the worlds were limited sizes, so they had to add a way to obtain [beetroot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Beetroot "Beetroot")
     in [Survival](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Survival "Survival")
     worlds. They were then added to _[Java Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    _ for parity reasons.

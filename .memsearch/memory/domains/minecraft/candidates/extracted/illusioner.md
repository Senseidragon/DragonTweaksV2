Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illusioner?section=16&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illusioner?action=edit&section=16 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   The illusioner displace sound is almost the same as the traditional sound of teleportation via an [ender pearl](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_pearl "Ender pearl")
     or [chorus fruit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chorus_fruit "Chorus fruit")
    , [endermen](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Endermen "Endermen")
     or [shulkers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shulker "Shulker")
    , albeit with a slight distortion at the end.
*   Illusioners shoot backward when they are in a [boat](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boat "Boat")
    .[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illusioner#cite_note-3)
    
*   Illusioners hold bows in a different direction compared to [skeletons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton "Skeleton")
     or players, a little more diagonally.
*   Despite being unused, the illusioner was present in a _[Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft "Minecraft")
    _ video about every mob in the game.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illusioner#cite_note-4)
    *   As a result of being unused, an episode of [The Story of Mobs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Story_of_Mobs "The Story of Mobs")
         gave instructions on how to spawn it to give context for what the mob is.[\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illusioner#cite_note-5)
        
*   The illusioner displace spell can be triggered by splashing them with a splash potion of invisibility.

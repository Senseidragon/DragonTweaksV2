Ravager
=======

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) This article is about the illager mob that only spawns during raids. For the jockey ridden by a pillager, vindicator, or evoker, see [Ravager Jockey](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ravager_Jockey "Ravager Jockey")
.

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For the mob in _[Minecraft Dungeons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
_, see [MCD:Ravager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Ravager "Dungeons:Ravager")
.

A **ravager** is a large [hostile mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hostile_mob "Hostile mob")
 that only spawns alongside [illagers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illager "Illager")
 during [raids](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Raid "Raid")
. It attacks [players](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
, adult [villagers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager "Villager")
, [wandering traders](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wandering_trader "Wandering trader")
, and [iron golems](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_golem "Iron golem")
 by charging, biting, and roaring at them. A ravager can sometimes become stunned if its attack is blocked with a [shield](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shield "Shield")
.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ravager?action=edit&section=23 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   When the ravager was first revealed at [MINECON Earth 2018](https://minecraft.wiki/w/MINECON_Earth_2018 "MINECON Earth 2018")
    , it had the placeholder name of "illager beast", and kept this name for multiple snapshots and betas as Mojang listened for name suggestions from the community. In [Java Edition 19w05a](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_19w05a "Java Edition 19w05a")
     and [Bedrock Edition beta 1.11.0.1](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition_beta_1.11.0.1 "Bedrock Edition beta 1.11.0.1")
    , illager beasts were named ravagers, although "behemoth" was also considered.[\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ravager#cite_note-8)
    
*   Ravagers originally had been designed to carry crossbows strapped to their backs, but it was decided that it was a bit much, so the idea was dropped. However, the same effect was gotten when a pillager was placed in its back.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ravager#cite_note-meet-9)
    
*   Ravagers were mainly inspired by the [manticore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manticore "wikipedia:Manticore")
    , particularly [the version](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Manticore)
     from [_Dungeons & Dragons_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeons_%26_Dragons "wikipedia:Dungeons & Dragons")
    .[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ravager#cite_note-meet-9)
    [\[9\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ravager#cite_note-10)
    *   Jeb and the design team then refined it into the mob seen in-game, which is different from what inspired it.
*   Ravagers have a vaguely bovine appearance, and are jokingly described as "a spicy cow" on the Minecraft.net page.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ravager#cite_note-meet-9)
     However, they cannot be [milked](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Milk_Bucket "Milk Bucket")
    .

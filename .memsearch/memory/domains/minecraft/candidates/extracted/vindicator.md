Vindicator
==========

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) Not to be confused with [Evoker](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Evoker "Evoker")
, [Illusioner](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illusioner "Illusioner")

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For other uses, see [Vindicator (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vindicator_(disambiguation) "Vindicator (disambiguation)")
.

A **vindicator** is an [illager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illager "Illager")
 equipped with an [iron axe](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_axe "Iron axe")
. Vindicators spawn in [woodland mansions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Woodland_mansion "Woodland mansion")
 and participate in [raids](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Raid "Raid")
. They [sprint](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sprint "Sprint")
 toward their targets to attack them with their axe.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vindicator?action=edit&section=26 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   The "Johnny" Easter egg is a reference to the 1980 movie _[The Shining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shining_(film) "wikipedia:The Shining (film)")
    _, where a troubled man possessed by evil spirits in an isolated, haunted, labyrinthine, mansion-like hotel attacks his family with an axe, and says the iconic line "Here's Johnny!" while breaking a door down.
    *   Similarly, the movements of the vindicators in _[A Minecraft Movie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/A_Minecraft_Movie "A Minecraft Movie")
        _ were also inspired by the film.
*   According to the _[Mobestiary](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mobestiary "Mobestiary")
    _, while a vindicator is stronger than a zombie, it still has low intelligence, 'a little smarter than a [skeleton](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton "Skeleton")
    ' (note that vindicators cannot use [bows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bow "Bow")
     in-game).

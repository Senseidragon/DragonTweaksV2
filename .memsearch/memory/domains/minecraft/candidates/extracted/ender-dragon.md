Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon?section=25&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon?action=edit&section=25 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   If the player kills a summoned dragon in the [Overworld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Overworld "Overworld")
     or [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether "The Nether")
    , it does not create the [exit portal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Exit_portal "Exit portal")
     or a [dragon egg](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dragon_egg "Dragon egg")
    .
*   In [Spectator](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spectator "Spectator")
     mode, the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
     cannot view the perspective of the ender dragon, unlike any other mob. However, it is possible through the use of commands (like `/[spectate](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands/spectate "Commands/spectate") `). When this happens, the player's camera appears about 1 block over the dragon's body.
*   In [Legacy Console Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Legacy_Console_Edition "Legacy Console Edition")
    , the ender dragon's death sound is bass-boosted.\[_[verify](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Special:TalkPage/Ender_Dragon "Special:TalkPage/Ender Dragon")\
    _\]
*   Using F3 + B‌\[_[JE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
     only_\] to show hitboxes reveals that the ender dragon's head rotation faces in the opposite direction to where its real head faces. This is also why the ender dragon faces the wrong way when set in a monster spawner.
*   When an ender dragon is spawned in the Overworld, it flies to coordinates X=0 and Z=0 and dives at the ground or does the same behavior as if in the End. If End crystals are placed in the Overworld, they heal the dragon as normal, but it does not perch anywhere and continues to fly around forever.
*   In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , there is an unused texture file for a dragon fireball item.
*   In _[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Super_Smash_Bros._Ultimate "Super Smash Bros. Ultimate")
    _, one of the spirits that comes with the Steve/Alex DLC is the Ender Dragon. It appears as a Legend-class "spirit" that makes the user breathe fire for a long time at the start of each battle.
*   If [commands](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands "Commands")
     or [Creative](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creative "Creative")
     mode are used to get [End portal frames](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_portal_frame "End portal frame")
     and [eyes of ender](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Eyes_of_ender "Eyes of ender")
    , a player can skip to the [End Poem](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_Poem "End Poem")
    .
    *   If a player destroys [End crystals](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_crystal "End crystal")
         or even hurts the dragon and then uses a player-made portal, upon returning, the player will retain their progress.‌\[_[BE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")\
         only_\]
*   There is a death message that reads "<player> was roasted in dragon's breath", for getting killed by the dragon fireball, that is currently unsendable due to a bug.[\[14\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-19)
    
*   The ender dragon was originally meant to play a large part in _[A Minecraft Movie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/A_Minecraft_Movie "A Minecraft Movie")
    _,[\[15\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-20)
     but would still be mentioned in [I Feel Alive](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Movie:I_Feel_Alive "Movie:I Feel Alive")
     as a past adversary of [Steve](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Movie:Steve "Movie:Steve")
    .
*   In the [New Nintendo 3DS Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/New_Nintendo_3DS_Edition "New Nintendo 3DS Edition")
    's e-manual, the fireballs the ender dragon spits are referred to as "Ender Acid", and its breath is referred to as "Acid breath".
*   While working on [Minecraft: Volume 1](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft:_Volume_1 "Minecraft: Volume 1")
    , [Sfé R. Monster](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sf%C3%A9_R._Monster?action=edit&redlink=1 "Sfé R. Monster (page does not exist)")
     had difficulty drawing the ender dragon and tried to make the illustrations as game accurate as possible.[\[16\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-21)
    

### Notch-related

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon?section=26&veaction=edit "Edit section: Notch-related")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon?action=edit&section=26 "Edit section's source code: Notch-related")\
\]

*   On Reddit, Notch suggested the ender dragon's name to be "Jean?" in the same manner he called the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
     "Steve?".[\[17\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-22)
    *   [Dinnerbone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dinnerbone "Dinnerbone")
         later stated that he thinks the name is officially "Jean?".[\[18\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-23)
        
    *   Despite this, an article on Minecraft.net states that "uttering its true name would unleash a destructive force that would obliterate not only the End, but the Nether and the Overworld at the same time."[\[19\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-24)
        
*   Also on Reddit, Notch referred to the ender dragon as a she[\[20\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-25)
    , and the Xbox avatar item [Ender Dragon Pet](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Xbox-360-avatar-ender-dragon-pet.png "File:Xbox-360-avatar-ender-dragon-pet.png")
     purporting to be her brother has the description "A cuter, friendlier version of his bigger, less playful Minecraft sister"; however, it has been later confirmed by Notch,[\[21\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-26)
     [Brandon Pearce](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brandon_Pearce "Brandon Pearce")
    [\[22\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-27)
     and [Jeb](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jeb "Jeb")
    [\[23\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-28)
     that all mobs in _[Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft "Minecraft")
    _ have no gender, which includes the ender dragon.
    *   Despite this, the ender dragon continues to be referred with feminine pronouns in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
        's in-game encyclopedia. In addition, certain universe canons still refer to the ender dragon with feminine pronouns.
*   Before it was textured, Notch reported that he was afraid to texture the ender dragon as it was a complicated model.[\[24\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-29)
     In the end, the dragon model was so complex Notch stated that he gave up making the texture manually and wrote a texture packer tool to aid him in adding the texture to the model. Notch also uploaded the code for the tool so others could use the pack. ([Link to the texture packer](http://pastebin.com/0AcY6YV2)
    )[\[25\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-30)
    *   Notch joked about how _Minecraft_ was "going next-gen" when deadmau5 mentioned this.[\[26\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-31)
        
*   Notch has uploaded a video showing why ender dragons do not spawn in the main world, as they would destroy any block they touch.[\[27\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-32)
    *   On Reddit, Notch explained that ender dragons destroy Overworld matter because "trying to make an AI to properly navigate arbitrary terrain before the code freeze next tuesday \[_[sic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic)\
        _\] isn't feasible".[\[28\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Dragon#cite_note-33)

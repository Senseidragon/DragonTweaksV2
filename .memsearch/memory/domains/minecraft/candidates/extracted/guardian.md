Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Guardian?section=24&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Guardian?action=edit&section=24 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   A guardian's eye is actually the "head" part of the mob's [model](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Model "Model")
    . This gives the illusion of a dynamic eye.
*   Guardians' laser-beam attacks are affected by the [potion of Strength](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion_of_Strength "Potion of Strength")
    , which normally affects only melee attacks.
*   When placed or spawned on a [slime block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slime_block "Slime block")
    , guardians start bouncing and continue and each bounce higher than the last, until each bounce brings the guardian to 17 [blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Block "Block")
     height.
*   In the texture file for the guardian, "jeb" is written in the bottom right corner.
*   Despite being classified as only a [monster](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Monster "Monster")
    , the guardian was referred to as an [animal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Animal "Animal")
     in a _Minecraft_ video about the [elder guardians](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_guardian "Elder guardian")
    .[\[1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Guardian#cite_note-9)
    
*   [Jens Bergensten](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jens_Bergensten "Jens Bergensten")
     stated that guardians were inspired by "floating eye orb monsters" like Beholders from _Dungeons and Dragons_ and Cacodemons from _Doom_.[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Guardian#cite_note-10)

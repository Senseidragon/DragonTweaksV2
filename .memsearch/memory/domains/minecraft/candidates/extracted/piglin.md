Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin?action=edit&section=43 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   In _[Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    _, baby piglins make angry noises when any piglin gets angry.[\[36\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin#cite_note-48)
    
*   Piglin sounds were made by blending sounds of a Swedish domestic pig known as a "Linderödssvin".[\[37\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin#cite_note-49)
    
*   The page for the Nether Update mentions that piglins have mined all of the [netherite](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherite "Netherite")
     out leaving only the [ancient debris](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ancient_Debris "Ancient Debris")
     behind.[\[38\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin#cite_note-50)
    
*   During development of piglins, Henrik Kniberg thought piglins flopping their ears was a fun way of conveying emotion and played around with piglins having different ear animations for different emotions which included when in idle, piglin ears would move slowly from side to side, when the piglin is excited, the ears would flop quickly from side to side but this was scrapped due to it not being important and not worth going the last mile for it.[\[39\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin#cite_note-51)

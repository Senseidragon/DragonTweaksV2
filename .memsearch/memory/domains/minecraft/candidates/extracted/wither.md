[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Wither_%28painting_texture%29_JE1_BE1.png?65707)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Wither_(painting_texture)_JE1_BE1.png)

The painting depicting how to spawn a wither

*   The wither was originally supposed to shoot flaming skulls, but the game would not stop crashing despite the best efforts of the developers. So, the skulls were changed to no longer be on fire.[\[14\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither#cite_note-20)
    
*   According to [Jeb](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jeb "Jeb")
    , the wither was inspired by _[Terraria](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Terraria)
    _, which has a summonable boss mechanic. The name itself is inspired by a poison spell in another game made by Jeb called _Whispers in Akarra_.[\[15\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither#cite_note-21)
    
*   There is a [painting](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Painting "Painting")
     showing the [soul sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Soul_sand "Soul sand")
     and [wither skeleton skull](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_skeleton_skull "Wither skeleton skull")
     formation needed to spawn a wither.
    *   Similarly, the wither appears on the four sides of [chiseled red sandstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chiseled_red_sandstone "Chiseled red sandstone")
        .
*   If the wither is damaged by the void, it frantically attempts to fire blue skulls.
*   Above half health, the wither always attempts to stay a few blocks above the mob it is targeting. This means that attempting to build or climb up to hit the wither using a sword is essentially impossible.
    *   If the wither is after a blaze and the blaze tries to fly up to it, the wither attempts to keep rising above the blaze, thus making both of them rise higher until they hit a ceiling or until the [blaze](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze "Blaze")
         despawns or dies.
*   Although the wither is 3.5 blocks tall, 3 blocks wide, and 1 block thick, its hitbox is only 3.5 blocks tall, 1 block wide, and 1 block thick.
    *   The true dimensions of the wither are: 2.844 blocks tall (varies), 3 blocks wide, and 1.719 blocks long (also varies).
*   An enemy called the [wither storm](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_storm "Wither storm")
     appears in _[Minecraft: Story Mode](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft:_Story_Mode "Minecraft: Story Mode")
    _. It was created by replacing the center block of soul sand with a [command block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Command_block "Command block")
    .
*   Despite not having visible feet, withers can make footstep sounds.[\[16\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither#cite_note-22)
    
*   In [Java Edition 3D Shareware v1.34](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_3D_Shareware_v1.34 "Java Edition 3D Shareware v1.34")
    , withers drop red keys.
*   According to [Quinn Richter](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Quinn_Richter "Quinn Richter")
    , Marketplace Partner Manager, the wither's name is "Simmons?", similar to the ender dragon's name being "Jean?".[\[17\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither#cite_note-23)
    
*   On the webpage for the 10th anniversary of Minecraft, an image of the wither is referred to internally as a ghast.[\[18\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither#cite_note-:Ghast-24)

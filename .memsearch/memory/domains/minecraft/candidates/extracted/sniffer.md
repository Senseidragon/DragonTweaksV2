Sniffer
=======

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For other uses, see [Sniffer (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer_(disambiguation) "Sniffer (disambiguation)")
.

A **sniffer** is an ancient[\[1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-1)
 [passive mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Passive_mob "Passive mob")
 that spawns only by hatching from [sniffer eggs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer_egg "Sniffer egg")
. It looks for and digs up [torchflower](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Torchflower "Torchflower")
 and [pitcher plant](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pitcher_plant "Pitcher plant")
 seeds in [dirt](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dirt "Dirt")
, [grass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Grass_Block "Grass Block")
, and [moss blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Moss_block "Moss block")
.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer?action=edit&section=21 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   According to _[Minecraft: Mobspotter's Encyclopedia](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft:_Mobspotter%27s_Encyclopedia "Minecraft: Mobspotter's Encyclopedia")
    _, Sniffers have feathers and fur.[\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-book-14)
    
*   The sniffer was originally a doodle Nekofresa made in their kitchen.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-15)
     The sniffer was also Nekofresa's first mob ever designed for Minecraft.[\[9\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-16)
    
*   The sniffer's walking animation was inspired by the leg movements of centipedes.[\[10\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-17)
    
*   During the prototype phase for the sniffer's digging up of items, the developers used beds as a placeholder.[\[11\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-18)
    
*   Due to the size of the sniffer, developers found it challenging to perfect its pathfinding, especially on 1 block edges.\[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "wp:Citation needed")\
    _\]
*   A new team made up of new developers were tasked with developing the sniffer, and most of them had never made a mob before.[\[12\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-19)
    
*   The sniffer's digging animation was inspired by the way [corgis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/corgi "wikipedia:corgi")
     spread their legs while lying down. The action is called a "[sploot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Splooting "w:Splooting")
    ".[\[13\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-20)
    
*   To this day, the developers still don't know what the sniffer's front yellow part is. Whether it's a beak, a nose, or eyes, or whether it's used for breathing or finding seeds is unknown.[\[14\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-21)
    
*   Original ideas for what a baby sniffer would be called were Sniffling, Snort, and Snifflet.[\[15\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-22)
    
*   Mojang developer [Cory Scheviak](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cory_Scheviak "Cory Scheviak")
     personally calls sniffers "[snuffleupaguses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Snuffleupagus)
    ".[\[16\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sniffer#cite_note-23)

Ruined Portal
=============

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For the April fools version, see [Ruined Portatol](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ruined_Portatol "Ruined Portatol")
.

A **ruined portal** is a [structure](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Structure "Structure")
 resembling a damaged [Nether portal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_portal "Nether portal")
, which generates commonly in both [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether "The Nether")
 and the [Overworld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Overworld "Overworld")
. It contains some decoration and a loot chest around it.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast?action=edit&section=22 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   In English, "[ghast](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ghast "wiktionary:ghast")
    " is an archaic word meaning "ghastly".[\[20\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast#cite_note-27)
     [H. P. Lovecraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._P._Lovecraft "wikipedia:H. P. Lovecraft")
     first coined its use as the name for a monster (a vaguely humanoid one, in _[The Dream-Quest of Unknown Kadath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dream-Quest_of_Unknown_Kadath "wikipedia:The Dream-Quest of Unknown Kadath")
    _), but "ghasts" have appeared in many other fantasy stories and games.
*   Ghasts are considered to be fire-breathing [ghosts](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghost "Ghost")
     in their [Meet the Mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Meet_the_Mob "Meet the Mob")
     [Minecraft.net](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft.net "Minecraft.net")
     article.[\[21\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast#cite_note-meet_ghast-28)
    *   In their [Mob Menagerie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob_Menagerie "Mob Menagerie")
         article, ghasts were said to be "large, incorporeal beings" whose origins are unclear. The article states that "they tend to avoid the [fungal forests](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crimson_Forest "Crimson Forest")
         where it's harder to track their prey". They are also "immune to heat damage" because they have "spent so long in the Nether" that "they love the warmth of lava" there.[\[22\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast#cite_note-2024ghast-29)
        
    *   Alternatively, the [Minecraft: Mobestiary](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft:_Mobestiary "Minecraft: Mobestiary")
        , their [appearance](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Movie:Ghast "Movie:Ghast")
         in _[A Minecraft Movie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/A_Minecraft_Movie "A Minecraft Movie")
        _, and later merchandise portray ghasts as organic beings with a stomach system resembling a cannon.[\[23\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast#cite_note-30)
        
*   The player can go inside of a ghast by falling through the top of it or flying (or jumping while attacking) up underneath it. If the player stands on top of the head, the player sees inside of it.
*   If a ghast is trapped in a cube of a side length of one block, it shoots fireballs through the cube. This is because all mobs that shoot spawn the projectile one block in front of it to prevent the projectile from hitting the mob.
*   On rare occasions, ghasts spawn in such a way as to see through blocks (usually trapped on all sides in a tight area). When this happens and the ghast has a "line of sight" through those blocks, it launches a fireball in the player's direction. This is reminiscent of a [blaze](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze "Blaze")
     but with explosive addition.
*   [C418](https://minecraft.wiki/w/C418 "C418")
     revealed in an AMA on [Reddit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reddit "wikipedia:Reddit")
     that ghast sounds are taken from his pet cat, who makes them when disturbed while sleeping.[\[24\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast#cite_note-31)
    
*   There is an unused ghast sound called "affectionatescream.ogg"
    
    in the game files. It can be heard in the last scene of the [Nether Update: Official Trailer](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Update:_Official_Trailer "Nether Update: Official Trailer")
     and during the elytra chase scene of _[A Minecraft Movie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/A_Minecraft_Movie "A Minecraft Movie")
    _.
*   These mobs have been referred to as "angry Ghasts" to distinguish them from happy ghasts and dried ghasts.[\[25\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast#cite_note-32)

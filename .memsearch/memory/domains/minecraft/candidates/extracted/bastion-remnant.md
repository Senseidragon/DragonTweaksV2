`bastion/jigsaw_test` in _Bedrock Edition_. The structure has been modified to replace the invalid `orientation` states with `north_up`.

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Abandoned_jigsaw_test_2.png/600px-Abandoned_jigsaw_test_2.png?c4570)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Abandoned_jigsaw_test_2.png)

Signs in `bastion/jigsaw_test`.

*   The structure `bastion/hoglin_stable/starting_pieces/stairs_1_mirrored`, despite containing the word "mirrored", it is not stored as mirrored like the other mirrored stairs. This has no effect on generation due to the placement of [jigsaw blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jigsaw_block "Jigsaw block")
     in the structure.
*   In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , there are multiple unused structure files. Unlike the unused structures in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    , these structures are more technical.
    *   Two files named `bastion/units/wall_units/edge_0`, and `bastion/treasure/ramparts/lava_basin` contain blocks that have not been added to the game, including `blackstone_bricks`, `blackstone_brick_stairs`, `cracked_blackstone_bricks`, and `chiseled_blackstone_bricks`.
    *   A test structure file, `bastion/jigsaw_test`, contains two oak signs, some jigsaw blocks, stained glass, and stone.
        *   All of the jigsaw blocks in this structure use invalid `orientation` states: `identity`, `rot_90_x_neg`, `rot_120_npp`, `rot_120_nnn`, `rot_180_edge_yz_pos`, `rot_90_x_pos`, `rot_120_ppn`, `rot_120_pnp`, `rot_180_edge_yz_neg`, `rot_180_face_xz`, `rot_90_y_neg`, `rot_90_y_pos`
*   Buried sections can exist within the bastion and alongside it. Ore may generate alongside or within [netherrack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherrack "Netherrack")
    , but [glowstone blobs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glowstone_blob "Glowstone blob")
     can do so within its air.

# Herobrine — Community Lore

### "White Eyes"

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/White_Eyes.png/600px-White_Eyes.png?45c4d)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:White_Eyes.png)

The original screenshot which inspired the "White Eyes" story, from July 30, 2010, with an enhanced excerpt below.

On July 31, 2010, [Minecraft Forum](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Forum "Minecraft Forum")
 user Flaky posted a comment, supposedly copied from a post on 4chan's /v/ board, containing a story of a player encountering an entity with glowing white eyes after listening to the ["13" music disc](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Music_Disc_13 "Music Disc 13")
.[\[1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-1)
 One supposed instance of these eyes was linked in the comment, which was a [YouTube](https://minecraft.wiki/w/YouTube "YouTube")
 upload of "13" posted the previous day (the same day that the disc was added to the game), showing a [screenshot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Screenshot "Screenshot")
 of a dark [cave](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cave "Cave")
 with two white eyes edited into the bottom left corner.[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-2)
 This story is the first known instance of the concept of a creepypasta regarding a white-eyed paranormal being in _Minecraft_.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-3)
 These "white eyes" were likely inspired by a graphical bug in older versions that caused white dots to appear in the seams between blocks, which could resemble pairs of white eyes if two such dots were viewed from a distance.[\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-4)

The following day, Forum user Endorphine posted a thread recounting a myth surrounding the [ambient sounds](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ambient_sounds "Ambient sounds")
 added to caves in [Alpha v1.0.3](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_Alpha_v1.0.3 "Java Edition Alpha v1.0.3")
, in which "a pair of white pixels" would appear whenever these sounds would play.[\[5\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-5)
 The same video was linked as an example of an appearance of these white pixels. The phenomenon was attributed to "White Eyes", a female creature who would stalk the player from afar with no visible traits other than her blank white eyes.[\[6\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-6)

### Herobrine creepypasta

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Edit_of_the_original_%282%29.png/200px-Edit_of_the_original_%282%29.png?e19d8)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Edit_of_the_original_(2).png)

Zoomed-in excerpt of Herobrine in the original image.

The "White Eyes" story began spreading through the Minecraft Forum, either stemming from or being popularized by the aforementioned thread. On August 24, a modified version of the story appeared, in which "White Eyes" was now a default player model with whited out eyes.[\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-7)

At some point before August 19,[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-8)
 a similar altered version of the story surfaced: "White Eyes" gained the name "Herobrine", and was now described as the deceased brother of [Notch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Notch "Notch")
 (in reality, Notch has never had a brother[\[9\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-9)
), taking the form of a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
 entity with white eyes. The exact origin of this version of the story is unknown, with the earliest known instances of it being from a blog post from August 22, 2010,[\[10\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-10)
 a comment in a Minecraft Forum thread on August 30,[\[11\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-11)
 and a now-deleted image posted on 4chan's paranormal /x/ board on August 31.[\[12\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-12)

The story details an encounter in _Minecraft_ with a nameless human entity in a single-player [world](https://minecraft.wiki/w/World "World")
 who displayed strange behavior such as stalking the player from afar, creating unnatural constructions such as [sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sand "Sand")
 pyramids in the [ocean](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ocean "Ocean")
 and long 2×2 tunnels, and removing [leaves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves "Leaves")
 from [trees](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tree "Tree")
. The writer then attempted to start a topic about the encounter on the Minecraft Forum, only to have it be repeatedly deleted, followed by a private message from a seemingly nonexistent user named "Herobrine" telling them to stop. The 4chan post also contained a screenshot of the encounter, showing Herobrine in the distance amidst the [fog](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fog "Fog")
.

### Brocraft streams

_For more detailed information regarding the Brocraft events, see [the Minecraft Wiki's interview with Copeland](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine/StreamFAQ "Herobrine/StreamFAQ")
 and [his recounting of the events](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine/StreamStory "Herobrine/StreamStory")
._

Brocraft streamer Copeland came across the Herobrine story on 4chan's /v/ board and took a liking to it. He edited Herobrine into several screenshots to show to his chat and, after getting largely good reactions, decided to stage a hoax; he changed the [texture](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Texture "Texture")
 of a [painting](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Painting "Painting")
 to display Herobrine and put it in a work-in-progress house in his world, planning to show it on a livestream. On August 30, Copeland started a livestream and played through the world as a normal player would, deliberately avoiding the work-in-progress room he had set up for the hoax. He talked about the "ghost" in his screenshots throughout the stream, asserting that it was real and referring to it as "Him", which would later become another nickname for the character.[\[13\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-13)
 At about 20 minutes and 45 seconds in, Copeland entered the room and saw the re-textured painting, then screamed and ran out, promptly ending the stream. He pretended to be shaken up in the chat and later posted a message onto the forums, linking to the video to spread it around in the hopes of getting similar reactions.[\[14\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-14)
 Other users in the thread began debating whether Copeland's sighting was real and started posting their own screenshots with Herobrine edited into them.[\[15\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-who-15)
 After a while, Copeland removed the painting and reverted to normal textures, then proceeded to stream further, pretending to be scared. He announced that he would delete the world to never meet Herobrine again.

Later on, fellow streamer Patimuss made a stream showing Herobrine near a user-made [lava](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lava "Lava")
 pool. This Herobrine was free-standing, unlike in the previous stream, indicating that he was a retextured [door](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Door "Door")
. The livestream went down after Patimuss ran away and saved his game, only to resume ten minutes later, where Herobrine had supposedly disappeared. Afterward, during the second stream, Patimuss could be heard talking to his wife, stating he was trolling, which flooded the livestream chat with outrage. He then faked a game [crash](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crash "Crash")
 and the stream proceeded later with no further Herobrine sightings. Copeland disliked that Patimuss outed Herobrine as a fake.

Around the time of Patimuss' stream, Copeland posted links in chat leading to the website [http://ghostinthestream.net/him.html](https://web.archive.org/web/20130710001351/http://ghostinthestream.net/him.html)
. The page featured [Steve](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Steve "Steve")
's face, but with his original eyes made pitch black and replaced with real, frantically rolling eyes. Below the face, there was a jumble of characters and letters. After removing all non-alphabet characters, the text is revealed to be a slightly edited version of another creepypasta entitled "Wake Up?", which is written as a note to the reader explaining that they have retreated to a "fantasy world" due to suffering from torture (specified as rape in the original text) and that they need to "wake up".[\[16\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-16)

“

In this catatonic state, the victim lived in a world just like their normal one, except they weren't being tortured. The only way that they realized they needed to WAKE UP was a note they found in their fantasy world. It would tell them about their condition, and tell them to WAKE UP. ### Increase in popularity

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Herobrine.jpg/600px-Herobrine.jpg?71cab)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Herobrine.jpg)

An example of a Herobrine sighting.

Following these events, the popularity of the character began to increase, propelling Herobrine to meme status and making him an icon of the community. Numerous screenshots, animations, pieces of artwork, constructions, and videos of Herobrine were created, with the earliest among them being created on the same day as the original livestreams.[\[17\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-17)

Not long after the Brocraft streams propelled Herobrine into popularity, all of the Brocraft streams in which he was featured were deleted, and not archived elsewhere. Before [the streams' recovery](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#Streams_recovered)
 on July 15, 2024, only a few screenshots and written descriptions of the contents of the livestreams still existed, despite many intensive and thorough search efforts. Copeland continued to adamantly state Herobrine was real for about a year onward.

Notch learned about the story of a "white eye miner" and stated on IRC, "there's no white eyed creepy mob like that - but I TOTALLY should add one now." He also joked about adding entire villages of white eyed humans.[\[18\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-18)
 At [MinecraftCon 2010](https://minecraft.wiki/w/MinecraftCon_2010 "MinecraftCon 2010")
, the day after Herobrine's popularity boost from the livestreams, Notch confirmed Herobrine was a hoax, but jokingly alluded to the possibility that he might not be.[\[19\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-19)
 The next day, Notch posted on Twitter about the subject of Herobrine: in reply to a tweet asking if Herobrine was a real entity in-game, Notch replied saying that he was not, but that he "might be soon".[\[20\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-20)
 This further confirmed there was no Herobrine, but again suggested the possibility that Notch planned to add him at a later date.

In October 2010, due to increased drama surrounding the [Minecraft Wiki's](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Wiki_(website) "Minecraft Wiki (website)")
 page on Herobrine, a wiki administrator asked Notch over [IRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Relay_Chat "w:Internet Relay Chat")
 if he would ever be added, to which Notch replied "i \[_[sic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic)\
_\] have no plans of adding herobrine as is, but I do like the rumors, haha."\[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "wp:Citation needed")\
_\] This showed Notch had decided against adding Herobrine, but the use of the wording "as is" suggested that something like Herobrine may be added, further fueling beliefs that he would be implemented. Once again, the same user asked for clarification, saying that the _Minecraft_ community was "getting frustrated by the hero-spam", to which Notch replied that he had no plans for adding Herobrine into the game.[\[21\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-21)

### Modded versions

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Herobrine_Shrine.png/600px-Herobrine_Shrine.png?f7a38)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Herobrine_Shrine.png)

An altar used to summon Herobrine in several mods, including the earliest known plugin.

On December 10, 2010, hMod Forums user tkelly released the earliest known Herobrine mod, a server plugin called "iLoveYou".[\[22\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-22)
[\[23\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-23)
 In the plugin, Herobrine can be summoned by building an altar made of [gold blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Gold_block "Gold block")
, [netherrack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Netherrack "Netherrack")
, [redstone torches](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_torch "Redstone torch")
 and [mossy cobblestone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mossy_cobblestone "Mossy cobblestone")
, and igniting the netherrack on top.[\[24\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-summon-24)
 The mod's version of Herobrine exhibits behaviors similar to those in the original creepypasta and livestreams, as well as new behaviors, such as placing redstone torches,[\[25\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-tkelly1-25)
 building underground dungeons,[\[26\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-tkelly2-26)
 teleporting behind players,[\[27\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-tkelly3-27)
 and luring players into traps.[\[28\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-28)
[\[29\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-trap-29)
 Many YouTube videos from around the time of this plugin's release show players encountering or summoning this modded version of Herobrine on many different servers,[\[30\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-how-30)
[\[31\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-to-31)
 indicating that the plugin was widespread enough to influence the playerbase's perception of Herobrine, and many of Herobrine's traits and abilities introduced in the mod became established in the community as part of the character. Updated versions of this plugin created by other users still exist on the Internet,[\[32\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-32)
[\[33\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Herobrine#cite_note-33)
 but the original plugin from the original creator is currently lost.

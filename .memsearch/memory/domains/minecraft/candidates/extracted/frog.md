Frog
====

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For the baby version, see [Tadpole](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tadpole "Tadpole")
. For other uses, see [Frog (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Frog_(disambiguation) "Frog (disambiguation)")
.

A **frog** is a [passive mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Passive_mob "Passive mob")
 found in [swamps](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Swamp "Swamp")
 and [mangrove swamps](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mangrove_swamp "Mangrove swamp")
. It performs large jumps, swims, and uses its long tongue to eat small [slimes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slime "Slime")
 and small [magma cubes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Magma_cube "Magma cube")
. [Froglights](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Froglight "Froglight")
 are created when frogs eat magma cubes; different frog types create different froglight colors.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Frog?action=edit&section=22 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   The warm frog is based on the [grey foam-nest tree frog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/grey_foam-nest_tree_frog "wikipedia:grey foam-nest tree frog")
    , a real-life species of frog native to Africa.[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Frog#cite_note-8)
    
*   The temperate frog is based on brown [bullfrogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/bullfrog "wikipedia:bullfrog")
    .[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Frog#cite_note-9)
    [\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Frog#cite_note-10)
    
*   Originally, frogs were meant to eat [fireflies](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Firefly_(mob) "Firefly (mob)")
    , but this was scrapped as in real life some fireflies are poisonous to some frogs. Instead, now they can eat small slimes and small magma cubes.[\[5\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Frog#cite_note-11)
    
*   During the frog's early development, the developers didn't know what the frog was going to eat, so they made the mouth huge. Some early ideas for what the frog was going to eat included [bees](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bee "Bee")
    .[\[6\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Frog#cite_note-12)
    
*   At one point in Bedrock Edition's development, in [beta 1.18.10.26](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition_beta_1.18.10.26 "Bedrock Edition beta 1.18.10.26")
    , frogs could eat [goats](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Goats "Goats")
    . However, this was later removed as it was leftover prototype code.[\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Frog#cite_note-13)
     This was later referenced in the trailer for [Minecraft Live 2022](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Live_2022 "Minecraft Live 2022")
    , where a frog eats a goat.
*   In _Minecraft_, all frogs are genderless and have [vocal sacs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/vocal_sac "wikipedia:vocal sac")
    . In real life, only male frogs can have vocal sacs.
*   Frogs were first in the 2019 biome vote for swamps, but lost to the Mountains and swamp gained second place, then 2 years later at Minecraft Live 2021 they were announced to be coming to the game, and came to the game part of The Wild Update in 2022 3 years after being declined in the 2019 biome vote.

Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Villager?section=37&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Villager?action=edit&section=37 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   When a zombie villager wears a [carved pumpkin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Carved_pumpkin "Carved pumpkin")
     or [jack o'lantern](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jack_o%27lantern "Jack o'lantern")
    , its head is so tall that it pokes out the top of the pumpkin.
*   In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    , zombie villagers have a glitched design when using the programmer art [resource pack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Resource_pack "Resource pack")
    .[\[20\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Villager#cite_note-33)
    
*   In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , when a zombie villager holds a [trident](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trident "Trident")
    , the hand that holds the trident lowers.
*   In _Bedrock Edition_, if a zombie villager is summoned using the /summon command with an invalid spawn event, a generic zombie villager that moves extremely fast spawns.
*   In _Bedrock Edition_, the icon for the [achievement](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Achievement "Achievement")
     Zombie Doctor uses the old zombie villager texture.
*   Curing a zombie villager [chicken jockey](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chicken_jockey "Chicken jockey")
     is the only way to get a villager riding a chicken without the use of [commands](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands "Commands")
    .[\[21\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_Villager#cite_note-34)
    ‌\[_[Java Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
     only_\]
*   Green-colored zombies are also present in _[Minicraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minicraft "Minicraft")
    _, a 2D _Minecraft_\-inspired game also created by [Markus Persson](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Markus_Persson "Markus Persson")
    .
*   In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    , the rarest mob is a baby zombie villager riding a chicken, with 3 pieces of enchanted diamond armor, an enchanted iron sword and a jack o' lantern on its head. It has approximately a 1 in 948 trillion (948,148,148,148,148) chance to spawn and can be found only during Halloween, as this is when mobs can spawn wearing jack o' lanterns.

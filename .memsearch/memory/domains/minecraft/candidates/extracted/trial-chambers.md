Trial Chambers
==============

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) Not to be confused with [Trail Ruins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trail_Ruins "Trail Ruins")
.

**Trial chambers**[\[note 1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_Chambers#cite_note-1)
 are uncommon underground [structures](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Structure "Structure")
 that serve as a mid-game combat challenge.[\[1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_Chambers#cite_note-2)
 They consist mostly of [copper](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Copper "Copper")
 blocks and [tuff bricks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tuff_bricks "Tuff bricks")
, and are the only place where [trial spawners](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_spawner "Trial spawner")
 and [vaults](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vault "Vault")
 naturally generate. Trial chambers are also the only place where [breezes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze "Breeze")
 spawn, whose [breeze rods](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze_rod "Breeze rod")
 can be used to craft [wind charges](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wind_charge "Wind charge")
. Various materials such as [heavy cores](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Heavy_core "Heavy core")
 and certain [armor trims](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Armor_trim "Armor trim")
 are obtained exclusively by unlocking vaults in trial chambers. As it is the only source of breeze rods and heavy cores, the structure is also the location where [maces](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mace "Mace")
 are obtained.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_Chambers?action=edit&section=49 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   The idea of trial chambers evolved from [CornerHard](https://minecraft.wiki/w/CornerHard "CornerHard")
     pitching in a meeting to revisit the [monster room](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Monster_room "Monster room")
    .[\[9\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_Chambers#cite_note-114)
    
*   The design of trial chambers was inspired by the architecture of [Mesoamerican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoamerica "wikipedia:Mesoamerica")
     civilizations, whose geometric designs fit well into _Minecraft_. The design combines the aesthetic of ancient civilizations with a futuristic feel.[\[10\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_Chambers#cite_note-115)
    
*   A scrapped chamber design appears in two [YouTube](https://minecraft.wiki/w/YouTube "YouTube")
     videos showcasing the trial chambers and the trial spawners.[\[11\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_Chambers#cite_note-116)
    [\[12\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_Chambers#cite_note-117)
     It includes a trial spawner, two chests, and two redstone lamps with levers on top.
*   [Brandon Pearce](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brandon_Pearce "Brandon Pearce")
     thinks that whoever created trial chambers in-universe seem to be "pretty tinkery".[\[13\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_Chambers#cite_note-118)

Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager?section=30&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager?action=edit&section=30 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   Alongside [crossbows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crossbows "Crossbows")
    , pillagers were taken from [Minecraft Dungeons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
    , even though they were added to the base game first.[\[10\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager#cite_note-16)
    
*   Pillagers were added in [Village & Pillage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Village_%26_Pillage "Village & Pillage")
     as a means to give villagers a "true" adversary in order to balance the new village mechanics.[\[11\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager#cite_note-17)
    
*   An original design of the pillager appeared like a pirate wearing an orange vest. However, Mojang Studios' mob designers changed the mob's design to look more like brigandine armor.
*   In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , a running pillager uses the player's running animation rather than that of other [illagers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illager "Illager")
    .
*   Summoning an unarmed pillager in _Java Edition_ that is invulnerable (`/summon pillager ~ ~ ~ {Invulnerable:1b}`) does not cause villagers to run away from the pillager.

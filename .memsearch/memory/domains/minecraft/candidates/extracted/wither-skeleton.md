Wither Skeleton
===============

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For the [enemy mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Mob "Dungeons:Mob")
 in _[Minecraft Dungeons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
_, see [MCD:Wither Skeleton](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Wither_Skeleton "Dungeons:Wither Skeleton")
.

A **wither skeleton** is a tall, black variant of a [skeleton](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton "Skeleton")
 equipped with a [stone sword](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stone_sword "Stone sword")
. They inflict the [Wither](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_(effect) "Wither (effect)")
 effect when attacking the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
. They are found exclusively in [Nether fortresses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_fortress "Nether fortress")
 and are the only source of [wither skeleton skulls](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_skeleton_skull "Wither skeleton skull")
, as well as the only [renewable](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Renewable "Renewable")
 source of [coal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Coal "Coal")
.

Evoker
======

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) This article is about the mob in the base game. For the powerful mob in _[Minecraft Dungeons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
_, see [Dungeons:Evoker](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Evoker "Dungeons:Evoker")

An **evoker** is a spell-casting [illager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illager "Illager")
 that can be found in [woodland mansions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Woodland_mansion "Woodland mansion")
 and as part of [raids](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Raid "Raid")
. They are the only source of [totems of undying](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Totems_of_undying "Totems of undying")
. Evokers use two spells to attack: one that summons armor-piercing fangs and one that summons [vexes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vex "Vex")
. This is the only way for vexes to spawn (without using [Creative](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creative "Creative")
 mode or [commands](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands "Commands")
).

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Evoker?action=edit&section=30 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   The sheep color conversion spell is a reference to the priest units from [_Age of Empires_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_(video_game) "wikipedia:Age of Empires (video game)")
    , where they can turn enemy units into friendly units (changing the unit's color in the process) after making a "wololo" sound.
    *   Because Microsoft controls the copyrights for both _Age of Empires_ and _[Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft "Minecraft")
        _, [Mojang Studios](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mojang_Studios "Mojang Studios")
         was able to get the actual "wololo" sound effect to use in the conversion sound effect.
*   Evokers are referred to as 'evocation illagers' in the code for _[Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    _.
*   Hitting an evoker in survival mode and converting the gamemode into creative mode still causes the evoker to summon fangs and vexes at the player, although they deal no damage.
*   [Vex Armor Trim](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vex_Armor_Trim "Vex Armor Trim")
     is based on the cloak and eyebrows of the evoker.
*   The texture for evokers includes a white hood. Although never used in-game, it ended up used in the book _[Minecraft: Maps](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft:_Maps?action=edit&redlink=1 "Minecraft: Maps (page does not exist)")
    _,[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Evoker#cite_note-3)
     as well as an article on [Minecraft.net](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft.net "Minecraft.net")
    .[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Evoker#cite_note-4)

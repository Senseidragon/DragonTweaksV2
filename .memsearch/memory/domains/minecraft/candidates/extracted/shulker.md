Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shulker?section=24&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shulker?action=edit&section=24 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   Originally, shulkers were going to fully camouflage to look exactly like a nearby block. However, [Jeb](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jens_Bergensten "Jens Bergensten")
     decided this was too difficult to code, so instead, they were simply made to be a similar shade of purple to purpur.[\[12\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shulker#cite_note-12)
    
*   The word "shulker" is a portmanteau of "shell lurker".[\[13\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shulker#cite_note-13)
    [\[14\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shulker#cite_note-14)
    
*   A shulker named '[Dinnerbone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nathan_Adams "Nathan Adams")
    ' or '[Grumm](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Erik_Broes "Erik Broes")
    ' flips upside-down. This appears to also lift the shulker by the same height as a [pressure plate](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pressure_plate "Pressure plate")
    .
*   By using commands, they can be made to be part of a [team](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Team "Team")
     to attack other teams and can be used as defense [mobs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob "Mob")
    . In that case, it does not attack its teammates but targets most hostile mobs without provocation.
*   Walking toward a shulker at the same level as a player's head pushes the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
     down to the one-block [crawling](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crawling "Crawling")
     position if there is space beneath.
*   In the game's [texture files](https://minecraft.wiki/w/List_of_mob_textures#Current_Entity_Skins "List of mob textures")
    , the shulker bullet is named spark.png.
    *   The shulker's texture file was named `endergolem.png` in version [1.9](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_1.9 "Java Edition 1.9")
         until it was fixed in [1.11](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_1.11 "Java Edition 1.11")
        . additionally the shulker still extends `AbstractGolem` in the current game files.
*   If a shulker in a boat or minecart is dropped into the void, the vehicle breaks but the shulker floats just above the void, taking no damage.
*   While the scale attribute of a shulker can be set as high as 16, its actual size increases only up to 3 times its original size.
*   In its [Mob Menagerie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob_Menagerie "Mob Menagerie")
    , shulkers were implied to be molluscs, a group of real-life invertebrate animals that include [squid](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Squid "Squid")
    , [glow squid](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glow_squid "Glow squid")
    , and [nautiluses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nautilus "Nautilus")
    , given the nature of their shells.[\[15\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shulker#cite_note-15)
    
*   In [Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , Shulkers play their damage sound with its shell open when they close their shell.

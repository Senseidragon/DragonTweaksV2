# Woodland Mansion — Lore

Woodland Mansion
================

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) This article is about the structure in Minecraft. For the sub-mission in _[Minecraft Dungeons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
_, see [Dungeons:Woodland Mansion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Woodland_Mansion "Dungeons:Woodland Mansion")
. For the location in _[A Minecraft Movie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/A_Minecraft_Movie "A Minecraft Movie")
_, see [Movie:Woodland Mansion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Movie:Woodland_Mansion "Movie:Woodland Mansion")
.

A **woodland mansion** is a massive systematically-generated [structure](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Structure "Structure")
 found in [dark forests](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dark_forest "Dark forest")
 and [pale gardens](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pale_garden "Pale garden")
. It is often found far away from the [world spawn point](https://minecraft.wiki/w/World_spawn_point "World spawn point")
. A mansion is inhabited by [vindicators](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vindicator "Vindicator")
, [evokers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Evoker "Evoker")
, and [allays](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay "Allay")
 and is one of the only places where [totems of undying](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Totems_of_undying "Totems of undying")
 can be obtained. They are primarily made of dark oak wood and cobblestone, and are filled with various rooms. Besides [illagers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illager "Illager")
, a mansion's interior lighting is sufficiently low for other Overworld monsters to spawn.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Woodland_Mansion?action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   Woodland mansions were added as a way to implement [illagers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Illager "Dungeons:Illager")
    , the main antagonists of the then-developing game _[Minecraft Dungeons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
    _, into the base game of _Minecraft_, providing them with a place to live.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Woodland_Mansion#cite_note-10)

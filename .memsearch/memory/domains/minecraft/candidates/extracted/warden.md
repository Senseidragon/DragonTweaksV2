Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden?action=edit&section=27 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   The warden is the most iterated mob in terms of designs with it having 175 iterations.[\[34\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-34)
     The second being the [bee](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bee#Concept_Artwork "Bee")
     with it having 13 iterations as seen in [The World of Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_World_of_Minecraft "The World of Minecraft")
     book.
*   Although the warden is supposed to be blind, [spectating](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spectator#Mob_view "Spectator")
     it reveals that it still has normal vision.[\[35\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-35)
    
*   [Brandon Pearce](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brandon_Pearce "Brandon Pearce")
     considers the warden as something entirely new, as it isn't a boss, a mini boss or a regular enemy, but a force of nature: "when a tornado is barreling towards you, you don't try to kill it - you run away! The warden is exactly the same."[\[36\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-36)
    
*   Brandon's thought process on finally giving the warden a drop after he was heavily against it was the warden should never drop something that is important or hard to get that would be worth going through the trouble of fighting it. In this case, fighting a warden is not worth a [sculk catalyst](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sculk_catalyst "Sculk catalyst")
    . This change was made for renewability farms and tech players.[\[37\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-37)
    
*   The warden and sculk were inspired by [Lovecraftian horror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lovecraftian_horror "wikipedia:Lovecraftian horror")
    , as the developers wanted to make something that wasn't traditionally scary (like jumpscares), but scary in a "mysterious and unknown" way.[\[38\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-38)
    
*   In the sixth episode of _[The Secrets of Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Secrets_of_Minecraft "The Secrets of Minecraft")
    _, it was joked that the warden's personal names are "Jonathan" and "William", depending on the scene, similar to how the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
    , the [ender dragon](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_dragon "Ender dragon")
     or the [wither](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither "Wither")
     all have personal joke names.[\[16\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-wardeninfo-16)
     "Warden" itself was also jokingly broken down into "War! Dens!" in the description.
    *   "Jonathan" can be seen on a blackboard for name suggestions alongside "the stalker" and "the hollowed".
    *   It is also joked that the name "warden" was the result of calling it "William" and spelling it wrong.
    *   In the tenth episode, both names were used when [Narrator](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Narrator_(YouTube) "Narrator (YouTube)")
         spotted the warden behind him.[\[39\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-39)
         He mistakenly called the warden Jonathan before correcting himself as William, foreshadowing [Jonathan Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jonathan_Minecraft "Jonathan Minecraft")
        , who appears in the eleventh and final episode leading into _[Around the Minecraft World in 80 Biomes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Around_the_Minecraft_World_in_80_Biomes "Around the Minecraft World in 80 Biomes")
        _.[\[40\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-40)
        
*   [Brandon Pearce](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brandon_Pearce "Brandon Pearce")
     stated in a tweet that the souls in the warden's chest is important to its lore.[\[41\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-41)
    *   He also said that the warden is inspired by the mystery creature heard at the end of the [Music Disc 11](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Music_Disc_11 "Music Disc 11")
        .[\[42\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden#cite_note-42)

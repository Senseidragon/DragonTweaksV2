Ocean Monument
==============

**Ocean monuments**, also known as simply **monuments**, are rare underwater [structures](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Structure "Structure")
 found in [deep ocean](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Deep_ocean "Deep ocean")
 [biomes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Biome "Biome")
 and variants. They are inhabited by [guardians](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Guardian "Guardian")
 and [elder guardians](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_guardian "Elder guardian")
. They are the only place where [sponges](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sponge "Sponge")
 can be found and one of the two places where [prismarine](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Prismarine "Prismarine")
 can be found; the other being [ocean ruins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ocean_ruins "Ocean ruins")
.

A **stronghold** is a rare underground [structure](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Structure "Structure")
 in the [Overworld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Overworld "Overworld")
, and is the only place where an [End portal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_portal "End portal")
 can be found. Strongholds can be located by using an [eye of ender](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Eye_of_ender "Eye of ender")
.

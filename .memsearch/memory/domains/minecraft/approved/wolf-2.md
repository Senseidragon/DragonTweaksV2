### Movement

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Wolf_shaking.gif?ecab8)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Wolf_shaking.gif)

A tamed pale wolf shaking off water.

Standing tamed wolves wander randomly when near their owner, but follow if more than 10 blocks away and teleport to a nearby free block (if any) if more than 12 blocks away. Besides making travel easier, teleportation can be used to rescue them from [lava](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lava "Lava")
, [water](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Water "Water")
 or pits, as they immediately teleport to a safe area.

*   Wolves can be told to "sit" by pressing use on them and made to stand again with another press of use.
    *   While sitting, they do not follow the player.
    *   Wolves stand up and follow the player if the wolf is pushed into water or injured while sitting.
*   Wolves can either attack (within 16 blocks) or flee from (within 24 blocks) [llamas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Llama "Llama")
    . The wolves' speed is increased by 50% when they flee.
*   Wolves find paths to their targets if attacking within 16 blocks, even in difficult terrain. They also navigate along the edges of cliffs and occasionally fall far enough to take [damage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage "Damage")
     if they leap.
*   Wolves attack their targets running about at the player's walking speed and by leaping at them in exactly the same manner as [spiders](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spider "Spider")
    , but cause no damage while in midair. Tamed wolves attack any animal the player starts to attack. They also can navigate and turn around in 1×1 horizontal tunnels.
*   After emerging from water, a wolf shakes the water off their fur. This is represented by an animation and water [particles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Particles "Particles")
    .

### Teleportation

Tamed wolves [teleport](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Teleportation "Teleportation")
 to their owner if they are more than 12 blocks away, unless the owner is not directly touching the ground\[_[verify](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Special:TalkPage/Wolf "Special:TalkPage/Wolf")\
_\] (e.g. using [elytra](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elytra "Elytra")
, swimming, flying, in a [boat](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boat "Boat")
).

Tamed wolves teleport to a block on the edge of a 5×1×5 region centered on the player. This block must be [transparent](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Transparent "Transparent")
, with a solid block below and another transparent block above. If no such block can be found, the wolf does not teleport.

It is possible for tamed wolves to teleport to an inaccessible location (e.g. under [ice](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ice "Ice")
) and be injured or die of [suffocation](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Suffocation "Suffocation")
 as a result. This happens when the wolf considers [transparent](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Transparent "Transparent")
 blocks, such as [glass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glass "Glass")
 or [slabs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slab "Slab")
, to be open.\[_[verify](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Special:TalkPage/Wolf "Special:TalkPage/Wolf")\
_\]

Wolves cannot teleport across [dimensions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dimension "Dimension")
. As such, if the player is in another dimension than the wolf, it remains in its current dimension and automatically sits until the player allows it to stand. However, wolves can be transported to another dimension by pushing them into the portal first.

Tamed wolves do not teleport if:

*   The wolf has been ordered to sit.
    *   Exception: The wolf is likely to teleport if it is injured while sitting (it no longer sits after teleporting). An example is if a wolf that is sitting is hit by another player, it teleports to their owner.
    *   Exception: If the wolf is in a loaded [chunk](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chunk "Chunk")
        , and the player gets damaged by a mob, there's a chance for the wolf to stop sitting, causing them to teleport if the player is far away, then attack the player's attacker and sit down afterward.
*   The wolf is angry, e.g. if it is chasing after a [skeleton](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton "Skeleton")
    . This can lead to wolves standing and jumping in one place, such as over a cavern if a skeleton is near. The wolf teleports once the skeleton is killed.
*   The wolf is in a [minecart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecart "Minecart")
     or a [boat](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boat "Boat")
    .
*   The wolf has been attached to a fence post with a lead.
*   The wolf is in an unloaded [chunk](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chunk "Chunk")
    , which can occur if the player moves away too quickly for the chunk to remain loaded (such as when flying with an elytra or travelling by boat on an ice highway).

Wolf teleportation is completely silent.[\[5\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#cite_note-6)

### Taming, health, and feeding

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Lots_of_tamed_wolves.png/600px-Lots_of_tamed_wolves.png?d18b9)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Lots_of_tamed_wolves.png)

Many different types of wolves (tamed) sitting.

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Begging-dogs.png/600px-Begging-dogs.png?8cd9b)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Begging-dogs.png)

A group of begging wolves.

A wolf can be [tamed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tamed "Tamed")
 by feeding it [bones](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone "Bone")
. Once tamed, a wolf does not accept any more bones. The number of bones required is random: each bone has a 1⁄3 chance of taming the wolf. If the wolf is tamed, it receives a red collar and, in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
, sits if not [swimming](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Swimming "Swimming")
. There is no limit to the number of wolves that a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
 can tame. A wolf cannot be tamed if it is [hostile](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hostile "Hostile")
 or already tamed. Wolves that are tamed by the same player can accidentally [attack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Attack "Attack")
 each other while attacking another mob, leading to a fight.\[_[verify](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Special:TalkPage/Wolf "Special:TalkPage/Wolf")\
_\]

A tamed wolf's tail rises and lowers depending on its [health](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Health "Health")
. The exact health of an individual wolf can be determined by measuring the angle between its hind legs and tail. The angle indicates the percentage of health that the wolf has.  only_\]). Untamed wolves have a maximum health of 8HP![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83)![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83)![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83)![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83); the position of an untamed wolf's tail does not depend on its health. ### Wolf armor

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Wolf_wearing_Wolf_Armor_JE2_BE2.png/600px-Wolf_wearing_Wolf_Armor_JE2_BE2.png?8f876)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Wolf_wearing_Wolf_Armor_JE2_BE2.png)

A pale wolf wearing wolf armor.

Main article: [Wolf Armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf_Armor "Wolf Armor")

Using wolf armor on a wolf that the player has [tamed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tamed "Tamed")
 equips it onto the wolf.

Wolf armor absorbs all damage done to the wolf with some exceptions (see the list below), until its durability runs out. When the armor absorbs damage, the wolf does not produce a hurt noise.

Baby or untamed wolves cannot wear armor.

Wolf armor does not absorb damage dealt by the following sources:

*   [Drowning](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Drowning "Drowning")
    
*   [Freezing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Freezing "Freezing")
    
*   [Suffocating](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Suffocating "Suffocating")
    
*   [Magic](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Magic_damage "Magic damage")
    
*   [Thorns](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Thorns "Thorns")
    
*   The [Wither](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_(effect) "Wither (effect)")
     effect
*   [Void](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Void "Void")
    
*   Being outside the [world border](https://minecraft.wiki/w/World_border "World border")
    
*   [Entity cramming](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Entity_cramming "Entity cramming")
    
*   The `/[kill](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands/kill "Commands/kill") ` command

If the owner uses [shears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shears "Shears")
 on a wolf that is wearing armor, the armor gets unequipped from the wolf.

If a wolf dies while equipped with wolf armor, the armor is dropped.

A wolf armor's durability is 64 Durability points. It can make a cracking sound in certain points. Damage particles appear as the armor goes down 60, 44, and 20 points.

Repairing wolf armor recovers 8 durability points per [armadillo scute](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Armadillo_scute "Armadillo scute")
. When merged with another wolf armor via crafting table or anvil it also gives a 5% durability bonus.

### Breeding

Main article: [Breeding](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeding "Breeding")

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Sitting_pup.png/600px-Sitting_pup.png?897ee)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Sitting_pup.png)

A pale puppy sitting.

Tamed wolves at full [health](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Health "Health")
 can be bred with any type of meat, including rotten flesh and raw chicken without causing the hunger [status effect](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Status_effect "Status effect")
 (see the list above). In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
, [rabbit stew](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rabbit_stew "Rabbit stew")
 and any type of fish can also be used to breed wolves. There is a 5-minute cooldown in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
 and a 1-minute cooldown in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
 for breeding, during which the wolf does not accept meat. In order to breed, both wolves must be standing.

Baby wolves have a 50% chance of being the same variant as either of its parents. Similar to a [sheep](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sheep "Sheep")
's wool color, the color of a baby wolf's collar is a mix of the colors of the parents' collars, if it is possible to mix them; otherwise, one of the parents' collars is randomly chosen.

Similarly, breeding two wolves with different sound variants or a single one will produce a baby with a randomized sound variant.

Baby wolves take 24000 ticks (20 minutes) to grow, but the growth time can be accelerated using any type of meat; in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
, rabbit stew and any type of fish can also be used. Each use takes 10% off the remaining time to grow up.

Breeding two wolves that recognize someone else as an owner causes the baby to also be owned by the owner of the original two wolves. If two tamed wolves have different owners, the baby is randomly assigned to one of their two owners as its permanent owner.

In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
, if an untamed wolf is fed any type of meat or fish, it enters love mode but doesn't look for a partner and cannot breed.

### Halloween

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Halloween")

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) "Halloween" redirects here. For other uses, see [Halloween (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Halloween_(disambiguation) "Halloween (disambiguation)")
.

On October 31, [zombies](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie "Zombie")
, [zombie villagers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie_villager "Zombie villager")
, [husks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Husk "Husk")
, [drowned](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Drowned "Drowned")
, [skeletons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton "Skeleton")
, [strays](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stray "Stray")
, [bogged](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bogged "Bogged")
, [parched](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Parched "Parched")
, [zombified piglins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombified_piglin "Zombified piglin")
, and [wither skeletons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_skeleton "Wither skeleton")
 have a 22.5% chance to wear [carved pumpkins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Carved_pumpkin "Carved pumpkin")
 and a 2.5% chance to wear [jack o'lanterns](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jack_o%27lantern "Jack o'lantern")
 on their heads. This feature was added in [Java Edition 1.4.2](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_1.4.2 "Java Edition 1.4.2")
 for Halloween 2012 onward, and to [Legacy Console Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Legacy_Console_Edition "Legacy Console Edition")
 sometime after.

The only [splash](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Splash "Splash")
 text shown is "OOoooOOOoooo! Spooky!"

### Christmas

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Christmas")

Main articles: [Chest § Christmas chest](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chest#Christmas_chest "Chest")
 and [Mentioned features § Joke features](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mentioned_features#Joke_features "Mentioned features")

From December 24–26, [chests](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chest "Chest")
, large chests, trapped chests and minecarts with chest have their textures changed to Christmas chests that resemble wrapped Christmas presents. This was added in [Java Edition 1.4.6](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_1.4.6 "Java Edition 1.4.6")
 for Christmas 2012. A texture file called "skis.png" was also added in the same folder as the Christmas chests, but it was added by [Dinnerbone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dinnerbone "Dinnerbone")
 as a [red herring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/red_herring "wikipedia:red herring")
. This was re-added for Christmas 2013,[\[1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs#cite_note-1)
 along with a new "rain feature"—[pigs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pig "Pig")
 riding [chickens](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chicken "Chicken")
, not planned to be implemented. This also happened again in 2014 with a new splash reading, "Merry X-Mas!" and repeated in every Christmas since. The change doesn't apply to [boats with chests](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boats_with_chests "Boats with chests")
.[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs#cite_note-2)

### Thing banner pattern

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Thing banner pattern")

A [thing banner pattern](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Thing_banner_pattern "Thing banner pattern")
 can be crafted using an [enchanted golden apple](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enchanted_golden_apple "Enchanted golden apple")
 and [paper](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Paper "Paper")
. When put on a [banner](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Banner "Banner")
, it displays Mojang Studios' old logo in whatever color [dye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dye "Dye")
 was used. It is labeled "Thing" in the inventory. The Swedish word "mojäng" means gadget or thing.

Languages
---------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=12 "Edit section: Languages")

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Information_icon.svg?15c1c)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Information_icon.svg "File:Information icon.svg")

This feature is exclusive to [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
.

 

There are five joke [languages](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Language "Language")
 available at the language menu: Pirate Speak, LOLCAT, Shakespearean English, Anglish (puristic English), and ɥsᴉlƃuƎ (uʍoᗡ ǝpᴉsd∩) (upside-down English).

_Minecraft_ is also translated into tlhIngan Hol, the Klingon language from _Star Trek_; Quenya, one of the Elvish languages from _Lord of the Rings_; [Lojban](https://mw.lojban.org/papri/Lojban)
, a conlang created by the Logical Language Group; and [Esperanto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esperanto "wikipedia:Esperanto")
, a conlang created by L.L. Zamenhof.

If a player types "**excitedze**" into the search bar in the [recipe book](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Recipe_book "Recipe book")
, their language is automatically switched to Pirate Speak. "ExcitedZe" is the username of [Maria Lemón](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Maria_Lem%C3%B3n "Maria Lemón")
, a former Mojang Studios staff member.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs#cite_note-3)

### Naming mobs

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=14 "Edit section: Naming mobs")

Several mobs can be renamed by using a [name tag](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Name_tag "Name tag")
 to create an effect. These names are all case sensitive.

#### Upside-down mobs

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=15 "Edit section: Upside-down mobs")

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Upsidedownskele.jpg/900px-Upsidedownskele.jpg?e6c8c)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Upsidedownskele.jpg)

An example of 2 pigs and a creeper being named "Dinnerbone".

Any mob (with the exception of the [ender dragon](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_dragon "Ender dragon")
, [squid](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Squid "Squid")
 and [glow squid](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glow_squid "Glow squid")
) named "[Dinnerbone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dinnerbone "Dinnerbone")
" or "[Grumm](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Grumm "Grumm")
" through [name tags](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Name_tag "Name tag")
, commands, or any other method, is rendered upside-down. The mob's behavior does not change.

This also applies to players, allowing Dinnerbone and Grumm's avatars to be rendered upside-down. This can be toggled using the "Cape" button from the "Skin Customization" menu.[\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs#cite_note-4)
 In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
, if the player names themselves Dinnerbone or Grumm, they are rendered upside-down. This works only if the player did not sign into Xbox Live.\[_[verify](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Special:TalkPage/Easter_eggs "Special:TalkPage/Easter eggs")\
_\]

#### Rainbow sheep

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Rainbow sheep")

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Jeb_Sheep_JE4_BE3.webp?ac096)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Jeb_Sheep_JE4_BE3.webp)

A sheep named "jeb\_"

Naming a sheep "[jeb\_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jens_Bergensten "Jens Bergensten")
" using a [name tag](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Name_tag "Name tag")
 or a renamed [spawn egg](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spawn_egg "Spawn egg")
 causes it to continually cycle through all colors a sheep can be dyed. When sheared using shears, however, the sheep drops wool of the original color of the sheep rather than the color that currently appears on the sheep. If a sheep is named jeb\_, and then sheared, the leftover wool on its skin continues to cycle through all the colors.

#### "Toast the Bunny"

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: "Toast the Bunny"")

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Toast_Rabbit_JE3_BE3.png/300px-Toast_Rabbit_JE3_BE3.png?35acd)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Toast_Rabbit_JE3_BE3.png)

A rabbit named "Toast"

When Ryan Holtz was developing [rabbits](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rabbit "Rabbit")
, user xyzen420 asked him to make a skin that would look like his girlfriend's lost rabbit, Toast.[\[5\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs#cite_note-5)
 Now, naming a rabbit with a name tag or a rabbit spawn egg called "Toast" causes the rabbit's skin pattern to look like Toast as a memorial. This also works if the player attempts to name The Killer Bunny; although while the rabbit's appearance uses the Toast skin, it can still attack as it would without the given name. When the player is killed by the renamed [Killer Bunny](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Killer_Bunny "Killer Bunny")
, the [death message](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Death_message "Death message")
 says "<playername> was slain by Toast". "Toast" is re-textured to have black spots on its fur.

#### deadmau5's ears

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: deadmau5's ears")

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Deadmau5_blue.png/200px-Deadmau5_blue.png?1af4b)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Deadmau5_blue.png)
 [![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Deadmau5_RED.png/200px-Deadmau5_RED.png?804b9)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Deadmau5_RED.png)

With the addition of [capes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cape "Cape")
, Notch also added ears for [deadmau5](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Deadmau5 "Deadmau5")
. The ears are additional geometry attached to the player's head, with the texture contained within deadmau5's skin file. deadmau5 cannot disable his ears; erasing the ear texture from the skin file would still render them with a solid black color. deadmau5's name tag is also programmed to render ten pixels higher to be above the ears. The ears are set to show up based on the username, rather than the UUID of the player, meaning that if deadmau5 were to change his name, he would not keep his ears.

Splashes

### Never displayed splash

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=22 "Edit section: Never displayed splash")

Within the "texts" folder, there is a text file named `splashes.txt`. The text file contains all the [splashes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Splash "Splash")
 that appear on the title screen. One of them is "This message will never appear on the splash screen, isn't that weird?" If the text file is modified to only contain this string of text, no splash is displayed. As its text implies, the splash is never displayed. However, in [Legacy Console Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Legacy_Console_Edition "Legacy Console Edition")
 it does display, but it has never been confirmed if it is a bug or an intentional feature.

### Coronavirus

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=23 "Edit section: Coronavirus")

Special splashes were displayed due to the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic")
. They include "Wash your hands!" and "Support elderly relatives and friends!"

Tributes to Technoblade
-----------------------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=24 "Edit section: Tributes to Technoblade")

Main article: [Technoblade](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Technoblade "Technoblade")

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Technoblade_On_Launch_Screen.png?6b368)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Technoblade_On_Launch_Screen.png)

The crown on the pig.

Following the announcement on June 30, 2022 that popular _Minecraft_ [YouTuber](https://minecraft.wiki/w/YouTube "YouTube")
 Technoblade passed away from stage 4 metastatic [sarcoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/sarcoma "wikipedia:sarcoma")
, a rare form of cancer, Mojang added his skin's crown to the pig on the _[Java Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
_ art in memory of him. A splash text "Technoblade never dies!" was also added in his honor in [Java Edition 1.19.1](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_1.19.1 "Java Edition 1.19.1")
 and [Bedrock Edition 1.19.10](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition_1.19.10 "Bedrock Edition 1.19.10")
. The launcher art was later changed to reflect [The Wild Update](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Wild_Update "The Wild Update")
, removing the pig and the crown from the launcher.

A Technoblade pig also appears in _[A Minecraft Movie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/A_Minecraft_Movie "A Minecraft Movie")
_. [Henry](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Movie:Henry "Movie:Henry")
 asks "is he some kind of king," to which [Steve](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Movie:Steve "Movie:Steve")
 responds "No, that’s a legend."

### Command blocks

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=40 "Edit section: Command blocks")

#### Searge

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=41 "Edit section: Searge")

Running "[Searge](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Searge "Searge")
" as the command in the command block without a preceding "`/`" sets the previous output to "#itzlipofutzli". This Easter egg is case insensitive.

#### autoexec.bat

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=42 "Edit section: autoexec.bat")

In the language files, the key for the command block’s “Always Active” feature is `advMode.mode.autoexec.bat`, a tribute to DOS-based operating systems that made use of the file "AUTOEXEC.BAT" to run commands upon startup of the system.

### Command blocks

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=44 "Edit section: Command blocks")

Command blocks have various Easter eggs with messages being added to the previous output.

#### Searge says

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=45 "Edit section: Searge says")

If the command `/[help](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands/help "Commands/help") ` is run via a [command block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Command_block "Command block")
, it gave various messages prefixed with "Searge says: " as the previous output when activated. The following messages existed:

*   "Yolo"
*   "/achievement take achievement.understandCommands @p"
*   "Ask for help on twitter"
*   "/deop @p"
*   "Scoreboard deleted, commands blocked"
*   "Contact helpdesk for help"
*   "/testfornoob @p"
*   "/trigger warning"
*   "Oh my god, it's full of stats"
*   "/kill @p\[name=!Searge\]"
*   "Have you tried turning it off and on again?"
*   "Sorry, no help today".

This no longer works in 1.13 or above ([MC-124295](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MC-124295 "mojira:MC-124295")
); instead the `/[help](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Commands/help "Commands/help") ` command now runs normally, but the previous output gives only the last line ("/xp -> experience") due to there being space for just one line.

### _Java Edition_ launcher

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Easter_eggs?action=edit&section=46 "Edit section: Java Edition launcher")

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Easter_Egg_Launcher.png?9a169)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Easter_Egg_Launcher.png)

Some launcher Easter eggs. The shrugging emoticon has been clicked, causing it to turn white. A witch has also appeared after the player hovered over the play button.

Before the release of the _Java Edition_ launcher version [2.1.497x](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Launcher_2.1.497x "Launcher 2.1.497x")
, the launcher contained multiple Easter eggs. If the player hovered their cursor over the "Play" button for a few seconds, a random mob would appear in the bottom right corner of the launcher. In the top left corner of the launcher, the player could see a translucent creeper face. There was about a 1⁄11 chance of the creeper face being replaced with a shrugging emoticon, ¯\\\_(ツ)\_/¯. When the player clicked on either of these, they turned solid white. Clicking them again would return them to their translucent state. In addition, if the player pressed `ctrl+b`, the [experience orb](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Experience_orb "Experience orb")
 pickup sound plays. These no longer work since the launcher has since been updated.

 found in the first generation of _Pokémon_ games. This was later changed to a [zero-width no-break space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/zero-width_no-break_space "wikipedia:zero-width no-break space")

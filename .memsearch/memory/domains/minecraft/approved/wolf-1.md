Wolf
====

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For other uses, see [Wolf (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf_(disambiguation) "Wolf (disambiguation)")
.

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) "Dog" redirects here. For the wolf subspecies in _[Minecraft: Story Mode](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft:_Story_Mode "Minecraft: Story Mode")
_, see [MCSM:Dog](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Story_Mode:Dog "Story Mode:Dog")
. For the song, see [Dog (song)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dog_(song) "Dog (song)")
.

A **wolf** is a [neutral mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Neutral_mob "Neutral mob")
 that can be [tamed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tamed "Tamed")
 using [bones](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone "Bone")
. Tamed wolves defend their owners against attackers and can assist in combat. They can also be equipped with [wolf armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf_Armor "Wolf Armor")
.

Spawning
--------

Wild wolves spawn naturally in multiple biomes on [grass blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Grass_block "Grass block")
, [coarse dirt](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Coarse_dirt "Coarse dirt")
, [snow](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snow "Snow")
 (in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
 only on 8 layers of snow), [snow blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snow_block "Snow block")
 or [podzol](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Podzol "Podzol")
. In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
, they have a 10% chance of spawning naturally as a baby. Their appearance and the amount of wolves that attempt to spawn depend on the biome.

### Sound variants

Wolves have seven sound variants: Big, Classic, Cute, Puglin, Angry, Grumpy, and Sad. These variants are meant to reflect a wolf's personality,[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#cite_note-2)
 but have no effect on the wolf's behavior.

Wolf sound variants are assigned independently of a wolf's spawning biome, with a 14.2% chance of spawning for one of each sound variant. Wolves will make the sounds associated with their variant when they make bark, pant, whine, growl, death, or hurt sounds. Sound variants are unrelated to color variants. Similarly, the angry sound variant is unrelated to the ![EntitySprite angry-wolf.png: Sprite image for angry-wolf in Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/images/EntitySprite_angry-wolf.png?9f301) angry wolf state.

For the sounds of each sound variant, see [§ Sounds](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#Sounds)
.

Drops
-----

Adult wolves drop 1–3XP [experience](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Experience "Experience")
 orbs when killed by a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
 or a tamed wolf. In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
, a tamed wolf that is killed by its owner does not drop experience.

Wolves wearing [wolf armor](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf_armor "Wolf armor")
 always drop their wolf armor when they die, regardless of what kills them.

Upon successful [breeding](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeding "Breeding")
, 1–7XP are dropped.

Like other baby animals, baby wolves will drop no experience when killed.

Wolves exhibit three different states depending on how the user interacts with them:

*   ![EntitySprite wolf.png: Sprite image for wolf in Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/images/EntitySprite_wolf.png?77c1e) **Untamed** wolves have a drooping tail and their eyes consist of a white pixel and a black pixel for the pupil (on each side). They are neutral toward the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
    . They are hostile toward [sheep](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sheep "Sheep")
    , [rabbits](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rabbit "Rabbit")
    , [foxes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fox "Fox")
    , baby [turtles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Turtle "Turtle")
    , [skeletons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton "Skeleton")
    , [wither skeletons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_skeleton "Wither skeleton")
    , [strays](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stray "Stray")
    , [bogged](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bogged "Bogged")
    , and [parched](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Parched "Parched")
    , taking on their angry appearance while attacking these mobs, and changing back when the targeted mob dies or moves out of range. They are not hostile towards [skeleton horses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton_horse "Skeleton horse")
     despite their skeletal appearance. They chase [bats](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bat "Bat")
     despite being unable to reach them.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#cite_note-3)
     They avoid or attack [llamas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Llama "Llama")
     (see [§ Movement](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#Movement)
     for details), although wolves will always attack a llama that spits on them. They do not [despawn](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Despawn "Despawn")
    , even if they are in an unloaded chunk, or 32 blocks away from the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
    . They can be ridden by baby [zombie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie "Zombie")
     variants.‌\[_[BE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")\
     only_\]
*   ![EntitySprite angry-wolf.png: Sprite image for angry-wolf in Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/images/EntitySprite_angry-wolf.png?9f301) **Angry** wolves are characterized by their constant growling and fearsome appearance. Wild wolves become hostile when they are either attacked by a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
     or [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob "Mob")
    , if not killed in one hit[\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#cite_note-4)
    , or when they see a [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob "Mob")
     they are hostile against. Their tail is held out straight, their eyes become blood red, their mouth is raised in a slight snarl, and they have angry eyebrows. Angry wolves are hostile only to the players or mobs that attacked them, or to the mobs they are hunting. They can see attackers even if they are under the invisibility effect, but they cannot track the attacker down if they get out of their render distance, or the attacker was able to unload the [chunk](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chunk "Chunk")
     where the wolves were. Notice that only the damage done to players gets scaled by difficulty, all other mobs will receive 4HP![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83)![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83) damage regardless of difficulty. Angry wolves cannot be [leashed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leash "Leash")
    , but a wild wolf may become angry while it is still leashed without dropping the lead.‌\[_[JE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
     only_\][\[note 1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wolf#cite_note-5)
    
*   ![EntitySprite tamed-wolf.png: Sprite image for tamed-wolf in Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/images/EntitySprite_tamed-wolf.png?e90cb) **Tamed** wolves have friendlier-looking eyes, and more health than untamed wolves. They have a default red collar around their neck, which can be dyed by using one of the sixteen colored [dyes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dye "Dye")
     on the wolf by the owner of the wolf. Pressing use on the wolf makes it sit and remain in place, and not follow the player. Tamed wolves that are not sitting attack players or mobs that their owner attacks, or those that injure their owner unless the target has the same owner or is on the same [team](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Team "Team")
    , dealing them 4HP![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83)![❤️](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Heart_%28icon%29.png?faf83) damage regardless of difficulty. They do not attack [creepers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper "Creeper")
     or [ghasts](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast "Ghast")
    . Standing tamed wolves attack only skeletons and their variants without provocation. Kills by tamed wolves count as player kills: this means mobs killed by a tamed wolf can drop [experience](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Experience "Experience")
    , [rare drops](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rare_drops "Rare drops")
     or [equipped items](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Drops#Equipped_items "Drops")
    . Tamed wolves are always passive to the [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
    , even if the player hits them or kills a tamed wolf on the same team. Tamed wolves teleport to the owner if they take damage. Mobs killed by tamed wolves give their owner kill credit, triggering [advancements](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement "Advancement")
     and updating [statistics](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Statistics "Statistics")
    .

A wolf becomes hostile to a player or other mob that attacks it unless the attacker is the wolf's owner, or is otherwise on the same team, or if killed in one hit. It also causes wild wolves and standing tamed wolves in a 33×21×33 cuboid centered on the attacked wolf to become hostile to the attacker, allowing coordination for attacks and team hunts (only in wild wolves).

In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
, any tamed wolf attacked by a player/mob causes all standing wolves on that team to attack that player/mob who attacked the tamed wolf.

Skeletons and their variants, foxes, baby turtles and passive rabbits actively avoid both tamed and wild wolves. [![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/EntitySprite_killer-bunny.png?a182f)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Killer_rabbit "Killer rabbit")

s‌\[_[JE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
 only_\] attack wolves actively. Sheep ignore wolves, but flee when attacked by one. Sheep ignore tamed wolves but also do not flee from them.

The behavior of baby wolves is the same as adult tamed wolves. Baby wolves have large heads, similar to other baby animals.

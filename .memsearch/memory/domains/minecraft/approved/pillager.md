# Pillager — Advisor Reference

An illager archer. The most common raider type and the earliest external threat a new colony faces.

## Stats
- **Health:** 24 HP (12 hearts)
- **Attack (crossbow, ranged):** Easy 2–3 HP / Normal 3–5 HP / Hard 5–7 HP
- **Detection range:** 32 blocks
- **Attack range:** fires from up to 64 blocks (pursuit radius); reloads when target closes within 8 blocks; fires every 3 seconds
- **Pursuit range:** 64 blocks
- **Crossbow durability:** breaks after ~465 shots without Unbreaking; unarmed pillager is passive

## Spawn Conditions & Timing
- **Patrols:** Begin after 5.5 in-game days. Arrive 24–48 blocks from the player on the highest solid block. Group size 1–5. One is always a patrol captain.
- **Pillager Outpost:** Spawn continuously within 72×54×72 blocks of the outpost top floor. Block light ≤ 8. Unlimited respawn. Building near an outpost means constant pressure.
- **Raids:** Triggered by killing a patrol captain outside a raid. Most numerous mob across all waves. One pillager rides a ravager in wave 5.

## Threat Assessment
- Hostile to: players, iron golems, wandering traders, adult villagers.
- Alert radius: if one pillager is attacked, all nearby pillagers target the attacker (does not apply if killed in one hit).
- Patrol members do not attack immediately — provoked only when player/villager approaches within 10 blocks or attacks first.
- Flee from creakings within 8 blocks (exploitable).
- Illagers do not attack each other. A pillager accidentally hitting another hostile mob causes that mob to retaliate against the pillager.

## Raid Mechanics (Colony Relevance)
- Raid triggered by killing a patrol captain outside a raid.
- Raid ends in pillager victory if all villagers are killed or all beds are destroyed. Pillagers celebrate by jumping and laughing — audible gloating over the ruins.
- Raid captains drop Ominous Bottle (level 1–5) on death when not in an active raid.
- During a raid, pillagers pick up ominous banners dropped within 3 blocks — can self-promote to captain.
- Pillagers are most numerous in early waves.

## Awareness & Detection
- Pillagers stare at and point their crossbows toward players even in creative mode or under the Invisibility effect — they sense presence regardless. Only if they spot an actual enemy do they break off and attack.

## Tactical Notes
- Crossbow reload takes time — pillagers move slowly while reloading. Window to close distance or retreat.
- An outpost within ~150 blocks of a colony is a persistent threat source — pillagers respawn continuously.
- Iron golems are natural counters; pillagers are hostile to them and golems are hostile back.
- Patrol captains are identifiable by the ominous banner on their back — avoid killing unless prepared for a raid.
- Unarmed pillagers (crossbow broken or removed) are passive but still frighten villagers and draw golem aggression.
- Pillagers will scavenge crossbows dropped on the ground by fallen comrades — killing one and leaving the weapon does not neutralize the threat.
- Pillagers with firework rockets in their offhand will fire them instead of arrows. More erratic, longer range. Different threat profile.
- Pillagers do not attack baby villagers — the youngest colony members are not their target.

## Drops
- Crossbow (may be enchanted; 8.5% chance when killed by player or tamed wolf)
- Ominous Bottle (raid captain only, outside raid; level 1–5)

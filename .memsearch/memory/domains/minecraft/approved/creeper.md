Trivia
------

*   [Notch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Notch "Notch")
     has stated on X that he imagined "creepers as being made out of leaves or similar" and that he is "not sure why they explode".[\[13\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-21)
     When asked to describe its physical texture, he said they would feel "crunchy, like dry leaves".[\[14\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-22)
    
*   Creepers use the classic [oak leaves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Oak_leaves "Oak leaves")
     texture to camouflage them from the same texture. As the oak leaves texture change, this camouflage is less effective.
*   In multiplayer, creepers will not change target unless their original target is no longer targeted.
*   When asked how creepers trigger their explosive nature, [Dinnerbone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dinnerbone "Dinnerbone")
     said "They're so nervous that they shake a lot, and then this sets them off the same way as how rubbing two sticks makes fire".[\[15\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-23)
    
*   If a charged creeper is given [Invisibility](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Invisibility "Invisibility")
    , the "aura" effect is still visible.
*   In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    , charged creeper explosion can inflict the greatest amount of damage any mob in the game can do at one time. In _[Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    _, the [wither](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither "Wither")
    's spawn explosion deals slightly more.
*   The creeper hiss sound uses the same sound file as when a block of TNT is primed but played at a lower pitch.
*   Creeper was the first hostile mob added in the [Survival test](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Survival_test "Survival test")
    .
*   Throughout many pieces of media, the creeper's anatomy is consistently shown to possess a skeleton and a [TNT](https://minecraft.wiki/w/TNT "TNT")
     block inside.[\[16\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-24)
    [\[17\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-25)
    [\[18\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-26)
    [\[19\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-27)
     This is even present in the _[Minecraft Dungeons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
    _ version of the [creeper](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Creeper "Dungeons:Creeper")
    , where a statue of their innards can be found at the [home](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Home "Dungeons:Home")
    . A statue of this design was also produced.[\[20\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-28)
    
*   [Jeb](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jeb "Jeb")
     has admitted that the creeper would probably not be added to _Minecraft_ if the game's modern design principles were followed, since it would be controversial to have a mob that destroys the player's builds. However, it continues to exist in the game due to its iconic status.[\[21\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-29)
     [Notch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Notch "Notch")
     agreed, stating that the creeper only explodes as the result of player (in-)action because random destructive events are "anti-fun."[\[22\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-30)
    
*   The design of the creeper is considered a fundamental "iconic" aspect of the _Minecraft_ franchise, and as such is not going to receive substantial changes to preserve its legacy.[\[23\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-31)
     Exceptions are made for spin-off material, where creepers are required to follow other art styles or practical functions, such as the camera position in _[Minecraft Dungeons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
    _.[\[24\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-32)
    *   However, further exceptions are also occasionally made for merchandise, often to represent in-game states.[\[25\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-33)

### Publicity

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Creeper_Danger.png/600px-Creeper_Danger.png?faaf5)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Creeper_Danger.png)

A sight that can scare even the most experienced _Minecraft_ players.

Creepers have a formidable reputation in the community because of their potential to hurt or kill even veteran players and destroy structures. Their distinct appearance and destructive abilities have made them a widely recognizable _Minecraft_ icon. Creepers are referenced in several of the items available at the _Minecraft_ merchandise depot,[\[27\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-35)
 and have become an internet meme complete with fan art, webcomic references, and demotivational posters. A central part of the meme is the creeper's unofficial catchphrase derived from the mob's tendency to sneak up on the player and hiss before detonating: 

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Creeper_face_1.png?b6233)

“

> _That'sssss a very nice <random valuable object> you've got there... It'd be a ssssshame if anything were to happen to it..._

„

— Creepers[\[28\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-36)

*   Stuffed toys
*   [Action figures](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Action_figures "Action figures")
    
*   Bracelets
*   T-shirts

*   [LEGO Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/LEGO_Minecraft "LEGO Minecraft")
    
*   Papercraft
*   _Minecraft_ Facebook profile image

     and [grass block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Grass_block "Grass block")
    .

#### Appearances

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/PHYSOC_OUTREACH_Creeper_and_Chest.jpg/600px-PHYSOC_OUTREACH_Creeper_and_Chest.jpg?b0c33)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:PHYSOC_OUTREACH_Creeper_and_Chest.jpg)

A creeper and a chest sent to the edge of space by the Physics Society Outreach Project

Main article: [Minecraft in popular culture](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_in_popular_culture "Minecraft in popular culture")

*   On June 18, 2012, the University of Southampton Physics Society Outreach Project sent a creeper and [chest](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chest "Chest")
     to the edge of space in a balloon with a tracker and camera.[\[29\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-37)
    
*   As part of an official collaboration, creepers are included as monsters in _[Monstrous Compendium Volume Three: Minecraft Creatures](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Monstrous_Compendium_Volume_Three:_Minecraft_Creatures?action=edit&redlink=1 "Monstrous Compendium Volume Three: Minecraft Creatures (page does not exist)")
    _, a free add-on for the roleplaying game _[Dungeons & Dragons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons_%26_Dragons?action=edit&redlink=1 "Dungeons & Dragons (page does not exist)")
    _.[\[30\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Creeper#cite_note-38)
     In the game, they are "Monstrosities" and have the alignment "Typically Neutral Evil".

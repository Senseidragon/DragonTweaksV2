**Zombified piglins** are [neutral](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Neutral "Neutral")
 and [undead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Undead "Undead")
 variants of [piglins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin "Piglin")
 that spawn in [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether "The Nether")
. Zombified piglins normally ignore [players](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
, but if one gets attacked and survives, it and all other zombified piglins in the vicinity become hostile and attack the aggressor.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombified_Piglin?action=edit&section=31 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   The pre-[Texture Update](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Texture_Update "Texture Update")
     zombie pigman texture file has "THX XAPHOBIA" written on the unused section as a credit by [Notch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Notch "Notch")
     to the skin's original creator.
*   The zombified piglin's angry sound is played twice the speed of the original sound file in the game.‌\[_[JE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
     only_\]
*   Applying the [Classic Texture Pack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Classic_Texture_Pack "Classic Texture Pack")
     changes the zombified piglin's model and texture back to a zombie pigman.‌\[_[Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")\
     only_\]
*   In narrative media such as _[Minecraft Legends](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Legends "Minecraft Legends")
    _, piglins do not zombify in the [Overworld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Overworld "Overworld")
     because they breathe in [Nether wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_wart "Nether wart")
     spores.[\[30\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombified_Piglin#cite_note-35)
    [\[31\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombified_Piglin#cite_note-36)
    [\[32\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombified_Piglin#cite_note-37)
    
*   In the 2013 _[Marvel's Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agents_of_S.H.I.E.L.D. "wikipedia:Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.")
    _ episode _Girl in the Flower Dress_, Jemma Simmons mentions that another character "runs a rather nasty mob" of zombie pigmen.[\[33\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombified_Piglin#cite_note-38)
    
*   If a player sits on a saddled pig and is struck by lighting, they end up sitting on the newly-transformed zombified piglin.‌\[_[JE](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
     only_\] However, after dismounting, the zombified piglin cannot be ridden again.

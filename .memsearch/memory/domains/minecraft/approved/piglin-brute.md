Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin_Brute?section=16&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin_Brute?action=edit&section=16 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   Piglin brutes were hinted to not have good eyesight.[\[5\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin_Brute#cite_note-9)
    
*   Piglin brutes were created late in [1.16](https://minecraft.wiki/w/1.16 "1.16")
    's development, so they were pushed to a minor release as the developers did not want to add a new feature so close to release.[\[6\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin_Brute#cite_note-10)
    
*   The golden arm guard is meant to work as a "protection" and is the reason behind their high health.[\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin_Brute#cite_note-11)
    
*   Sounds that are used by the piglins are lower-pitched when used by the piglin brute (like the `converted_to_zombified` sound).
*   In _[Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    _, in the `piglin_brute.json`, file, there is a `minecraft:despawn` component, which follows the same despawning rules as [zoglins](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zoglin "Zoglin")
    . However, since piglin brutes are spawned by world generation, this component never functions, and therefore, unused. If behavior packs were made to make piglin brutes spawn naturally without travelling to bastions, the component would function properly.
*   Piglin brutes were made to trigger normal piglins when attacked, as to encourage players to avoid or be careful around them.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin_Brute#cite_note-12)

Trivia

\]

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Castleintheskyrobot.png?f0e9f)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Castleintheskyrobot.png)

Robot from _Laputa: Castle in the Sky_

*   The iron golem's holding out [flowers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Flower "Flower")
     to [villagers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager "Villager")
     is a reference to the ancient robots in Hayao Miyazaki's animated film [_Laputa: Castle in the Sky_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castle_in_the_Sky)
    .[\[18\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Golem#cite_note-21)
     When baby villagers notice the poppy in its hand, they slowly approach, and take the flower eventually.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Golem#cite_note-baby-bug-3)
     Iron golems do not actually pick up poppies; they spawn them in their hands.
*   Another inspiration was [_The Iron Giant_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Iron_Giant)
    , about an extraterrestrial war machine that crash landed on Earth and found a new life as a pacifist, motivated by [Superman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superman)
    .[\[19\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Golem#cite_note-22)
    
*   Iron golems do not get into boats or minecarts on their own, although in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , pushing a minecart on rails into a golem forces it into the minecart.
*   It is possible for an [enderman](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman "Enderman")
     to cause an iron golem to spawn by placing a pumpkin in the correct position on an arrangement of iron blocks previously placed by the player.
*   The iron golem is a purchasable avatar item on the Xbox 360 Marketplace.
*   It is impossible to spawn an iron golem holding a poppy using a command (such as `summon` or `data`). Its NBT matches with a naturally generated golem (`equipment:{mainhand:{id:"minecraft:poppy",count:1}}`), but does not visually update.
*   The iron golem has a smaller [LEGO](https://minecraft.wiki/w/LEGO_Minecraft "LEGO Minecraft")
     variant exclusive from "Advent Calender 2025", as well as one of the [warden](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warden "Warden")
    .

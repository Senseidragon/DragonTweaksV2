# Enderman — Lore & Trivia

Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman?section=31&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman?action=edit&section=31 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   The developers have stated that the belief that enderman sounds are backward distortions is just speculation.[\[24\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#cite_note-24)
     Despite this, there is a [loading tip](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Loading_tip "Loading tip")
     in _[Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    _ that reads out "The Enderman language is actually English in reverse."
    *   The enderman's 4th ambient sound (
        
        ) seems to sound like it's saying "Look for the eye".[\[25\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#cite_note-25)
        
*   [Notch](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Notch "Notch")
     claimed in public that the enderman is a "subtle reference" to the [Slenderman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slenderman "wikipedia:Slenderman")
    , a similar-looking monster with a similar name. Given the later development of the End, it is possible he already had the name in mind, and did not choose it because of the Reddit thread in which their name was revealed.[\[26\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#cite_note-26)
    
*   [Dinnerbone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dinnerbone "Dinnerbone")
     suggested "a haunting of endermen" as the collective noun for Endermen.[\[27\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#cite_note-27)
     Additionally, in the third episode of _[Minecraft: Story Mode](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft:_Story_Mode "Minecraft: Story Mode")
    _, _[The Last Place You Look](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Story_Mode:The_Last_Place_You_Look "Story Mode:The Last Place You Look")
    _, a player is given the option to correct the grammar of other characters by specifying that the collective noun for a group of endermen is "a haunting of Endermen". In the fourth Minecraft novel _[Minecraft: The End](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft:_The_End "Minecraft: The End")
    _, the collective term used by endermen themselves is "an End of Endermen".
*   In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    , a player in [Spectator](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spectator "Spectator")
     mode can enter an enderman's perspective. This applies a [shader](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Shader "Shader")
     that inverts the colors in the world. In this perspective, [the End](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_End "The End")
     appears as an island of grayish purple [cobblestone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cobblestone "Cobblestone")
     in a white sky, with white pillars. Other endermen and the [ender dragon](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_dragon "Ender dragon")
     are white and have green eyes. Cobblestone also looks similar to End stone.
*   In _[Java Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    _, the texture for an enderman's eyes is located in a different texture file than the body, allowing their eyes to glow. This causes the enderman's eyes to be visible even when under the [Invisibility](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Invisibility "Invisibility")
     effect.
    *   In _[Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
        _, the eyes are in the same texture as the body (while keeping the glowing effect), and the texture of the enderman is a `.tga` file format. The real object that makes the enderman's eyes visible even when under the [invisibility](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Invisibility "Invisibility")
         effect, is the `enderman_invisible` material in the enderman's `client_entity` [JSON](https://minecraft.wiki/w/JSON "JSON")
         file in the vanilla resource pack.
    *   With [Vibrant Visuals](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Vibrant_Visuals "Vibrant Visuals")
        , the eyes of endermen are emissive and have actual glowing.
*   The enderman teleportation sound also plays when a player teleports using an [ender pearl](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_pearl "Ender pearl")
     or [chorus fruit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chorus_fruit "Chorus fruit")
    .
*   Endermen are allegedly trying to bring about the collapse to all dimensions, as revealed in the 2019 book Minecraft Game Design.[\[28\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#cite_note-28)
    
*   Notch changed the eyes of endermen from green to purple to match the portal particles.[\[29\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#cite_note-29)
    
*   Endermen possess a skeleton with visible teeth and limb joints.[\[30\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman#cite_note-30)

*   Several endermen appear as enemies in the game _[Magicka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magicka "wikipedia:Magicka")

    _ as an alternate costume for Steve, though with altered proportions to be at Steve's height. Additionally, there is an enderman "spirit" that makes the user vulnerable to damage by water, as are regular endermen.
*   Endermen appear as NPCs in [_Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sonic_Racing:_CrossWorlds "Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds")

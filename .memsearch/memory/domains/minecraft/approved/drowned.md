Drowned
=======

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) This article is about the mob. For the mechanic, see [Damage § Drowning](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Damage#Drowning "Damage")
.

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For other uses, see [Drowned (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Drowned_(disambiguation) "Drowned (disambiguation)")
.

**Drowned** are common [neutral](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Neutral_mob "Neutral mob")
 underwater [zombie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Zombie "Zombie")
 variants that spawn in [oceans](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ocean "Ocean")
, [rivers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/River "River")
, and [dripstone caves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dripstone_caves "Dripstone caves")
, or when zombies drown. They primarily attack through melee attacks, but may also come armed with [tridents](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trident "Trident")
 that they can throw at range. A baby drowned is referred to as a **gurgle**.[\[1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Drowned#cite_note-1)

Trivia

*   Drowned are not considered as aquatic mobs, instead, they are considered as undead and are therefore unaffected by the [Impaling](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Impaling "Impaling")
     enchantment.‌\[_[Java Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
     only_\][\[21\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Drowned#cite_note-29)
    
*   A gurgle [chicken jockey](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chicken_jockey "Chicken jockey")
     that spawns underwater floats to the surface because the chicken it is riding floats, causing the gurgle riding it to burn in sunlight during the day, resulting in a [chicken](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Chicken "Chicken")
     in the middle of the ocean or river where the gurgle chicken jockey spawned in.‌\[_[Java Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")\
     only_\]
*   In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , drowned have a more inflated outer layers. This causes their heads and arms to look larger than in [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
     and causes helmets to go inside their heads and their clothing goes off the sides of their leggings.
*   The sounds for drowned were recorded by [Samuel Åberg](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Samuel_%C3%85berg "Samuel Åberg")
     gurgling milk.[\[22\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Drowned#cite_note-30)

Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress?section=21&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress?action=edit&section=21 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   In rare cases, a fortress may not generate any indoor rooms except one single lava room[\[2\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress#cite_note-8)
    . This usually occurs when a portion of the exterior section of the fortress blocks the exit to the interior section of the lava well room.
*   Sometimes, two or more fortresses can generate close to or even within one another, creating an even larger cumulative fortress.
*   There is an opening out of the fortress under the lava well, with a single block of [Nether bricks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_bricks "Nether bricks")
     from which the [lava](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lava "Lava")
     spreads out.
*   The soul sand valley [bone blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone_blocks "Bone blocks")
     and [basalt pillars](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Basalt_pillars "Basalt pillars")
     (down to within three blocks as-usual of a walkway) can replace more Nether bricks. Less-often, [huge fungi](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Huge_fungi "Huge fungi")
    , [Nether wart blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_wart_block "Nether wart block")
    , [warped wart blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warped_wart_block "Warped wart block")
    , [crimson stems](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crimson_stem "Crimson stem")
    , and [warped stems](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warped_stem "Warped stem")
     can replace Nether brick, most visibly along walls.
*   A fortress can be seen in the background in the Nether in _[A Minecraft Movie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/A_Minecraft_Movie "A Minecraft Movie")
    _.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress#cite_note-9)

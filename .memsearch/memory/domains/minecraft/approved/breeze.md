Breeze
======

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) Not to be confused with [Blaze](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze "Blaze")
.

A **breeze** is a [hostile mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hostile_mob "Hostile mob")
 spawned by certain [trial spawners](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_spawner "Trial spawner")
 found in [trial chambers](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trial_chambers "Trial chambers")
. It moves via jumping large distances when attacking, attacks by firing [wind charges](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wind_charge "Wind charge")
, and deflects all projectiles that hit it except wind charges. Breezes are the only source of [breeze rods](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze_rod "Breeze rod")
, which are dropped upon death.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze?action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   Regarding a change made to prevent breezes from drowning while swimming, a changelog for a _Bedrock Edition_ Preview said that breezes breathe through their eyes rather than their "feet".[\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze#cite_note-lore-10)
     This is referring to how eye height defines drowning height for most [mobs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob "Mob")
    .
*   During the design process to determine how the breeze would look, the developers reportedly sat in a room for half a day sketching and throwing out designs.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze#cite_note-11)
     One of the designs they came up with was a "strider with really long legs wearing cowboy pants".[\[9\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze#cite_note-12)
    
*   While Tom Olovsson was designing the breeze's sounds he tried using [granular synthesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/granular_synthesis)
     to create the whoosh sounds, but mis-clicked while trying to load in a specific file and loaded in a bunch of different "whoosh" sounds instead, and he felt this was actually the right sound for the breeze.[\[10\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze#cite_note-13)

Skeleton
========

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For other uses, see [Skeleton (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton_(disambiguation) "Skeleton (disambiguation)")
.

**Skeletons** are common [undead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Undead "Undead")
 [hostile mobs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hostile_mob "Hostile mob")
 that perform ranged attacks with a [bow](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bow "Bow")
, shooting arrows from a distance. They are most commonly found in the [Overworld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Overworld "Overworld")
 at night or in dark locations, but they also occasionally spawn in [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether "The Nether")
 in [Nether fortresses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_fortress "Nether fortress")
 and [soul sand valleys](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Soul_sand_valley "Soul sand valley")
. They are an important source of [bones](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone "Bone")
 and [arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Arrow "Arrow")
, which they drop when killed.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton?action=edit&section=31 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   If alerted to a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
    's presence, a skeleton can fire [arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Arrow "Arrow")
     backward, occasionally hitting themselves.
*   The artificial intelligence of skeletons in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
     is a mix of skeleton AI from _[Java Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    _ between versions 1.5 and 1.8.9 and the skeletons in [Update Aquatic](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Update_Aquatic "Update Aquatic")
    . They shoot faster when they are on land and the target underwater, causing enough knockback to make combat hard. Additionally, they do not strafe left to right, and they shoot more rapidly at closer targets.​\[_until: [BE 26.30](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition_26.30 "Bedrock Edition 26.30")\
    _\] Skeletons also use ranged attacks on land and switch to melee attacks when sinking underwater.
*   If a skeleton has a [sword](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sword "Sword")
     or any other melee item, generally with commands, its [AI](https://minecraft.wiki/w/AI "AI")
     resembles the [wither skeleton](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wither_skeleton "Wither skeleton")
    's AI.
*   In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , skeletons do not animate their [bow](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bow "Bow")
    .
*   [C418](https://minecraft.wiki/w/C418 "C418")
     used [bamboo wind chime sounds](https://freesound.org/people/jppi_Stu/sounds/17090/)
     as a base for the skeleton sounds.[\[9\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Skeleton#cite_note-14)

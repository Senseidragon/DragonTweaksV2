Trivia
------

\]

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Red_Phantom.jpg/400px-Red_Phantom.jpg?9e82e)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Red_Phantom.jpg)

The red phantom design was scrapped because it looked like a [Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether "Nether")
 mob.

*   At one point before the release of [18w10a](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition_18w10a "Java Edition 18w10a")
    , the phantom texture was made red, but it was decided that it appeared too much like a [Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether "Nether")
     mob.[\[11\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom#cite_note-meetphantom-13)
    
*   The unused red design was used later in [_Minecraft Dungeons_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Dungeons "Minecraft Dungeons")
     as a [pet phantom](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons:Phantom_Familiar "Dungeons:Phantom Familiar")
    .
*   The phantom was originally going to have a mouth, like the drawing shown during [MINECON Earth](https://minecraft.wiki/w/MINECON_Earth_2017 "MINECON Earth 2017")
    , but it was eventually scrapped because "it looked so much like a [muppet](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Muppet "Muppet")
    ".[\[11\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom#cite_note-meetphantom-13)
     Earlier designs can be seen on [Minecraft](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft "Minecraft")
    's [Instagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram "wikipedia:Instagram")
     account.[\[12\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom#cite_note-14)
    
*   The sounds of the phantom were taken from [Samuel Åberg](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Samuel_%C3%85berg "Samuel Åberg")
    's son screaming.[\[13\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom#cite_note-15)
    [\[14\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom#cite_note-16)
    
*   Despite being flying mobs, phantoms can destroy [turtle eggs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Turtle_egg "Turtle egg")
     when on top of them.[\[15\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom#cite_note-17)
    
*   In its [Mob Menagerie](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob_Menagerie "Mob Menagerie")
    , phantoms real-world parallel was stated to be [bats](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bat "Bat")
    , as well as mentions of the extinct pterosaur, [quetzalcoatlus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/quetzalcoatlus "wikipedia:quetzalcoatlus")
    .[\[16\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom#cite_note-18)

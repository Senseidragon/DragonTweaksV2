Elder Guardian
==============

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) This article is about the mob in the base game. For the joke mob, see [Plaguewhale Slab](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Plaguewhale_Slab "Plaguewhale Slab")
. For other uses, see [Guardian (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Guardian_(disambiguation) "Guardian (disambiguation)")
.

An **elder guardian** is a large [hostile mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hostile_mob "Hostile mob")
 and a variant of the [guardian](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Guardian "Guardian")
. It is the largest and strongest [aquatic](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Aquatic "Aquatic")
 mob. It attacks the same way as a normal guardian, and it also applies [Mining Fatigue](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mining_Fatigue "Mining Fatigue")
 to [players](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
 in a large radius around itself. They are only found in [ocean monuments](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ocean_monument "Ocean monument")
; three of them generate in each one.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_Guardian?action=edit&section=21 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   The elder guardian's status as a boss or simply a powerful mob has been historically ambiguous. While it lacks important characteristics of a boss, chiefly a health bar, it has been grouped with boss mobs and addressed as one by Mojang before.[\[3\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_Guardian#cite_note-12)
    [\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_Guardian#cite_note-:0-13)
     The elder guardian was also marketed as a boss in [_Pocket Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pocket_Edition "Pocket Edition")
    , having been introduced in the [Boss Update](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Boss_Update "Boss Update")
    . Jeb later stated that it is intended as a miniboss opposed to a full boss which is why it does not have a health bar.[\[5\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_Guardian#cite_note-miniboss-14)
    
*   The elder guardian's eye is the "head" part of the [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob "Mob")
    's [model](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Model "Model")
    , giving the illusion of a dynamic eye.
*   According to [Jeb](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jeb "Jeb")
    , elder guardians were introduced to encourage [players](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
     to solve the temple maze rather than dig through it easily. He acknowledges there are ways around it, but players would still have to solve the challenge in a different way.[\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_Guardian#cite_note-:0-13)
    *   Also according to Jeb, the "jumpscare" by an elder guardian is unintentional; instead, he added it to provide a visual cue as to why players get slowed down by [Mining Fatigue](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mining_Fatigue "Mining Fatigue")
        .[\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_Guardian#cite_note-:0-13)
        
*   In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , elder guardians make their underwater death sounds if killed on land.
*   The elder guardian drops a sponge to guarantee that a player can get at least three from an ocean monument without a sponge room.[\[6\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_Guardian#cite_note-15)
    
*   Despite being classified as only a [monster](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Monster "Monster")
    , the elder guardian was referred to as an "[animal](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Animal "Animal")
     of the deep" in a _Minecraft_ video.[\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Elder_Guardian#cite_note-16)

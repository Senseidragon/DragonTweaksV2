**Villagers** are humanoid [passive mobs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Passive_mob "Passive mob")
 that inhabit [villages](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Village "Village")
, work at their professions (see [Villager professions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager_professions "Villager professions")
), [breed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breed "Breed")
, and interact with each other. Their outfit varies according to their occupation and [biome](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Biome "Biome")
, and a [player](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
 can [trade](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Trade "Trade")
 with them using [emeralds](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Emerald "Emerald")
 as currency, with their prices affected by reputation.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager?action=edit&section=66 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   The villagers were inspired by the shopkeepers in _[Dungeon Master II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeon_Master_II "wikipedia:Dungeon Master II")
    _.[\[18\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-25)
    
*   Originally, the mobs populating villages were to be [pigmen](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pigmen "Pigmen")
    .[\[19\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-26)
    
*   When a villager is in love mode, it walks slowly. However, when a villager runs indoors as the night falls, it runs faster than the player's sprinting speed.
*   The villager skins added in the [Village and Pillage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Village_and_Pillage "Village and Pillage")
     update were inspired by 2018 fashion shows, such as Gucci's.[\[20\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-27)
    
*   Villagers are genderless, meaning they are neither male nor female.[\[21\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-28)
    
*   Villagers occasionally sleep in odd ways during the night inside their beds, sometimes hanging halfway off the side of the bed or even glitching into walls.
*   Although the villages in [snowy taiga](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snowy_taiga "Snowy taiga")
     biomes spawn the snowy villager variant in [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , they use the [taiga](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Taiga "Taiga")
     village variant.
*   In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    , when the Programmer Art resource pack is enabled, all villagers wear a green hood on their heads.[\[22\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-29)
     This is because the Programmer Art nitwit texture (which is directly copied from the pre-1.14 vanilla resource pack and had the hood in the texture since its addition) is called the same as the [Village & Pillage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Village_%26_Pillage "Village & Pillage")
     base villager texture (`...\entity\villager\villager.png`).
    *   In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
        , when the Classic Textures pack from the [Marketplace](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Marketplace "Marketplace")
         is enabled, the villagers still use their default texture instead of the old texture.[\[23\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-30)
         This is because the old textures of villager are located in `...\entity\villager`, while the textures for new villagers are in `...\entity\villager2`.
*   Giving a villager any item (with commands) causes it to hold the item as if offering it, but it cannot be traded.
*   Fisherman villagers have been intentionally textured by [Jasper Boerstra](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jasper_Boerstra "Jasper Boerstra")
     to display the long-since-removed raw fish texture.[\[24\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-31)
    
*   Villagers display their held items differently than most creatures do, using the "ground" parameter instead of the usual hand parameter in model display settings.
*   Villagers (and baby villagers) on boats that have claimed a bed can still sleep when the bed is near to them resulting in them sleeping in the boat instead.‌\[_[Bedrock Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")\
     only_\]
*   Ancient villagers have been shown in _Minecraft Legends_, although they were hinted at in _Minecraft Dungeons_.
*   In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
    , the [death messages](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Death_messages "Death messages")
     of villagers are recorded in the game's logs.[\[25\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-32)
    
*   Baby villagers taking poppies from iron golems is a reference to the 1986 Japanese animated movie _[Castle in the Sky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castle_in_the_Sky)
    _, in which a giant robot covered in vines (inspiration for the iron golem) gives the main characters flowers to put on a memorial.[\[26\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-33)
    
*   Their vocal sounds are performed by [Daniel Rosenfeld](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Daniel_Rosenfeld "Daniel Rosenfeld")
    .[\[27\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Villager#cite_note-34)
    
*   If a villager accidentally harms a neutral or hostile mob by setting off a firework, the mob retaliates and attacks the villager. For example, a villager that accidentally damages a player by setting off a firework causes tamed wolves and trusting foxes to attack the villager.

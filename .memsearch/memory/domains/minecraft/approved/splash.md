Splash
======

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) This article is about the message on the title screen. For information shown when loading worlds, see [Loading tips](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Loading_tips "Loading tips")
. For the potion, see [Splash Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Splash_Potion "Splash Potion")
.

**Splash texts** are yellow lines of text on the [title screen](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Title_screen "Title screen")
. They are randomly chosen from the list below and often feature references to popular culture (e.g. websites, quotes, and video games). The splash pulsates on the title screen twice per second or 120 times per minute.

[![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/thumb/Java_Edition_26.1.2.png/600px-Java_Edition_26.1.2.png?547db)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/File:Java_Edition_26.1.2.png)

The [title screen](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Title_screen "Title screen")
 of _[Java Edition](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
_ with random splash text.

Splash texts are not translated and appear the same regardless of the language setting chosen. In [_Java Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Java_Edition "Java Edition")
, the splashes are defined in a plain-text file within the `[minecraft.jar](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft.jar "Minecraft.jar") ` file (specifically, in `[_version_.jar](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Client.jar "Client.jar") /assets/minecraft/texts/splashes.txt`). In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
, the splashes are defined in a `splashes.json` file. Splashes can be modified by editing the edition's respective file via a [resource pack](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Resource_pack "Resource pack")
.

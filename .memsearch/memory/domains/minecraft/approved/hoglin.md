A **hoglin** is a breedable [hostile mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hostile_mob "Hostile mob")
 found in [the Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether "The Nether")
, and a source of [porkchops](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Porkchop "Porkchop")
 and [leather](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leather "Leather")
. Hoglins are repelled by [warped fungi](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Warped_fungi "Warped fungi")
 that is placed in the world as well as active [Nether portals](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_portal "Nether portal")
 and [respawn anchors](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Respawn_anchor "Respawn anchor")
. It attacks by thrusting its tusks upward, which can also launch its target a short distance into the air.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hoglin?action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   Hoglins run away from warped fungi because they dislike their smell.[\[12\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hoglin#cite_note-24)
     When asked if Nether portals also smell bad to hoglins, Henrik responded that hoglins are annoyed by the sounds Nether portals make.[\[13\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hoglin#cite_note-25)
    
*   Similar to the [ravager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ravager "Ravager")
     originally being called "[illager](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Illager "Illager")
     beast" before its official name was chosen by the community, the hoglin's name was also chosen by the community. Its initial placeholder name was "piglin beast".
*   In [_Bedrock Edition_](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bedrock_Edition "Bedrock Edition")
    , there are unused texture files named `hoglin_meat_raw` and `hoglin_meat_cooked` that are textured identically to [raw](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Raw_Beef "Raw Beef")
     and [cooked beef](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cooked_beef "Cooked beef")
    , respectively.
*   [Henrik Kniberg](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Henrik_Kniberg "Henrik Kniberg")
     released a video[\[14\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hoglin#cite_note-26)
     of a group of hoglins killing a [piglin](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Piglin "Piglin")
    , then "celebrating" by bobbing their heads, then getting on their hind legs and dancing. This feature was never added into any snapshot and is not actually in _Minecraft_, although the reverse situation has a small chance to occur, in which piglins dance after killing a hoglin.
*   The hair and body texture of the hoglin do not line up, as the body was inflated to avoid Z-fighting.

Blaze
=====

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For other uses, see [Blaze (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_(disambiguation) "Blaze (disambiguation)")
.

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) Not to be confused with [Breeze](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze "Breeze")
.

A **blaze** is a fiery [hostile mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Hostile_mob "Hostile mob")
 found in [Nether fortresses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_fortress "Nether fortress")
 and is the only source of [blaze rods](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_rod "Blaze rod")
. A blaze attacks by floating upward and shooting [fireballs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Small_Fireball "Small Fireball")
 at its target in volleys of three at a time. It can also attack its target in melee range with its spinning rods.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze?action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   When throwing a [potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion "Potion")
     of [Invisibility](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Invisibility "Invisibility")
     on a blaze, the smoke remains visible.
*   According to [Jens Bergensten](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jens_Bergensten "Jens Bergensten")
    , blazes look the way they do because all mobs in _Minecraft_ are cuboid-shape, so to make them flame-like and magical, he created them as a core surrounded by rotating rods.[\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze#cite_note-6)
    
*   Blazes originally didn't have eyes when Jens Bergensten was first creating them and "just looked like a yellow rock". Eyes were added to give it more personality.[\[5\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze#cite_note-7)
    
*   Despite not having visible feet, blazes are still capable of making footstep sounds.[\[6\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze#cite_note-8)
    
*   According to Minecraft's "Visit the Nether!" blazes are sentient beings made of pure lava. The article theorizes they were created by the original inhabitants of Nether fortresses to serve as guards, but their creators lost control over the blazes and were subsequently destroyed by them.[\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze#cite_note-9)
    

### Publicity

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze?action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Publicity")

*   On the official T-shirt "Alchemy" by JINX, the blaze is in the form of the [Vitruvian Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitruvian_Man "wikipedia:Vitruvian Man")
     and the text uses the [Standard Galactic Alphabet](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Standard_Galactic_Alphabet "Standard Galactic Alphabet")
    . The words on the outside of the ring translate to: "nigredo", "albedo", "citrinitas", and "rubedo",[\[note 1\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze#cite_note-10)
     which are all relating to [magnum opus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnum_opus_(alchemy) "wikipedia:Magnum opus (alchemy)")
    . The words on the ring translate to (starting from the top): "B \* Crowmer", "poison \* speed", "strength \* resist" \[_[sic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic)\
    _\], "vision \* heal", and "regeneration" relating to [potions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potions "Potions")
     and probably the artist's name. On the connecting lines the words translate to: "potln of thickness" \[_[sic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic)\
    _\], "potln of awkward" \[_[sic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic)\
    _\], "weakness potion" and "mundane potion" relating to [base potions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Base_potions "Potion")
    .
*   As part of an official collaboration, blazes are included as monsters in a free add-on for the roleplaying game [_Dungeons & Dragons_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeons_%26_Dragons "wikipedia:Dungeons & Dragons")
    .[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze#cite_note-11)
     In the game, they are "Elementals" and have the alignment "Typically Neutral Evil."
*   In [Minecraft: Mobspotter's Encyclopedia](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft:_Mobspotter%27s_Encyclopedia "Minecraft: Mobspotter's Encyclopedia")
    , blazes are depicted using the smoke particles used for [campfires](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Campfires "Campfires")
     instead of their traditional ones.

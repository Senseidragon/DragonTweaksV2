Axolotl
=======

![](https://minecraft.wiki/images/Disambig_color.svg?2db52) For other uses, see [Axolotl (disambiguation)](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl_(disambiguation) "Axolotl (disambiguation)")
.

An **axolotl** is a passive aquatic [mob](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mob "Mob")
 found in [lush caves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lush_caves "Lush caves")
 that hunts most other aquatic mobs, and can assist [players](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Player "Player")
 with aquatic combat and grant them [Regeneration](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Regeneration "Regeneration")
. They can also be carried around in a [bucket](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bucket "Bucket")
.

Trivia
------

[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl?action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: Trivia")

*   In the [release notes Minecraft Launcher](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Launcher_2.2.25xx "Launcher 2.2.25xx")
     there is an encrypted message T⍑ᒷ ᔑ ̇/𝙹ꖎ𝙹ℸ ̣ ꖎᓭ ᔑ∷ᒷ リ𝙹ℸ ̣ ∴⍑ᔑℸ ̣ ℸ ̣ ⍑ᒷ|| ᓭᒷᒷᒲ, which translates to "The Axolotls are not what they seem".
*   [Axolotls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axolotl "wikipedia:Axolotl")
     are small salamanders that are endemic to Mexico, which are critically endangered. Following [Mojang Studios](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mojang_Studios "Mojang Studios")
    ' trend of adding endangered species such as [polar bears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Polar_bear "Polar bear")
    , [giant pandas](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Panda "Panda")
     (no longer endangered as of 2022), [sea turtles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sea_turtle "Sea turtle")
    , endangered species of wild and native [bees](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bee "Bee")
    , and [fireflies](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Firefly_Bush "Firefly Bush")
    , axolotls were added to the game to raise awareness.[\[6\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl#cite_note-10)
    [\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl#cite_note-11)
    
*   The probability of a blue axolotl spawning is 1 in 1200 because that was the approximate number of axolotls left in the wild in real life at the time.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl#cite_note-C&C_Dev-12)
    
*   Axolotls can eat only [buckets of tropical fish](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Buckets_of_tropical_fish "Buckets of tropical fish")
     and not [tropical fish items](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tropical_fish_item "Tropical fish item")
     because axolotls in real life eat only living fish.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl#cite_note-C&C_Dev-12)
    
*   Even though they are not in the game, the green variant appears in the [Caves & Cliffs Update: Part I – Official Trailer](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Caves_%26_Cliffs_Update:_Part_I_%E2%80%93_Official_Trailer "Caves & Cliffs Update: Part I – Official Trailer")
    , as well as a recap.[\[9\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl#cite_note-13)
     When asked on a livestream, [Agnes Larsson](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Agnes_Larsson "Agnes Larsson")
     said she cannot say why the green variant was cut from the game.[\[10\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl#cite_note-14)
    
*   Both the "play dead" behavior and the regeneration ability of axolotls are based on the abilities of real life axolotls.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl#cite_note-C&C_Dev-12)
    
*   Because real axolotls don't actually vocalize, the sounds of axolotls were recorded from the incidental "hiccups" and "burps" real axolotls occasionally make when surfacing, as well as heavily edited dog sounds.[\[11\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl#cite_note-15)
    
*   During [Minecraft Live 2021](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Live_2021 "Minecraft Live 2021")
    , [Lydia Winters](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lydia_Winters "Lydia Winters")
     created an “[aloto](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Aloto "Aloto")
    ,” a hybrid of an axolotl and alligator.[\[12\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl#cite_note-16)

# End City

Tall generated structures found in the End's outer islands. Inhabited by shulkers. Contain valuable
loot including enchanted diamond/iron gear and horse armor. End ships (spawning off some towers) are
the only source of elytra and dragon heads.

## Overview

- Location: End outer islands (beyond the main island / End gateway)
- Biomes: "midlands" and "highlands" End biomes
- Inhabitants: Shulkers (several per city)
- Materials: Purpur blocks, purpur pillars, purpur stairs/slabs, end stone bricks, end rods (light)
- Height: Can exceed 100 blocks tall

## Generation

End cities generate across the End's outer islands on large flat areas. Reaching them requires
crossing the void gap from the main island, either via End gateway (throw ender pearl to activate),
elytra, or building. Cities cluster in some areas and can be thousands of blocks apart in others.
Unlike other structures they do not require specific seeds to locate — they are common across outer
islands.

Each tower of an End city has a 50% chance per direction of generating a bridge. Each bridge has a
12.5% chance of generating an End ship at its end.

## Structure

### Room Types

- **Base floor**: Small entrance room, 2 shulkers guard entrance
- **Banner rooms**: Banners hang on exterior; shulker inside
- **Large rooms**: Parkour-like staircases connecting towers
- **Loot rooms**: 2 chests at end of parkour area; some have a normal chest + ender chest
- **Fat tower (large tower)**: 4 shulkers always spawn in center section; loot room at top with
  2 chests in corners
- **Small tower**: Spiral purpur slab staircase; 1 shulker at top with banners

### Lighting

End rods provide the only light source inside towers.

## End Ship

A floating boat-shaped structure that generates off a bridge at 12.5% chance per bridge. One End
ship per city maximum.

- Always has stern facing toward the city, bow facing away
- 3 shulkers spawn: one on main deck, one at stern, one in treasure room
- Dragon head at the bow (only obtainable in Survival this way)
- Brewing stand on aftercastle with 2 splash potions of Instant Healing
- Treasure room below deck: floor lined with obsidian, 2 loot chests + item frame with elytra
  (elytra is the only way to obtain elytra in Survival mode)
- Crow's nest has a hole above magenta stained glass on deck (legacy beacon beam relic)

## Loot (City Chests)

Chests draw from 2 pools. Pool 1 gives 2-6 items; Pool 2 gives 1 item.

**Guaranteed/high-value loot includes:**
- Gold Ingot (2-7 stack)
- Iron Ingot (4-8 stack)
- Beetroot Seeds (1-10 stack)
- Diamond (2-7 stack)
- Emerald (2-6 stack)
- Saddle
- Copper / Iron / Golden / Diamond Horse Armor
- Spire Armor Trim Smithing Template
- Enchanted Iron tools and armor (pickaxe, shovel, sword, helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots)
- Enchanted Diamond tools and armor (pickaxe, shovel, sword, helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots)
- Enchanted Diamond Spear (BE only)

All gear items are enchanted with random enchantments at a random level.

## Trivia

- Some rooms resemble traditional Asian pagodas. Jeb visited the Summer Palace before the 15w31a
  snapshot, which inspired the design.
- The tower banners have a magenta/black pattern resembling the missing texture.
- End cities can exceed 100 blocks tall.
- The crow's nest hole above magenta stained glass on End ships is a legacy relic: originally the
  treasure was a beacon that shot a beam through the glass and the hole. The beacon was replaced with
  elytra but the glass and hole remain.
- Beetroot seeds are in End city chests because Legacy Console Edition worlds had size limits and
  needed a way to obtain beetroot in Survival. They were later added to Java Edition for parity.

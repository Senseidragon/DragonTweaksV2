Trivia
------

\[[edit](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay?section=19&veaction=edit "Edit section: Trivia")\
 | [edit source](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay?action=edit&section=19 "Edit section's source code: Trivia")\
\]

*   The allay was originally planned for the [Nether Update](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Update "Nether Update")
    .[\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#cite_note-TSM_Allay-6)
    
*   Some previous names considered for the allay were the wisp and the pixie.[\[4\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#cite_note-TSM_Allay-6)
    
*   The allays were created to solve the problem of how players would build structures and collect resources in _[Minecraft Legends](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Legends "Minecraft Legends")
    _ while focusing on defending the Overworld.[\[5\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#cite_note-7)
    
*   The reason why allay duplication uses amethyst shards is because the item is renewable, has a musical sound that fits with the allay, and it adds more value to [amethyst geodes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Amethyst_geode "Amethyst geode")
    .[\[6\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#cite_note-8)
    [\[7\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#cite_note-9)
    
*   According to the Meet the Allay article on [Minecraft.net](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft.net "Minecraft.net")
    , the reason why allays can be tamed by players is because they form a "magical connection" when that player gives it an item. This bond is what prevents players from accidentally harming their allay.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#cite_note-meet-allay-10)
    
*   Originally, Mojang considered having allays naturally spawn within [forest](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Forest "Forest")
     biomes. However, the reason they went with [pillager outposts](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pillager_outpost "Pillager outpost")
     and [woodland mansions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Woodland_mansion "Woodland mansion")
    , was because they wanted to highlight the companionship aspect.[\[8\]](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Allay#cite_note-meet-allay-10)
    *   This wound up being referenced in [Minecraft Mash-Up](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Minecraft_Mash-Up "Minecraft Mash-Up")
        , where [an allay](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Beautiful_blue_allay "Beautiful blue allay")
         is shown living in a birch forest.

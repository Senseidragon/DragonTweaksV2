# Piglin

A neutral mob found in the Nether. Hostile to players unless wearing at least one piece of
golden armor. Wields a golden sword (45%), crossbow (50%), or golden spear (5%). Trades
random items in exchange for gold ingots via bartering.

Baby piglins do not grow up, are passive, and take gold without giving anything in return.

## Stats

- Health: 16 HP (8 hearts)
- Armor: 0
- Behavior: Neutral (adult) / Passive (baby)
- Mob type: Monster
- Speed: 0.35
- Hitbox (JE adult): 1.95 blocks tall, 0.6 blocks wide
- Hitbox (JE baby): 0.99 blocks tall, 0.49 blocks wide
- Hitbox (BE adult): 1.9 blocks tall, 0.6 blocks wide

## Spawning

- Nether Wastes: groups of 4, light level 11 (JE) / 7 (BE) or below
- Crimson Forest: groups of 3-4
- Also spawn in Bastion Remnants during world generation
- Baby chance: 20% (JE) / 5% (BE)
- Each adult spawns with a weapon and has a 10% chance per armor slot to wear golden armor
  (34.39% chance of at least 1 piece; 0.01% chance of full set)
- Baby piglins never spawn with weapons or armor
- Piglins that spawned in a bastion or picked up items do not despawn naturally
- Do not naturally spawn in Peaceful difficulty (JE), except in bastions during world generation

## Drops

### On death (adults)

- 5 XP (not affected by armor) if killed by a player or tamed wolf
- Baby piglins drop 5 XP (JE) / 1 XP (BE)
- Natural equipment drops at 8.5% chance per piece, +1% per Looting level
- Picked-up equipment always drops on death
- Gold ingots, raw porkchops, and cooked porkchops that were picked up are NOT dropped
- If killed in one hit while admiring a gold ingot, the ingot drops

### Weapons (natural spawn)

- Golden Sword: 45%
- Crossbow: 50%
- Golden Spear: 5%

### Armor (natural spawn)

- Each slot independently: 10% chance of Golden Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, or Boots

## Behavior

- Can open and close wooden and copper doors (like villagers); cannot open fence gates,
  trapdoors, or iron doors
- Cannot climb ladders
- Cannot swim; can drown if submerged
- Not immune to fire or lava (unlike zombified piglins)
- Retreat from: zombified piglins (except babies), zoglins, soul fire items (soul torch,
  soul lantern, lit soul campfire)
- Baby piglins also retreat from wither skeletons and withers
- Baby piglins play with baby hoglins, riding them (up to 3 stacked)
- Crossbow piglins do not reload until provoked
- Iron golems attack piglins; piglins do not retaliate without provocation
- Detection range halved when player wears a piglin head

## Aggravation

Adult piglins are neutral if the player wears at least one piece of golden armor.

Piglins are provoked when a player:
- Is not wearing any gold armor (short range, light aggravation)
- Attacks a piglin or piglin brute (long range, heavy aggravation to target; medium to others)
- Opens/breaks a chest, trapped chest, copper chest, ender chest, shulker box, barrel,
  minecart with chest, or boat with chest (short range for open, mid-range for break; medium)
- Breaks a block of gold, gold ore, gilded blackstone, nether gold ore, block of raw gold,
  or deepslate gold ore (mid-range, medium aggravation)

Note: opening/breaking a hopper does NOT provoke piglins.

### Aggravation levels

- Light: triggered by player not wearing gold. Can de-aggravate if player equips gold, distracts
  with gold, or breaks line of sight.
- Medium: triggered by container interaction or witnessing another piglin/brute get hit.
  Can be distracted with gold. Light aggravation does not upgrade to medium.
- Heavy: triggered by taking damage. Cannot be distracted by gold; cannot barter.

Piglins remain hostile for 30 seconds when provoked. Invisibility effect allows container
interactions and even attacks (JE only) without provoking piglins.

## Attacking

- Sword piglins: rush the player with melee
- Crossbow piglins: shoot arrows every 2 seconds, walk backward when player is close (JE);
  crossbows can break from durability in JE (piglins then use fists), never break in BE
- Spear piglins: use charge attack while running toward target; walk away to reset charge
- Attack in hordes (congregate within 16 blocks); emit celebration sounds after defeating target
- Hostile to wither skeletons and withers
- Retaliate if rammed by a goat (JE only)

### Hunting hoglins

- Piglins randomly decide to hunt hoglins; other piglins join the hunt
- If outnumbered by hoglins, piglins flee and make "retreat" sounds
- 10% chance of "victory dance" (T-pose bob) after defeating any entity
- After a hunt, nearby piglins pick up porkchops (not leather) dropped by the hoglin
- Piglins that spawned as part of a bastion do not hunt

## Bartering

Adult piglins take gold ingots (dropped nearby or used by player while looking at them):
- Piglin "examines" ingot for 6 seconds (JE) / 8 seconds (BE)
- Drops a random item from the bartering loot table
- Baby piglins examine the ingot then run away without giving anything
- Piglins can barter while riding another mob
- Heavily aggravated piglins cannot barter

## Picking Up Items

- Piglins have 8 hidden inventory slots
- Prefer and actively seek gold-related items (piglin_loved tag)
- Full inventory: will still pick up gold ingots but not other gold items
- Automatically equip better armor/weapons; prefer gold armor over others
- Gold equipment requires 6 seconds inspection before equipping; non-gold equips instantly
- Do not pick up leather
- Pick up raw/cooked porkchops from within 1 block (but don't seek them out)
- Dropped gold ingots, raw porkchops, and cooked porkchops are NOT re-dropped on death

### Piglin-loved items (piglin_loved tag)

Bell, Block of Gold, Block of Raw Gold, Clock, Deepslate Gold Ore, Enchanted Golden Apple,
Gilded Blackstone, Glistering Melon Slice, Gold Ingot (triggers bartering), Gold Nugget,
Gold Ore, Golden Apple, Golden Axe, Golden Boots, Golden Carrot, Golden Chestplate,
Golden Dandelion, Golden Helmet, Golden Hoe, Golden Horse Armor, Golden Leggings,
Golden Nautilus Armor, Golden Pickaxe, Golden Shovel, Golden Spear, Golden Sword,
Light Weighted Pressure Plate, Nether Gold Ore, Raw Gold

## Zombification

When a piglin enters the Overworld or the End, it turns into a zombified piglin after 15 seconds.
The transformation preserves any held or worn items.

## Trivia

- In Bedrock Edition, baby piglins make angry noises when any piglin gets angry.
- Piglin sounds were made by blending sounds of a Swedish domestic pig known as a "Linderödssvin".
- The Nether Update page mentions piglins mined all the netherite out, leaving only ancient debris.
- During development, Henrik Kniberg experimented with ear animations to convey emotion (idle:
  slow ear sway, excited: fast flop) but scrapped it as not worth the effort.

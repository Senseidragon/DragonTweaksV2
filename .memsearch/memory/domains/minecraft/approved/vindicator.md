# Vindicator

A hostile illager that wields an iron axe. Found in woodland mansions and raids. Has a high melee
attack. Features the "Johnny" Easter egg (attacks ALL mobs when named "Johnny").

## Stats

- Health: 24 HP (12 hearts)
- Behavior: Hostile
- Mob type: Illager (Monster)
- Speed: 5.612 blocks/sec (~1.4x faster than walking)
- Hitbox (JE): 1.95 blocks tall, 0.6 blocks wide

## Attack Strength

Armed with iron axe:
- Easy: 7.5 HP
- Normal: 13 HP
- Hard: 19.5 HP

## Spawning

### Woodland Mansions

Spawn in groups of 1-3 in woodland mansions. Non-respawning after initial generation.
Some vindicators in mansions start neutral until the player gets too close or attacks.

### Patrols (JE Only)

Vindicators occasionally appear in patrol groups in the overworld.

### Raids

Appear from wave 1 onward (exact wave depends on difficulty). Raiders wielding axes have
Sharpness I/II enchantment -- Sharpness II on wave 6+.

In JE, vindicators can ride ravagers (appear as ravager jockeys in later waves). In BE, they
do not ride ravagers.

JE: A spawn egg can produce a raid captain vindicator with an ominous banner.

## Drops

- Emeralds: 0-1 (JE; requires kill by player or tamed wolf; scales with Looting)
- BE: more extensive loot table including emeralds, enchanted books, iron tools/armor
- Iron axe: 8.5% base chance to drop the equipped axe (scales with Looting)
- 5 XP base + 1-3 XP per non-dropped equipment piece

## Behavior

### Targeting

Attacks:
- Players (always)
- Villagers
- Iron golems
- Snow golems
- Adult turtles? (JE)
- Wandering traders

Does NOT attack other illagers or pillagers unless provoked.

### "Johnny" Easter Egg

If a vindicator is named "Johnny" (via name tag), it attacks every mob it encounters
regardless of type, including other illagers, piglins, the wither, and the ender dragon.
This is a reference to The Shining.

### Raid Behavior

During a raid, vindicators count toward the raid bossbar. When a vindicator is the last
remaining raider, it does not flee.

## Trivia

- The "Johnny" behavior is a reference to Jack Nicholson's character in The Shining (1980).
- Vindicators have among the highest melee attack in the game for a non-boss mob.
